## Workflow 3.1.3: Unserializable `STREAM_SERVER_TRUSTED` Symbol Boundary Protection

### Step-by-Step Execution
1. **[Chrome DevTools]** From an authenticated non-member consultee session, attempt to invoke any `"use server"` chat channel action (`syncWebinarChannel` / `addUsersToChannel`) for a foreign webinar ID.

### Expected Invariants & Assertions
- Because `Symbol("STREAM_SERVER_TRUSTED")` cannot be serialized over HTTP/Flight from the client, `trusted !== STREAM_SERVER_TRUSTED` evaluates true and `verifyChannelAccess` rejects the unauthorized caller (`403` / unauthorized error).

## Workflow 3.1.4: Post-Completion Retention Freeze (`chatFrozenAt`)

### Step-by-Step Execution
1. **[Supabase MCP]** Verify a completed Webinar (`completedAt < NOW() - INTERVAL '31 days'`) or inspect `chatFrozenAt` after `expireEventChannels` runs.
2. **[Stream IO MCP]** Inspect `channel.frozen` on `team:webinar-<id>`.

### Expected Invariants & Assertions
- Stream channel has `frozen === true`, `Webinar.chatFrozenAt IS NOT NULL`, and subsequent calls to `syncEventChannelCore` short-circuit cleanly without unfreezing the channel.

---

## Workflow 3.1.5: Cancelled Event 48-Hour Wind-Down Freeze

### Step-by-Step Execution
1. **[Supabase MCP]** Verify that a `CANCELLED` webinar/class keeps chat writable during the 48-hour refund/coordination wind-down window (`CANCELLED_WIND_DOWN_HOURS = 48`) and freezes (`chatFrozenAt IS NOT NULL`) once `cancelledAt + 48h` elapses.

### Expected Invariants & Assertions
- Matches `CANCELLED_WIND_DOWN_MS` contract in `lib/stream/channel-lifecycle.ts`.
