Support Tab — PM Review

🔴 Critical: Navigation & Naming Issues

1. Nav label "Support" routes to /feedback — URL and label mismatch
   The top navigation shows the tab as "Support" but the URL is /feedback
   and the page heading reads "Feedback & Support". This three-way naming
   inconsistency (nav label / URL path / page heading) creates confusion:
   - If a user bookmarks /feedback, the bookmark says "feedback" but the nav
     says "Support"
   - If support links to /feedback in an email ("visit your Support page"),
     users have to visually reconcile the URL with the nav label
   The URL, nav label, and heading should all use the same term. Given the
   tab contains both a feedback form and a support ticket form, "Support" or
   "Help" as the single label is cleaner than "Feedback & Support."

---
🟠 High: UX Issues

2. No response time / SLA disclosure on the Support ticket form
   Users submitting a support ticket have no idea when to expect a response.
   There is no "We typically respond within 24 hours" or SLA label anywhere
   on the form or in the empty state. Without this, users will submit tickets
   and then immediately open another channel (email, WhatsApp) because they
   don't know if anyone is watching.

3. Issue type dropdown options not visible without interaction
   The "What's this about?" dropdown defaults to "Select issue type" but the
   available options are hidden until the user clicks. The a11y tree confirms
   this is a combobox with options but they cannot be verified without
   opening it. If the issue type list is narrow (e.g., only 3-4 options),
   they could be surfaced as radio buttons or chips for faster selection
   and better discoverability.

4. Priority defaults to "Medium" (shown in orange) — alarming for no reason
   The Priority field defaults to "Medium" styled in orange text. Orange
   typically signals urgency or warning. A user who hasn't touched the field
   will submit with Medium priority without meaning to, and the orange colour
   may make them feel they are submitting something urgent when they aren't.
   The default should be "Low" or "Normal" with neutral styling.

5. No live chat option
   For a platform where consultees may have active booking problems (can't
   join a session, payment failed, access denied), a ticket form with an
   unknown response time is insufficient. The page offers no live chat,
   no WhatsApp support number, no emergency contact for day-of session issues.
   At minimum, there should be a note: "For urgent issues affecting a live
   session, contact us at [emergency channel]."

---
🟡 Medium: Feedback Form Issues

6. Feedback and Support are two different workflows — consider separating
   "Feedback" (rate your experience, share thoughts) and "Support" (report
   a problem, get help) are conceptually distinct. Mixing them under one
   "Feedback & Support" page with two tabs means users who come here with a
   problem have to choose between "Feedback" and "Support" — a cognitive
   step that adds friction. Consider a single page with a clear intent
   selector at the top: "I want to share feedback" / "I need help with
   something."

7. Feedback form has no appointment/session context field
   The Feedback form (Title + Description + Rating) is generic. There is no
   way to tie feedback to a specific session, consultant, or booking. If
   William wants to leave feedback about a specific class, he must manually
   describe it in the description field. Contextual feedback (linked to an
   appointment ID) would be far more actionable for the team.

8. Rating stars are accessible but unlabelled
   The five star-rating buttons are present in the a11y tree as unnamed
   buttons. Screen readers would announce them as "button, button, button,
   button, button" with no indication of value. They should have ARIA labels
   ("Rate 1 star", "Rate 2 stars", etc.) or use a labelled radio group.

9. No confirmation or expected response time after ticket submission
   After submitting feedback or a support ticket, there is no visible
   confirmation toast or next-steps message in the UI to tell the user what
   happens next. The history panel on the right handles this partially
   ("Your Feedback History" / "Your Support Tickets"), but a submission
   confirmation toast should also appear.
