ISSUES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

[1] 🔴 P0 — Settings, Help, and Analytics have no sidebar nav link
Three of the 14 consultant pages (Settings at .../settings, Help at .../help, Analytics at .../analytics) are completely absent from the sidebar navigation. Help is partially accessible via a small unlabelled icon in the top right, but there is no text label and no aria-label on that link (uid=5_32 has no accessible name). Settings and Analytics appear completely undiscoverable unless the user guesses the URL.

> **Proposed fix:** Add Settings, Help, and Analytics to the sidebar. A natural grouping would be a fourth section (ACCOUNT) with Settings and Help as sticky bottom items, and Analytics under a new INSIGHTS section or adjacent to Earnings.

[2] 🟠 P1 — All 4 stat cards show 0 despite 451 historical appointments
Today's Sessions (0), Upcoming (0), Pending Requests (0), This Week (0) are all 0. This is technically accurate (all seed appointments are past-dated) but creates a misleading "empty account" impression for any consultant whose upcoming pipeline is temporarily quiet. There is no historical context: no "You completed X sessions last month" or "X total lifetime sessions." A new consultant can't distinguish "my dashboard works but I have nothing upcoming" from "my data isn't loading."

> **Proposed fix:** Add a "Lifetime" or "Last 30 Days" stat row below the 4 forward-looking cards. Example: "Total Completed: 451" and "Joined: [date]" give consultants confidence their data is intact even when upcoming slots are empty.

[3] 🟠 P1 — "Upcoming Sessions" and "Today's Appointments" both empty with no CTA to create availability
Both panels show empty states ("No appointments today", "No upcoming appointments") with only a "View all" link. There is no prompt to set up availability, create a plan, or invite a client — the most common next action for a consultant with an empty pipeline. The empty state copy ("Enjoy your free time or check upcoming sessions" / "Your schedule is clear for now") is passive and misses a conversion opportunity.

> **Proposed fix:** Add a secondary CTA button in the empty state, e.g. "Set your availability" → links to Settings → Availability, or "Create a plan" → links to Event Planner.

[4] 🟠 P1 — "Total Revenue" (₹4.3K) in Business Overview only reflects PAID earnings
The ₹4.3K figure matches the consultant's share of the single PAID earnings row (₹5,396 gross × ~80% = ~₹4,317). The REFUNDED row (₹65,544 gross) is correctly excluded, but the label "Total Revenue" implies all-time gross without clarifying it means "net paid out" or "cleared earnings." A consultant who just had a large refund might be confused why their revenue doesn't reflect their subscription sales.

> **Proposed fix:** Rename to "Net Earnings (Paid)" or add a tooltip explaining "Cleared earnings after platform fee and refunds." Link to Earnings page for breakdown.

[5] 🟡 P2 — "Next Payout: ₹0 / Min ₹50.0K" is cryptic without tooltip
The "Min ₹50.0K" label appears directly beneath the ₹0 payout amount. There is no tooltip, popover, or explanation of what "Min" means. New consultants will not know this is the minimum payout threshold (₹50,000 floor before a payout is triggered), and will not understand why their ₹4.3K earned hasn't been paid out.

> **Proposed fix:** Add a tooltip on the "Min ₹50.0K" badge: "Payouts are initiated once your available balance reaches ₹50,000." Also consider showing progress toward threshold (e.g. a small progress bar: ₹4.3K / ₹50K = 8.6%).

[6] 🟡 P2 — "Active Clients: 0" with no definition shown
Active Clients shows 0. With 451 appointments, a consultant might expect a non-zero number. No definition of "active" is shown (active subscription? sessions in last 30 days? non-cancelled?). The label alone is ambiguous.

> **Proposed fix:** Add tooltip: "Consultees with an active (non-cancelled) subscription or a session in the last 30 days." Or rename to "Clients (Active Subscriptions)" to be self-explanatory.

[7] 🟡 P2 — "Welcome back" greeting lacks personalization
The hero heading reads "Welcome back" with no name. The consultee dashboard's navbar shows "Good [time], [Name]." The consultant home doesn't display the consultant's name anywhere in the main content area (name appears only in the left sidebar). This feels impersonal compared to the consultee side.

> **Proposed fix:** Change to "Welcome back, Andrew!" (using the authenticated user's first name).

[8] 🟡 P2 — Pending Requests mini-panel uses table layout even for empty state
The Pending Requests panel renders column headers (Type, Title, Requested By, Requested At) even when there are no rows. The empty message "No pending requests" appears as a table body row. This is visually awkward — the user sees a table structure with headers and then a single center-aligned message inside it.

> **Proposed fix:** Hide the table headers when no rows exist; show only the empty state message + "View all requests →" link.

[9] 🟡 P2 — No subscription/class progress tracking on Home
The plan's protocol called for checking "Upcoming Sessions progress bars for subscriptions/classes." No such component exists. Subscriptions (184 in DB) and Classes (109 in DB) typically have multi-session commitments, and a consultant managing 20+ active subscriptions would benefit from seeing how many sessions remain per subscriber. This info is absent from the home dashboard.

> **Proposed fix:** Add a "Subscription Progress" panel or incorporate session-remaining indicators into the Upcoming Sessions cards when appointment type is SUBSCRIPTION or CLASS.

[10] 🟡 P2 — No "Join" button visible (correct, but no explanation)
No Join button is shown since no sessions are imminent. This is correct behavior (Join should only appear within a 5-min pre-session window). However, first-time consultants have no way to know that the Join button will appear automatically — there is no tooltip, help text, or documentation pointing to this. They might wonder "how do I join sessions?"

> **Proposed fix:** Add a small info tooltip or "?" icon near Today's Appointments: "Join buttons appear 5 minutes before a session starts."
