ISSUES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

[1] 🔴 P0 — Analytics is not in the sidebar nav — page is hidden (cross-ref: 1-home.txt Issue [1])
Like Settings and Help, Analytics has no sidebar nav link. A consultant who wants to see their performance data would have no visual path to this page. The absence of 3 out of 14 pages from the sidebar nav is a systemic navigation architecture issue: Settings, Help, and Analytics are completely unreachable without knowing the direct URL.

> **Proposed fix:** Add "Analytics" to the sidebar nav — logically below Referrals in the Finance group, or as a standalone section below Finance. Use a chart/bar-chart icon consistent with the Analytics concept.

[2] 🟡 P2 — "Coming Soon" has no timeline or roadmap hint
The placeholder gives no indication of when Analytics will be available. "Coming Soon" with no context is a dead end — a consultant who navigates here repeatedly hoping to find data will have no signal that the feature is progressing.

> **Proposed fix:** Add a sentence with a rough timeline: "Expected in Q2 2026." Or add a "We're building this — let us know what metrics matter most to you" prompt to collect feature requests.

[3] 🟡 P2 — No "Notify me when available" CTA
The Coming Soon screen has no mechanism for a consultant to sign up to be notified when Analytics launches. This is a missed retention and engagement signal — consultants who care about performance data are likely to be power users who would benefit most from being re-engaged at launch.

> **Proposed fix:** Add an email/notification opt-in: "Get notified when Analytics is ready" → click → Novu/email capture. Store interest in a `featureWaitlist` table.

[4] 🟡 P2 — Coming Soon card is visually indistinguishable from an empty-state error
The card style (centered icon + heading + text + white card on grey background) is identical to the empty state shown on other pages (e.g. Recordings "No recordings yet" card). A first-time visitor cannot tell if Analytics failed to load or is simply not yet built. A "Coming Soon" state warrants differentiated visual treatment.

> **Proposed fix:** Use a different visual treatment: add a "🚧 In Development" badge on the card, a different background color (e.g. a subtle gradient), or a progress/roadmap illustration. Avoid the same card pattern used for empty data states.
