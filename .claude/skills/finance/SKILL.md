## Non-negotiables

Money truth is written in exactly one Serializable transaction per event, and every status transition moves through a CAS-in-WHERE guard, never a read-then-write. `Payment.paymentStatus` is written by the confirmation pipeline (`handlePaymentSuccess` and its siblings, routed through `routeCapturedPayment`) and by nothing else — see `references/doctrine.md` for the outage this rule prevents. The funding-leg sum identity excludes `REFERRAL_CREDIT` legs, because `Payment.amount` has always meant the post-credit gateway charge; treating that as a defect once broke every credit-funded checkout in production (#1347). The ledger is a double-entry journal; `walletBalance` is a reconciled cache, never a second source of truth, and account balances are aggregated from the journal rather than stored. Every refund goes through one of the two front doors and reserves its amount before any gateway call. GST is billed as principal supplier (ADR 26): 18% on the full price, the platform's own tax invoice and credit note, and no Section 52 TCS. The database pool is capped at one connection per function instance, so nothing inside a `$transaction` may read through the global Prisma client. Full detail and sources for every one of these lives in `references/doctrine.md`.
