## Non-negotiables

- Write money truth in one Serializable transaction per event; guard every status transition with CAS-in-WHERE.
- Confirm gateway captures through the single confirmation pipeline, routed by `routeCapturedPayment`.
- Let the deferred payment-leg trigger enforce the funding sum; exclude `REFERRAL_CREDIT`, which is already netted out of `Payment.amount`.
- Show the payer's rail from the funding legs, never from `Payment.paymentMethod`, which is a fallback for a row with no legs only. Use `paymentRailLabel` and `paymentFundingBreakdown` in `lib/appointments/payment-display.ts`; do not add a second leg-wording map.
- Treat the immutable double-entry journal as authoritative. Wallet balances are reconciled caches; account balances are journal aggregates.
- Advance both scheduled and authenticated manual ledger audits through the same bounded `runReconcileLedgers` core and fail-closed lease. Continue idle checkpoints; authenticated manual calls can refresh a completed verdict on the same day. Never re-freeze historical findings on resume or label an incomplete report as drift. Evaluate exception expiry at save time, not the audit start. Each comparison uses one read-only Repeatable Read snapshot. Partial reports remain RUNNING; missing BOOKING findings and baselines pin paymentId. Freeze each wallet page immediately, release a frozen wallet the page finds back in sync, and deduplicate the fatal page by billing account. See `docs/finance/20-ledger/06-integrity-and-reconciliation.md` for operation and resumption.
- Reserve refunds before any gateway call. Booking refunds use `refundBookingPayment`, `refundWholeEventPayments` or `refundRemovedAttendeeSeat`; wallet top-up refunds use `refundRemainingWalletBalance` or `bookTopUpRefund`. Support and incident paths enter through the booking refund door.
- An operator refund passes `settlesOwedByHand`. In the refund's own transaction it clears the payment's owed-refund marker when it covers the owed amount, and otherwise lowers `refundOwedPaise` by its amount and leaves the marker retrying. Every marker drive passes `owedMarker` (key and amount). First writer wins: a cancel door (the cancel route, trial cancel, seat leave, the whole-event cancel) whose stamp loses refuses with nothing committed (`heldMarkerRefusal`, 409 `REFUND_IN_PROGRESS` / `REFUND_NEEDS_SUPPORT`). A door that proceeds anyway (rejection, moderation of a 1:1 or a removed attendee, a no-show, the freeze, an overage credit-back) adds nothing; unless a full-balance marker already covers it, it records an `owed-already-held:` WARN event with its top-up amount and places an `OPERATOR` `EarningsHold` keyed on it in the same transaction (`stampOwedOrHeld` / `heldInsteadOf`; a hold race rolls the door back). The Refunds tab lists it while the top-up is unpaid, read from the payment's refunds and balance, then until the hold is released. A failed drive backs off 1 h, then 4 h (`refundOwedNextAt`) before the third failure pages. See `references/doctrine.md` and ADR 37.
- Never book a payment through the admin recover door unless `unreturnedCaptureWhere` (`lib/payments/webhooks/auto-refund-marker.ts`) matches it: no `PENDING` or `SUCCEEDED` refund, no owed-refund marker, no `Auto-refund` description and no open or lost dispute. The route refuses with `PAYMENT_RETURNED` and `handlePaymentSuccess` re-reads the predicate in its Serializable transaction (`RecoveryRefusedError`). There is no `REFUNDED` payment status, so `SUCCEEDED` with no booking is not proof the money is still held.
- Every exit of a checkout from `PENDING` that is not a payment returns what checkout consumed, in the same transaction as its CAS: `reverseCreditsForPayment`, `returnDiscountUse` and `reverseBookingUtilization`. Route a new exit through `expirePendingPaymentsInTx` (`lib/booking/pending-payment-expiry.ts`) rather than writing `EXPIRED` yourself.
- Price a reload or second tab with the buyer's own open order for the same item counted as released (`ownOpenOrderHolds`, `lib/payments/checkout/order-reuse.ts`; `releasableHeldCreditsPaise` in `lib/referrals/service.ts`); never match an open order of another time (two non-renewal "start now" subscription checkouts of one plan are one item while the hold is live), reuse one only at the same amount and the same discount code, and keep the commit-time credit CAS as the final decision. Match items only through `classifyOpenOrders` and `openOrderTargetOf`.
- A wallet top-up marked `FAILED` is only "abandoned" and is never deleted. `confirmTopUp` claims `PENDING` then `FAILED` under one journal key; a late capture on a frozen wallet or closed organisation goes back to the card through `refundTopUpCapture`; the row's note says the refund is owed until the call succeeds, and it is not retried automatically. The refund-remaining door returns top-up money only (`grantedWalletCreditPaise` stays, re-read with the balance in one snapshot before each refund).
- Count a credit restoration at its credit value in the staff refund limits and the second-approver price (`remedyValuePaise`, `lib/support/case-actions.ts`).
- Never release an earning while its payment has a `PENDING` refund or an owed-refund marker (`RELEASABLE_PAYMENT`); the marker is a hold that lifts only when a drive or a hand refund clears it.
- Once GST-registered, bill GST as principal supplier: 18% on the full price, platform invoices and credit notes, no Section 52 TCS (while unregistered, see the next rule). Book each supply's GST once; invoice journals cover only supplies without booking journals, and overages tax only their surcharge.
- Charge GST only in the registered regime. `currentGstRegime()` (`lib/payments/tax/gst-regime.ts`) derives it from `PLATFORM_GSTIN` alone and throws `GST_CONFIG_INVALID` on a set but unusable GSTIN; pass it to `deriveCheckoutAmount` (`gstRegime`) and `determineTax` (`gstRegistered`), and stamp `Payment.gstRegime` on every Payment that computes tax. Mint a tax invoice, or add a payment to an organisation invoice, only for a payment stamped `REGISTERED`: an `UNREGISTERED` or NULL stamp is a receipt only (`CONSUMER_INVOICEABLE_PAYMENT_WHERE`, `mintConsumerInvoice`, `rollupOrgInvoiceAccruals`, `settle-invoice-accruals`), and with no invoice there is no credit note; refuse organisation-funded rails and org invoices with `GST_REGISTRATION_REQUIRED` while unregistered. See `docs/finance/60-tax/05-gst-registration-and-tax-regime.md`.
- Hold every platform-funded lever to one floor. A platform code, the welcome discount and referral credits together never exceed `platformFundedCapPaise` (`lib/payments/pricing/platform-funded-cap.ts`): the platform fee on the list price less `GATEWAY_COST_BPS` (354: Razorpay's worst-case 3 % plus 18 % GST on it) on the largest amount the order can charge, using the order's `promoFeeBps` (0 on a fee-waiver or host-organisation order). Stack at most one pre-tax modifier (a code suppresses the welcome discount), tax after it, credits after tax; the programme caps remain extra limits. Any preview (the checkout math, the discount validate route) must apply the same cap. A code on a zero-cap order (fee waiver, host org) is refused with `DISCOUNT_NOT_APPLICABLE` before commit, and `consumeFeeWaiver` never spends a waiver on an order carrying any platform-funded promo, a discounting code included. Every Payment copy or tombstone carries `gstRegime`. The invariant suite is `__tests__/payments/pricing-levers-invariants.test.ts`.
- Pass the transaction client through every helper inside `$transaction`; global-client reads can deadlock at `PG_POOL_MAX=1`.
- Render every email before the money transaction opens and only stage it inside; a notice's render failure never rolls back a capture, refund, ledger entry, payout or dunning claim. Receipts, refund, dunning and payout notices leave a `review:render_failed` row per recipient instead of a Sentry event (notification ADR decision 15).

Detailed invariants and regime facts live in `references/doctrine.md`; verification procedures live in `references/verification.md`.

## Standalone sales (replays and digital products)

- Settle each rail only in its handler: `handleRecordingPurchaseSuccess` (`lib/payments/webhooks/recording-purchase.ts`) and `handleDigitalProductPurchaseSuccess` (`lib/payments/webhooks/digital-product-purchase.ts`), routed by `routeCapturedPayment` on the order notes' `type`. Stage a capture that fulfils nothing with `stageCaptureRefund` in `lib/payments/webhooks/standalone-sale-capture.ts`; never grant it.
- Revoke on full refund or lost chargeback through `revokeStandaloneSaleEntitlement` (`lib/payments/digital-product-entitlement.ts`), which looks the sale up by `paymentId`. Never infer what a payment bought from `appointmentId === null`.
- A free digital product claim is a `SUCCEEDED` purchase of 0 paise with no order, Payment, invoice or earnings.
- Resume a pending replay or product purchase only when its stored amount equals the price derived in the same request; otherwise retire it (`PENDING` to `FAILED`, count checked) and mint a new order. Insert a purchase only after a same-transaction conditional write on the recording or product proves it is still for sale, and for a product still at the quoted price (`NOT_ELIGIBLE` / `NOT_FOR_SALE` / `PRICE_CHANGED` otherwise).
- Never delete a purchase row that holds a gateway order: the reconcile sweep finds a lost capture only through that row. A product with any purchase row answers `409 HAS_PURCHASES` or `409 HAS_ORDERS` on DELETE and is archived instead.
- Digital product earnings use `DIGITAL_PRODUCT` (168 h hold); the invoice SAC comes from `DIGITAL_PRODUCT_SAC_CODES` in `lib/payments/payouts/constants.ts` (998431 or 998439, pending CA sign-off).
- Refund a digital product through the existing support and admin doors; the operator checks `firstDownloadedAt`, which no door enforces.
- Recover a lost capture with `reconcile-standalone-purchases` or the buyer's `purchases/[purchaseId]/verify` route, both through `recoverStandaloneOrder`. Both read `FAILED` purchases under 7 days old, since a declined attempt's order can still capture; neither ever reads `REFUNDED`.

## Referral credits

The referral programme design, launch scope and fraud doctrine live in the `growth` skill (`.claude/skills/growth/SKILL.md`); this section keeps only the money rules.

- Vest, void, hold and breakage live in `lib/referrals/vesting.ts`; every referral and credit move is a CAS on the prior status. A `PENDING` credit holds nothing in the ledger.
- Reverse a credit's unused balance only through `reverseCreditBalance` (`lib/referrals/credit-reversal.ts`, key `referral-reverse:<creditId>`); the operator route and the lost-chargeback settlement share it. `settleLostDispute` calls `voidVestedReferralRewards` on its own transaction; the spent part is absorbed.
- The shared-payer void compares `Payment.payerVpaHash` and `payerPhoneHash`, stamped once from the capture webhook by `recordPayerInstruments`; never store raw payer instruments and never call Razorpay from the vest job.
- Review-hold thresholds and the budget are `ReferralProgramConfig` fields; their values are the owner's and live in Notion, never in code, comments or docs. A released `HELD` referral carries `reviewReleasedAt` so the rules skip it.
- Referral request signals (`Referral.ipHash`) hash only the middleware-stamped IP (`stampedClientIp`), under a key derived from `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` with its own label (`lib/referrals/signal-hash.ts`).

## Settlement, wallet and operator surfaces

- **Settlement recon.** Razorpay card money sits in the `GATEWAY_CLEARING` ledger account until it settles to `CASH`. `reconcile-gateway-settlements` (`jobs/reconcile/reconcile-gateway-settlements.ts`) matches Razorpay's settlement report into `GatewaySettlementLine` rows and books `GATEWAY_SETTLEMENT` (`GATEWAY_CLEARING` to `CASH`); the per-capture fee is booked by `lib/payments/ledger/gateway-fee.ts`. `reconcile-ledgers` does not check the clearing residual; read that job for it.
- **Clawback aging.** The ledger audit applies voids to their specific originating debts and retains them after payout reversal. Failed collecting payouts invalidate their recoveries, not origin voids; a newer void must not hide an older unpaid debt.
- **Wallet corrections.** `adjustWallet` (`lib/api/organizations/wallet.ts`, kind `WALLET_ADJUSTMENT`) moves cache and journal by the same amount, so it cannot repair a cache-vs-journal drift. Repair drift with a balanced correcting `LedgerTransaction`, then unfreeze.
- **Wallet freeze.** `WALLET_BALANCE_DRIFT` freezes discretionary spend via `lib/payments/wallet-freeze.ts` (`isWalletFrozen`, `freezeDriftedWallets`), even when the finding is baselined. The next audit that finds the cache equal to the journal lifts a drift-tagged freeze (`context.source: "reconcile-drift"`) it saw in its snapshot as actor `SYSTEM`, never an ops freeze (`releaseReconciledWalletFreezes`, the same compare-and-set `unfreezeWalletSpend` as the admin door). A wallet debit also needs its `Dr WALLET` line (`assertWalletDebitJournaled`).
- **Support holds and remedies.** `EarningsHoldReason.SUPPORT_CASE` holds are placed by `lib/support/case-holds.ts`. A service complaint (`SupportCase`) refund is proposed as a `SupportFinancialRecommendation`, decided by a second operator, and only then executed through `refundBookingPayment` (`lib/support/case-actions.ts`); a recommendation moves no money. The staff caps in `lib/support/money-policy.ts` (`assertBoundedRefund`) are checked on that approve-and-execute path. A dispute is the gateway chargeback (`Dispute`), a different thing.
- **Metrics totals.** The revenue total and this month on Metrics (both trees, ADMIN and STAFF) sum the same `SUCCEEDED` payments in paise (`lib/backoffice/platform-totals.ts`); "this month" starts at 00:00 IST.
- **Ops proposals.** Back-office money doors above ₹25,000 answer `202` and wait for a second admin (`lib/backoffice/ops-proposals.ts`, `withOpsAction` in `lib/backoffice/ops-action-log.ts`).

## Invoice capture recovery

Provider holding reads reject nonpositive, unsafe or non-INR captured amounts;
invalid evidence must retain the order reference for recovery.

Lock a payable invoice without invalidating its current order. Booking-refund
credits and bank receipts retain the reference until gateway inspection; gateway
settlement clears it only when the captured payment has been accounted for.
The existing healer also reads PAID invoices with a retained reference, parks any
additional capture through canonical settlement and retires the exact inspected
pointer with its audit in one transaction. Live payments, read errors and
inconsistent paid-order evidence retain the pointer.

Checkout may replace a stale-amount unpaid order only after its bounded payment
read is empty. CAS the inspected order ID and recompute current debt inside the
attachment transaction; retain the replaced ID in the initiation audit. A provider
read is no cancellation: an old tab can still capture later with a lost webhook.
The healer does not consume audit history. Void/overage-credit clearers remain
separate coverage limits; do not claim discovery of every superseded order.

Use `applyOrgInvoiceCapture` with the transaction client for webhook, checkout
read-back and recovery. Keep receipt, journal, PAID audit and notification staging
atomic. An unreleased unapplied receipt stays in operator recovery; a later amount
match must not settle it again. Failed payment attempts retain retryable order
references. Keep provider waits inside the remaining admission budget and gateway
calls outside transactions. An SDK timeout does not cancel a provider effect.
Before dunning suspension, inspect the retained order and hold live, uncertain or
parked evidence, then recheck debt/order identity inside the claim transaction.
The suspension budget starts at stage entry, independently of earlier reminders.
Every dunning stage stages its bell and email inside the winning claim
transaction (`stageOverdueNotices`, dedupe key `orgInvoice:<id>:overdue:<stage>`);
the email renders before the claim. A roster or read failure rolls the claim back
and counts in `failed`; a render failure commits it with a `review:render_failed` row.
Read `docs/maintenance/12-worker-recovery.md` for cadence, rotation and limits.
