## Non-negotiables

Money truth is written in exactly one Serializable transaction per event, and every status transition moves through a CAS-in-WHERE guard, never a read-then-write. `Payment.paymentStatus` is written by the confirmation pipeline (`handlePaymentSuccess` and its siblings, routed through `routeCapturedPayment`) and by nothing else — see `references/doctrine.md` for the outage this rule prevents. The funding-leg sum identity excludes `REFERRAL_CREDIT` legs, because `Payment.amount` has always meant the post-credit gateway charge; treating that as a defect once broke every credit-funded checkout in production (#1347). The ledger is a double-entry journal; `walletBalance` is a reconciled cache, never a second source of truth, and account balances are aggregated from the journal rather than stored. Every booking refund goes through one of three front doors (`refundBookingPayment` in `lib/payments/operations/booking-refund.ts`, `refundWholeEventPayments` and `refundRemovedAttendeeSeat` in `lib/payments/operations/event-refunds.ts`) and reserves its amount before any gateway call; the cancel markers, the incident door (`refundIncidentSeat`) and the support door drive `refundBookingPayment`, and an organisation top-up refund goes through `refundRemainingWalletBalance` or `bookTopUpRefund` in `lib/payments/operations/wallet-refund.ts`. GST is billed as principal supplier (ADR 26): 18% on the full price, the platform's own tax invoice and credit note, and no Section 52 TCS. GST is booked once per supply: a booking credits it in `booking:<paymentId>`, so an org invoice journals `invoice-issued:<invoiceId>` only when it bills no booking (subscription and manual fees), and an overage taxes only its surcharge. The database pool is capped at one connection per function instance, so nothing inside a `$transaction` may read through the global Prisma client. Full detail and sources for every one of these lives in `references/doctrine.md`.

## Settlement, wallet and operator surfaces

- **Settlement recon.** Razorpay card money sits in the `GATEWAY_CLEARING` ledger account until it settles to `CASH`. `reconcile-gateway-settlements` (`jobs/reconcile/reconcile-gateway-settlements.ts`) matches Razorpay's settlement report into `GatewaySettlementLine` rows and books `GATEWAY_SETTLEMENT` (`GATEWAY_CLEARING` to `CASH`); the per-capture fee is booked by `lib/payments/ledger/gateway-fee.ts`. `reconcile-ledgers` does not check the clearing residual; read that job for it.
- **Wallet corrections.** `adjustWallet` (`lib/api/organizations/wallet.ts`, kind `WALLET_ADJUSTMENT`) moves cache and journal by the same amount, so it cannot repair a cache-vs-journal drift. Repair drift with a balanced correcting `LedgerTransaction`, then unfreeze.
- **Wallet freeze.** `WALLET_BALANCE_DRIFT` freezes discretionary spend via `lib/payments/wallet-freeze.ts` (`isWalletFrozen`, `freezeDriftedWallets`), even when the finding is baselined. A wallet debit also needs its `Dr WALLET` line (`assertWalletDebitJournaled`).
- **Support holds and remedies.** `EarningsHoldReason.SUPPORT_CASE` holds are placed by `lib/support/case-holds.ts`. A service complaint (`SupportCase`) refund is proposed as a `SupportFinancialRecommendation`, decided by a second operator, and only then executed through `refundBookingPayment` (`lib/support/case-actions.ts`); a recommendation moves no money. The staff caps in `lib/support/money-policy.ts` (`assertBoundedRefund`) are checked on that approve-and-execute path. A dispute is the gateway chargeback (`Dispute`), a different thing.
- **Ops proposals.** Back-office money doors above ₹25,000 answer `202` and wait for a second admin (`lib/backoffice/ops-proposals.ts`, `withOpsAction` in `lib/backoffice/ops-action-log.ts`).
