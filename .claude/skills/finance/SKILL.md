## Non-negotiables

- Write money truth in one Serializable transaction per event; guard every status transition with CAS-in-WHERE.
- Confirm gateway captures through the single confirmation pipeline, routed by `routeCapturedPayment`.
- Let the deferred payment-leg trigger enforce the funding sum; exclude `REFERRAL_CREDIT`, which is already netted out of `Payment.amount`.
- Treat the immutable double-entry journal as authoritative. Wallet balances are reconciled caches; account balances are journal aggregates.
- Advance both scheduled and authenticated manual ledger audits through the same bounded `runReconcileLedgers` core and fail-closed lease. Continue idle checkpoints; authenticated manual calls can refresh a completed verdict on the same day. Never re-freeze historical findings on resume or label an incomplete report as drift. Evaluate exception expiry at save time, not the audit start. Each comparison uses one read-only Repeatable Read snapshot. Partial reports remain RUNNING; missing BOOKING findings and baselines pin paymentId. Freeze each wallet page immediately, release a frozen wallet the page finds back in sync, and deduplicate the fatal page by billing account. See `docs/finance/20-ledger/06-integrity-and-reconciliation.md` for operation and resumption.
- Reserve refunds before any gateway call. Booking refunds use `refundBookingPayment`, `refundWholeEventPayments` or `refundRemovedAttendeeSeat`; wallet top-up refunds use `refundRemainingWalletBalance` or `bookTopUpRefund`. Support and incident paths enter through the booking refund door.
- An operator refund passes `settlesOwedByHand`. In the refund's own transaction it clears the payment's owed-refund marker when it covers the owed amount, and otherwise lowers `refundOwedPaise` by its amount and leaves the marker retrying. A drive of a marker read back from the row (`markerFromRow`) passes `owedMarker` (key and amount). A failed drive backs off 1 h, then 4 h (`refundOwedNextAt`) before the third failure pages. See `references/doctrine.md` and ADR 37.
- Bill GST as principal supplier: 18% on the full price, platform invoices and credit notes, no Section 52 TCS. Book each supply's GST once; invoice journals cover only supplies without booking journals, and overages tax only their surcharge.
- Pass the transaction client through every helper inside `$transaction`; global-client reads can deadlock at `PG_POOL_MAX=1`.

Detailed invariants and regime facts live in `references/doctrine.md`; verification procedures live in `references/verification.md`.

## Settlement, wallet and operator surfaces

- **Settlement recon.** Razorpay card money sits in the `GATEWAY_CLEARING` ledger account until it settles to `CASH`. `reconcile-gateway-settlements` (`jobs/reconcile/reconcile-gateway-settlements.ts`) matches Razorpay's settlement report into `GatewaySettlementLine` rows and books `GATEWAY_SETTLEMENT` (`GATEWAY_CLEARING` to `CASH`); the per-capture fee is booked by `lib/payments/ledger/gateway-fee.ts`. `reconcile-ledgers` does not check the clearing residual; read that job for it.
- **Clawback aging.** The ledger audit applies voids to their specific originating debts and retains them after payout reversal. Failed collecting payouts invalidate their recoveries, not origin voids; a newer void must not hide an older unpaid debt.
- **Wallet corrections.** `adjustWallet` (`lib/api/organizations/wallet.ts`, kind `WALLET_ADJUSTMENT`) moves cache and journal by the same amount, so it cannot repair a cache-vs-journal drift. Repair drift with a balanced correcting `LedgerTransaction`, then unfreeze.
- **Wallet freeze.** `WALLET_BALANCE_DRIFT` freezes discretionary spend via `lib/payments/wallet-freeze.ts` (`isWalletFrozen`, `freezeDriftedWallets`), even when the finding is baselined. The next audit that finds the cache equal to the journal lifts a drift-tagged freeze (`context.source: "reconcile-drift"`) it saw in its snapshot as actor `SYSTEM`, never an ops freeze (`releaseReconciledWalletFreezes`, the same compare-and-set `unfreezeWalletSpend` as the admin door). A wallet debit also needs its `Dr WALLET` line (`assertWalletDebitJournaled`).
- **Support holds and remedies.** `EarningsHoldReason.SUPPORT_CASE` holds are placed by `lib/support/case-holds.ts`. A service complaint (`SupportCase`) refund is proposed as a `SupportFinancialRecommendation`, decided by a second operator, and only then executed through `refundBookingPayment` (`lib/support/case-actions.ts`); a recommendation moves no money. The staff caps in `lib/support/money-policy.ts` (`assertBoundedRefund`) are checked on that approve-and-execute path. A dispute is the gateway chargeback (`Dispute`), a different thing.
- **Ops proposals.** Back-office money doors above ₹25,000 answer `202` and wait for a second admin (`lib/backoffice/ops-proposals.ts`, `withOpsAction` in `lib/backoffice/ops-action-log.ts`).

## Invoice capture recovery

Provider holding reads reject nonpositive, unsafe or non-INR captured amounts;
invalid evidence must retain the order reference for recovery.

Lock a payable invoice without invalidating its current order. Booking-refund
credits and bank receipts retain the reference until gateway inspection; gateway
settlement clears it only when the captured payment has been accounted for.
The existing healer also reads PAID invoices with a retained reference, parks any
additional capture through canonical settlement and retires the exact inspected
pointer with its audit in one transaction. Live payments, read errors and
inconsistent paid-order evidence retain the pointer.

Checkout may replace a stale-amount unpaid order only after its bounded payment
read is empty. CAS the inspected order ID and recompute current debt inside the
attachment transaction; retain the replaced ID in the initiation audit. A provider
read is no cancellation: an old tab can still capture later with a lost webhook.
The healer does not consume audit history. Void/overage-credit clearers remain
separate coverage limits; do not claim discovery of every superseded order.

Use `applyOrgInvoiceCapture` with the transaction client for webhook, checkout
read-back and recovery. Keep receipt, journal, PAID audit and notification staging
atomic. An unreleased unapplied receipt stays in operator recovery; a later amount
match must not settle it again. Failed payment attempts retain retryable order
references. Keep provider waits inside the remaining admission budget and gateway
calls outside transactions. An SDK timeout does not cancel a provider effect.
Before dunning suspension, inspect the retained order and hold live, uncertain or
parked evidence, then recheck debt/order identity inside the claim transaction.
The suspension budget starts at stage entry, independently of earlier reminders.
Read `docs/maintenance/12-worker-recovery.md` for cadence, rotation and limits.
