# Go-live

This skill is an index. The proof for each step lives in the linked docs, so open them rather than trusting a summary here. The full checklist is [the money go-live checklist](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md); the README carries a [highlights version](../../../README.md#go-live-checklist-highlights). Never run a step yourself that moves real money or changes a live vendor setting. These are owner steps; prepare, explain and verify.

## Company prerequisites (before step 1)

These are owner and company steps that decide whether the live accounts can exist at all. None is code, and dates, amounts and names are kept in Notion (Familiarise / Tax & CA Working Notes / Tax, Legal & Regulatory Register), not here. Without them step 7 cannot happen.

- **The company PAN** is in hand and its details match the registered company.
- **A current account in the company's name is open and the capital deposit is made.** The settlement bank account in [checklist 1.1](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#11-account-dashboard) must be this account.
- **INC-20A is filed**, the company's declaration that business can commence, because the account and the payment-gateway activation depend on it.
- **Razorpay is activated as a registered company, with the legal pages live.** Activation reviews the public site, so the Terms, the Privacy policy and the refund page must be reachable and must say what the platform does (see `app/(pages)/` for the pages). An earlier individual application was rejected at KYC, so do not reuse it.
- **The expert-card badge wording is decided.** The badge on expert cards still reads "Verified by Familiarise" (driven by `isVerified`) while the marketing copy says "personally interviewed by our founder". The owner kept the badge on purpose; confirm it with the lawyer before go-live, or reword it (see the go-live checklist).
- **RazorpayX: confirm the bank and the GSTIN question.** Ask whether the RazorpayX account can sit on the same current account and whether it needs a GSTIN while `PLATFORM_GSTIN` is unset (the platform launches unregistered; step 3).

## The ordered sequence

Do these in order. Several only make sense after an earlier one.

1. **Resume the job fleet first.** Delete the Upstash key `ops:active_cron_platform` or set it to `netlify`, confirm a new production `cron-tick` row, then unpause the Better Stack heartbeats and probe and re-enable the Sentry monitor and detector. Commands: [the pause decision](../../../docs/decisions/2026-11-06-pause-scheduled-jobs-prelaunch.md#how-to-resume-go-live-or-any-time). The digest and freshness checks below need the fleet running.
2. **Rotate the exposed secrets.** Follow [the secret rotation runbook](../../../docs/maintenance/19-secret-rotation-runbook.md), section 3.1 first.
3. **Money settings before the live Razorpay keys.**
   - **GST registration before ₹20 lakh of turnover.** The platform launches unregistered (`PLATFORM_GSTIN` unset: no GST, receipts, organisation billing refused). Register when the daily ops digest's "GST registration" alarm fires at ₹15 lakh, and no later than 30 days after crossing ₹20 lakh; then set `PLATFORM_GSTIN` in Netlify production only. A malformed or wrong-state value makes checkout refuse with `GST_CONFIG_INVALID`. [The tax regime runbook](../../../docs/finance/60-tax/05-gst-registration-and-tax-regime.md#5-switch-over-runbook) and [checklist 1.2](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#12-keys-and-the-test-key-guard).
   - **Approve a `PlatformFeeSchedule`.** With none approved both rails fall back to 20% (`readActiveFeeSchedule`), which also sizes the profitability floor on every promotion. The approved `marketplaceBps` and `ownLinkBps` are kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Finance Lever Register); a maker proposes and a different checker approves.
   - **Set the referral programme.** Set the `ReferralProgramConfig` fields from the approved values kept in Notion (Familiarise / Growth & Marketing / Growth & Marketing Lever Register), starting with `monthlyBudgetPaise` (zero keeps learner referral off), then confirm the fields that stay at their defaults.
   - **Deactivate the seed discount codes** through the Supabase SQL gate, with the owner approving the statement; deactivate rather than delete, because seed payments point at them.
   - **Set the payout constants in code before live payouts.** The hold, the new-expert rule and the minimum payout are constants in `lib/payments/payouts/constants.ts`, not admin settings: `PAYOUT_CONSTANTS.HOLD_PERIOD_HOURS.CONSULTATION` (it must be long enough to cover the 48-hour scope-claim window the public promise describes, so an earning cannot be released while a claim can still be made), `NEW_EXPERT_EXTRA_HOLD_DAYS`, `NEW_EXPERT_LIFETIME_SHARE_PAISE` and `MINIMUM_PAYOUT_AMOUNT`. The approved values are kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Finance Lever Register); change them in a reviewed code pull request. Payouts run weekly (`create-payout-batch`, Mondays), and no copy may promise a faster payout.
   - **Per-format minimum prices (planned).** A price-bounds constants file, planned under `lib/payments/pricing/`, will hold a minimum and a default price per format and session length, with no maximum, and will be enforced when a plan is saved and at checkout price derivation. The file does not exist yet; its values are the one lever kept in code, and the reasoning is kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Finance Lever Register).
   - **Obtain a TAN before the first payout**, and **pin `TDS_ENGINE` and `ENABLE_TDS_194O_GROSS`** in Netlify production before `ENABLE_LIVE_PAYOUTS` (step 11). Both default to 194-O on the gross; the chartered accountant's answer decides the pinned values.
4. **Make the daily ops digest read clean.** No unexpected config skips and no paused or muted monitor. While `PLATFORM_GSTIN` is unset, the `PLATFORM_GSTIN not configured` skips of `heal-consumer-invoices`, `gst-outward-register-export`, `irp-uploader` and `settle-invoice-accruals` are expected (`isExpectedConfigSkip` in `lib/cron/config-skip.ts`): the digest lists them as a note, "Expected while unregistered for GST", and `/api/health/jobs` does not page on them. `generate-subscription-invoices` logs the same skip but records a completed run, so it never appears. Any other skip, or any skip after `PLATFORM_GSTIN` is set, is not expected and pages. [Checklist Part 3](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#part-3-the-daily-ops-digest-reads-clean).
5. **Set `EMAIL_DELIVERY_MODE=live`.** Same Part 3. Any other value holds mail to outside domains (`lib/email/delivery-guard.ts`). First confirm the Resend domains and the webhook ([Checklist 4.1](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#41-resend-domains-and-webhook)).
6. **Purge the sandbox identities, once.** [Checklist 1.5](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#15-clear-sandbox-identities-from-the-database). Never rerun it after a live key has minted any identity.
7. **Switch to live keys and run the two live ₹1 tests**, a checkout and a full refund. [Checklist 1.2 and 1.6](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#16-proof-for-collection-two-live-1-tests). Delete `RAZORPAY_ALLOW_TEST_KEYS_IN_PRODUCTION` in the same change.
8. **Unpause the eight Better Stack Telemetry alerts** only after the go-live data reset and once every money gauge in the latest `cron-tick summary` event reads zero. [Checklist 2.1](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#21-pre-flip-checks) and [the Better Stack setup guide](../../../docs/maintenance/00-betterstack-setup.md).
9. **Buy the Better Stack responder seat and the Telemetry bundle when their trigger fires**, not on the launch date; the trigger is kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Vendors & Spend). Until then the four money backlog alerts page by email and Slack. Once the seat exists, add phone and SMS to them. Same checklist 2.1 item.
10. **Buy a static-egress proxy before the first live payout**, set `RAZORPAYX_EGRESS_PROXY_URL`, and allowlist its addresses in RazorpayX. The vendor is open: QuotaGuard Static or any other fixed-IP proxy works, because the code only reads the URL (`lib/payments/payouts/razorpay-payouts.ts`). The first live payout is the latest moment; the purchase trigger is kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Vendors & Spend). Checklist 2.1, "Static egress".
11. **Flip payouts with a canary.** Set `ENABLE_LIVE_PAYOUTS=true`, redeploy, pay one ₹1.00 verified payout, watch it reach `COMPLETED`, then release the rest. [Checklist 2.3](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#23-flip-canary-rollback).
12. **Split the Stream app before real users arrive.** Create the Familiarise-dev app, put its four keys on the Netlify deploy-preview and branch-deploy contexts and in every local `.env`, run `npx tsx scripts/stream/ensure.ts --apply --routes-are-deployed` against it, then refresh and merge #2376 and rebuild previews. Also clear the mock users and channels from the production app: the step 6 purge is Razorpay-only and leaves Stream untouched. [The shared-app decision](../../../docs/decisions/2026-11-06-shared-stream-app-until-launch.md#when-this-changes).
13. **Notifications: Novu Production and the outbox recount. Done on 2026-11-08, in the window of release "Release 2026-11-08".** `npm run novu:sync -- --env production` applied all 16 families and two `npm run novu:check -- --env production` runs (08:29 and 08:34 UTC) were clean. In-App HMAC is on for the Production in-app integration, verified by behaviour: an inbox session with no hash or a wrong hash gets 400 "Please provide a valid HMAC hash", and the correct hash gets 201. `novu-step-failure-probe` ran once and completed with 0 failed steps. The read-only recount found 0 `PENDING`/`RETRY` rows in `FailedEmail`, `FailedEmailBatch` and `NotificationOutbox`, so the legacy outbox guards were deleted. Still to run before launch: the vendor-side checks in checklist 4.1 (Resend webhook events, DMARC report record, Netlify secret flags), which the code cannot show, and the test booking that proves `flushNotificationsAfter` delivers both the Novu bell and the Resend email (checklist 4.1 and Step 6 of `docs/notifications/05-pre-production-checklist.md`). [Checklist 4.2 and 4.3](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#42-novu-production-at-the-release-promotion), [decision record](../../../docs/decisions/2026-11-06-notification-single-preference-owner.md).
14. **Move GitHub to Team and require the slim check.** Org to Team with one seat per active contributor (the count is kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Vendors & Spend)), cancel the personal Pro once nothing personal depends on it, Indian credit card (debit only for a one-time "Pay now"), Actions budget about $15, require `Release checks` on `prod`. [Checklist Part 5](../../../docs/finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md#part-5-github-plan-and-ci-at-launch).
15. **Optional: name the grievance officer and sign the dark-pattern self-audit.** Fill `grievanceOfficerName` and `phone` in `lib/company.ts` so `/grievance` shows the Officer and Phone rows, then have the director sign [the self-audit record](../../../docs/compliance/06-dark-pattern-self-audit.md) after the browser walk and fill `DARK_PATTERN_AUDIT` in the same file. Setting the signatory name and the signing date publishes `/compliance-certificate`. Due by 31 December 2026 under the 2026 E-Commerce Rules, so it can follow launch.

## Traps

- **A standing config skip pages once live keys exist.** A paging job that has skipped for more than 7 days makes `/api/health/jobs` answer 503 (`STANDING_CONFIG_SKIP`) as soon as `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` is live. Clear skips before the keys go in. While `PLATFORM_GSTIN` is unset, the GST jobs' `PLATFORM_GSTIN not configured` skips are expected: `runOpsDigest` (`lib/observability/ops-digest.ts`) leaves them out of the stored `standingSkips` that `/api/health/jobs` reads, so they never page. Do not set a placeholder GSTIN. Once a real GSTIN is set, any config skip pages again.
- **The fleet switch fails open.** If Upstash is unreachable or slow (over 1 s), that tick runs the jobs even while the key says paused. Do not read "no run" as proof of a pause, and do not rely on the pause to keep jobs off real data.
- **Netlify secrets are write-only.** You cannot retrieve a value, only set a new one, so rotate rather than recover, and keep each value in the password manager.
- **`CRON_SECRET` lives in three places:** Netlify, the Better Stack monitor header and the Sentry detector header. A rotation that misses one makes that monitor fail with 401. See rotation runbook section 7.
- **Shared database.** Every context uses one Postgres, so a preview is not a sandbox. See [the environment split](../../../docs/start-here/10-environment-split.md).
- **Email stays held until step 5**, even with live payment keys.
- **A timed-out sidecar step leaves three uniques missing.** After `npm run db:push`, if `db:sidecars` fails with "Query read timeout" (`PG_QUERY_TIMEOUT_MS` defaults to 6000 ms), rerun `PG_QUERY_TIMEOUT_MS=60000 npx tsx -r dotenv/config scripts/db/apply-sidecars.ts --all && npx tsx -r dotenv/config scripts/ci/check-db-sidecars.ts` and confirm `appointment_feedback_level_key`, `consultant_earnings_occurrence_key` and `organization_earnings_split_key` exist (`.claude/skills/schema/references/this-repo.md`).
- **Do not sync Novu Production early.** Old production code still writes subscriber preferences, so `novu:sync --env production` waits for step 13's release window. Development and Production runs share one 10,000-run Novu quota.

## How to verify each step

| Step              | Check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fleet resumed     | A new production `cron-tick` row in `SystemJobExecution`; `/api/health/jobs` answers 200.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Secrets rotated   | The per-credential "how to verify" in the rotation runbook.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `PLATFORM_GSTIN`  | Unregistered: `heal-consumer-invoices` skips, and the ops digest's "GST registration" section shows the financial-year turnover. Registered: run it from System Jobs, expect `completed` with no config skip, and a new ₹1,000 checkout quotes ₹1,180 with `Payment.gstRegime = REGISTERED`. |
| Digest clean      | The latest ops digest email lists no item under "Config skips"; while unregistered it shows only the note "Expected while unregistered for GST: …" (the four jobs above), and once registered nothing. No paused monitor.                                                                    |
| Email live        | The digest's "Settings" line reads `EMAIL_DELIVERY_MODE: live`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Purge             | Stored `razorpayCustomerId`, contact and fund-account ids are null, and payout accounts show unverified until re-verified.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Live ₹1 tests     | `Payment` is `SUCCEEDED`, the `Refund` becomes `SUCCEEDED` via `refund.processed`, and the ledger imbalance query in 1.6 returns `0`.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Alerts unpaused   | Tick off each of the eight in checklist 2.1; the digest cannot see them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Novu Production   | Done 2026-11-08. `npm run novu:check -- --env production` exits `0`; the three outbox recounts read zero.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Egress and canary | No `RAZORPAYX_EGRESS_NOT_CONFIGURED` refusal; the payout reaches `COMPLETED` through the live `payout.processed` webhook; `reconcile-ledgers` is clean.                                                                                                                                      |

For the environment variables involved, see [the README's environment section](../../../README.md#environment-variables) and `.env.sample`.
