# Vertical Expansion Framework

**Last updated:** March 2026
**Purpose:** Standard template for all vertical-specific playbooks. Every new vertical follows this 12-section structure.

---

## How to Use This Framework

1. Each vertical playbook follows the exact 12-section structure below
2. Every section must be completed before launching outreach for that vertical
3. Each playbook should be independently executable — a future sales hire should be able to pick up a vertical doc and execute without reading every other doc
4. Estimated time to research and write a new vertical playbook: 4-6 hours
5. Cross-reference the Master GTM Strategy (`../00-master-gtm-strategy.md`) for overall timeline and metrics

---

## The 12-Section Template

### Section 1: Market Size & Opportunity (India)

Answer these questions:
- How many professionals exist in this vertical in India?
- What is the total addressable market size (in ₹)?
- What is the growth rate?
- What percentage are digitally active / willing to monetize online?
- Are there seasonal demand patterns (e.g., tax season for CAs)?
- What macro trends are driving or hindering this vertical?

Include: Hard numbers, sourced data, growth projections.

### Section 2: Consultant ICP (Who We Recruit)

Define the ideal consultant for this vertical:
- Experience level (years, seniority)
- Digital presence (LinkedIn connections, social followers, content output)
- Domain specialization (which sub-niches are highest-demand)
- Disqualified profiles (who should we NOT recruit and why)
- Scoring rubric: Rate each potential consultant 1-10 on:
  - Audience size (1-10)
  - Engagement quality (1-10)
  - Domain demand (1-10)
  - Platform fit (1-10)
  - Activity likelihood (1-10)
  - Minimum score to pursue: 25/50

### Section 3: Consultee ICP (Who Buys)

Define who will pay for consultations in this vertical:
- Primary personas (top 2-3 buyer types)
- Secondary personas
- Pain points driving them to seek consultation
- Budget expectations
- Where they currently solve this problem (and why it's inadequate)

### Section 4: Where to Find Consultants — Online

Rank channels by ROI for this specific vertical:
- Which social platforms are they most active on?
- Which communities/forums do they participate in?
- Which competitor platforms host them?
- Which newsletters, podcasts, or content do they follow?
- Specific search queries for LinkedIn/Twitter/Google

### Section 5: Where to Find Consultants — Offline

Identify physical touchpoints:
- Professional association events and chapters
- Conferences and summits (with specific event names and dates)
- College/university partnerships
- Coworking spaces and office clusters
- Local meetup groups
- Print/physical collateral opportunities

### Section 6: Messaging That Resonates

For each vertical, define:
- **Primary pain point** they experience today (one sentence)
- **Value proposition** Familiarise offers (one sentence)
- **Objection handling** — top 3-5 objections and scripted responses
- **Outreach DM template** — personalized for this vertical's language and concerns
- **Positioning statement** — how we describe ourselves TO this vertical (not generic)

### Section 7: Pricing Benchmarks

Research and document:
- What competitors charge in this vertical (both platform commission and consultant pricing)
- What offline equivalents charge (e.g., in-person CA consultation rates)
- Recommended pricing range for Familiarise consultants in this vertical
- Minimum pricing floors per service type
- Premium pricing opportunities (high-ticket niches)

### Section 8: Platform Features to Highlight

Rank Familiarise features by relevance to this vertical:
1. Which feature is the "killer feature" for this vertical? (e.g., document review for legal)
2. Which service types matter most? (e.g., subscriptions for ongoing CA advisory)
3. Which features are irrelevant or should be de-emphasized?
4. Are there feature gaps that would block this vertical?

### Section 9: Onboarding Friction Points

Identify barriers to getting consultants active:
- Technical literacy level (can they self-onboard or need hand-holding?)
- Time constraints (when are they busiest/least available?)
- Professional regulation concerns (advertising restrictions, licensing)
- Trust concerns specific to this vertical
- Solutions for each friction point

### Section 10: Digital Adoption Barriers

Broader barriers beyond individual onboarding:
- What percentage of this vertical is digitally active?
- Age demographics and tech comfort
- Regional/language considerations
- Cultural barriers to charging for online advice
- Infrastructure constraints (internet, devices)
- How competitors overcame these barriers

### Section 11: Competitive Landscape (Vertical-Specific)

Map the competitive landscape for this specific vertical:
- Direct competitors (platforms specifically serving this vertical)
- Indirect competitors (how the need is currently met without a platform)
- Our advantages vs. each competitor in this vertical
- Their advantages vs. us
- Migration opportunities (users on competing platforms we can target)

### Section 12: Founding Cohort Plan

Specific recruitment plan for this vertical's first 10-15 consultants:
- Total target: 10-15 founding consultants
- Source breakdown: How many from each channel (LinkedIn, communities, events, migrations, personal network)
- Timeline: How long to recruit this cohort
- Offer: Same founding member benefits (0% commission, badge, direct access) or modified?
- Success criteria: What does "this vertical is working" look like? (sessions/month, revenue, retention)
- Kill criteria: What signals tell us to pause/abandon this vertical?

## Vertical Launch Checklist

Before activating any vertical:

- [ ] 12-section playbook completed and reviewed
- [ ] Landing page created for this vertical (SEO-optimized)
- [ ] 10-15 founding consultants identified and contacted
- [ ] At least 5 consultants signed up and profiles complete
- [ ] 3+ free webinars scheduled for demand generation
- [ ] Messaging tested (sent 50+ outreach messages, measured response rate)
- [ ] Pricing validated (3+ consultants confirmed pricing is reasonable)
- [ ] Regulatory compliance verified (advertising rules, licensing, disclaimers)
- [ ] Cross-promotion plan with existing verticals defined
- [ ] Metrics dashboard set up for this vertical

## Vertical Expansion Order (Current Plan)

| Priority | Vertical | Timeline | Rationale |
|----------|----------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Tech/Engineering | Month 1-3 | Founder's network, highest digital adoption, proven demand |
| 2 | Finance/CA | Month 3-5 | ICAI advertising liberalization Apr 2026, structured profession, seasonal demand |
| 3 | Creative Arts | Month 5-7 | Low regulatory friction, high social media presence, easy digital acquisition |
| 4 | Legal | Month 7-9 | High willingness to pay, complex regulation requires preparation |
| 5 | Medicine/Health | Month 9-12 | Highest regulatory barriers, start with non-doctor experts |

This order is not fixed — adjust based on:
- Which verticals show organic demand (consultants signing up unprompted)
- Where founding cohort recruitment succeeds fastest
- Regulatory developments (e.g., if BCI loosens advertising rules, move Legal up)
- Competitive moves (if a competitor enters a vertical, accelerate or avoid)
