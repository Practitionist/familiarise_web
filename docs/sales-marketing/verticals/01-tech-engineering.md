## 1. Market Size & Opportunity

### India Software Engineering Workforce

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Software engineers in India | 4.3-5.8M | Nasscom (CAGR 7-8%) |
| Digitally active (social, communities, forums) | 70-80% | Highest of any professional vertical |
| r/developersIndia members | 400K+ | Reddit (growing ~5K/month) |

### Demand Signals

| Signal | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Tech interview prep market | $2B+ globally; India is the single largest demand source |
| "System design interview" search volume | Highest in the consultation/mentorship category on Google Trends India |
| FAANG/product company prep | Massive seasonal demand — placement season runs **Sep-Mar** |
| Remote work normalization (post-COVID) | Engineers in Tier 2-3 cities actively seek mentorship from Tier 1 experts; geography no longer a barrier |
| Career transitions (backend to ML, SDE to PM) | Growing segment willing to pay for structured guidance |

### Why Tech Is the Launch Vertical

1. **Highest digital fluency** — zero friction adopting an online platform.
2. **Proven willingness to pay** — engineers already spend on courses, mock interviews, and career coaching.
3. **Seasonal spikes** — placement season (Sep-Mar) creates predictable, recurring demand surges.
4. **Community density** — concentrated in discoverable online communities (Reddit, Twitter, Discord, GitHub).
5. **Content virality** — tech content (system design threads, open-source projects, technical blogs) spreads organically.

## 2. Consultant ICP

### Primary ICP

Senior engineers (5-15 years experience), tech leads, architects, and engineering managers who meet the following criteria:

| Attribute | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Experience | 5-15 years in software engineering |
| LinkedIn connections | 2K+ (or 1K+ followers) |
| Content creation | Active in at least one channel: blog, Twitter/X, YouTube, open-source |
| Domain | One or more high-demand sub-niches (see ranking below) |
| Verifiability | Public GitHub, published blog, conference talks, or verifiable employer |

### Sub-Niches Ranked by Demand

| Rank | Sub-Niche | Demand Level | Session Type Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System design | Very high | 1:1 consultations, webinars |
| 2 | DSA / competitive programming | Very high | 1:1 mock interviews, classes |
| 3 | Backend architecture | High | 1:1 consultations, subscriptions |
| 4 | DevOps / cloud (AWS, GCP, K8s) | High | 1:1 consultations, webinars |
| 5 | Mobile development (iOS, Android, Flutter) | Medium-high | 1:1, classes |
| 6 | ML / AI | Medium-high (growing fast) | 1:1, webinars, classes |
| 7 | Frontend / React | Medium | 1:1, classes |
| 8 | Career coaching for engineers | Medium | 1:1, subscriptions |

### Secondary ICP

- Engineering managers transitioning to coaching (leadership + technical credibility).
- CTOs advising early-stage startups (high session pricing, lower volume).
- Retired senior engineers with deep domain knowledge and available time.

### Disqualified Profiles

| Profile | Reason |
|---|---|
| Engineers with <3 years experience | Insufficient credibility for paid consultations |
| No verifiable work (no GitHub, no blog, no identifiable employer) | Consultees cannot validate expertise |
| Inactive online presence (no posts in 6+ months) | Unlikely to drive organic bookings |

### ICP Scoring Matrix

Use this to prioritize outreach targets:

| Factor | Weight | Scoring (1-5) |
|---|---|---|
| Audience size (LinkedIn + Twitter + YouTube) | 25% | 1 = <1K, 2 = 1-5K, 3 = 5-10K, 4 = 10-50K, 5 = 50K+ |
| Engagement quality (comments, reshares, discussions) | 25% | 1 = ghost followers, 5 = active conversations |
| Domain demand (per sub-niche ranking above) | 20% | 1 = niche, 5 = system design / DSA |
| Platform fit (needs 1:1 + subscriptions + webinars) | 15% | 1 = only wants async, 5 = wants all 4 service types |
| Activity likelihood (responsiveness, time availability) | 15% | 1 = too busy, 5 = actively looking for side income |

**Target score for founding cohort:** 3.5+ out of 5.0 weighted average.

## 6. Messaging That Resonates

### Primary Pain Point

> **"You spend hours giving free advice on LinkedIn DMs and WhatsApp. That's consulting — you're just not getting paid for it."**

This lands because every senior engineer recognizes the pattern: juniors DM them constantly for career advice, resume reviews, and interview prep. They already do the work. Familiarise just formalizes and monetizes it.

### Core Value Proposition

> **"One platform: 1:1 mock interviews, subscription mentorship, group webinars, and multi-week bootcamps. Built-in HD video. ₹900 per ₹1,000 session (vs ₹830 on TopMate)."**

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I already use TopMate" | "Try us in parallel at 0% commission for 3 months. Compare your take-home after fees. Keep whichever earns you more. No exclusivity required." |
| "I don't have a big audience" | "You don't need 100K followers. You need 10 people who trust your system design skills. Start with your LinkedIn network. We've seen consultants with 2K connections fill their first 10 slots." |
| "Is the video quality good?" | "Stream.io HD — same infrastructure powering major platforms. Built-in, no Zoom links needed. Session recording is automatic. Book a demo and test it yourself." |
| "What about Preplaced?" | "Preplaced takes 20% commission. We take 10% (0% for founding members for 3 months). On a ₹2,000 session, that's ₹400 more in your pocket per session. Over 20 sessions/month, that's ₹8,000/month difference." |
| "Will I actually get bookings?" | "We guarantee your first session within 2 weeks of going live, or we extend your 0% commission period. We actively drive demand through SEO, content, and community partnerships." |
| "I don't have time" | "Start with 2 slots per week. That's 2 hours. If a ₹2,000/session earns you ₹4,000/week for 2 hours of work, that's ₹2,000/hr — likely more than your day job's effective hourly rate." |

### DM Template (Tech-Specific)

> Hi [Name],
>
> Loved your [post about X / open-source project Y / talk at Z conference]. [One specific sentence about what you found insightful.]
>
> I'm building Familiarise — like TopMate but with integrated HD video, session recordings, document review, and 4 service types (1:1s, subscriptions, webinars, classes).
>
> We're recruiting 30 founding members in tech at 0% commission for 3 months. Founding Member badge is permanent.
>
> 15-min demo call this week?

**Rules for using this template:**
1. Never batch-send. Personalize every message.
2. The `[specific thing]` must be real and specific — a blog post title, a GitHub repo name, a conference talk topic.
3. Follow up once after 5 days. No more.
4. If they decline, ask: "No worries. Know anyone who might be interested?"

See the GTM doc ([`05-go-to-market-india.md`](../../competition/05-go-to-market-india.md), Phase 0) for the full outreach principles and follow-up cadence.

### Commission Comparison

| Platform | Commission | Consultant Take-Home per ₹1,000 |

| Session Duration | Minimum Price |
|---|---|

| 30 min | ₹499 |

### Pricing Strategy

- **Do not compete on price.** Compete on integrated experience (video + recording + document review + 4 service types).
- Guide consultants who undervalue their time: "If your employer bills you at $100/hr, charging ₹2,000 for a 60-min consultation is reasonable and likely still underpriced."
- Encourage tiered pricing: free webinar (top of funnel) -> paid 1:1 consultation -> subscription mentorship (highest LTV).

## 8. Platform Features to Highlight

### Feature Priority for Tech Vertical

| Priority | Feature | Why It Matters for Tech |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (KILLER) | **Built-in Stream.io HD video** | No Zoom links. Automatic recording. Chat before/during/after session. Engineers hate context-switching between tools. |
| 2 | **Document review** | Resume review, code review, LinkedIn profile review. **Unique to Familiarise** — no competitor offers this. |
| 3 | **Subscription service** | Monthly mentorship with automatic scheduling. Creates recurring revenue for consultants. Ideal for ongoing career coaching. |
| 4 | **Webinars** | "System Design in 45 Minutes" free webinars as top-of-funnel. Converts attendees to paid 1:1 clients. |
| 5 | **Classes** | Multi-week bootcamp-style programs (e.g., "8-Week System Design Masterclass"). Cohort revenue model. |
| 6 | **Trial sessions** | Free 15-min trial with conversion tracking. Lowers barrier for first booking. |
| 7 | **UPI payments** | Frictionless for Indian users. ₹900 per ₹1,000 vs TopMate's ₹830. |

### Features to De-Emphasize (Initially)

| Feature | Why De-Emphasize |
|---|---|
| Collaborator revenue splits | Less relevant for solo tech consultants in the founding cohort. Becomes relevant later for group classes. |
| Multi-currency payments | Indian tech vertical is primarily domestic. International payments matter more in later verticals. |

### Feature-to-Use-Case Mapping

| Use Case | Primary Feature | Supporting Features |
|---|---|---|
| Mock interview prep | 1:1 consultation + video | Session recording (candidate can review) |
| Resume review | Document review | Chat (async follow-up) |
| Career mentorship | Subscription | Video + chat + document review |
| "System Design 101" session | Webinar | Trial session (convert to paid 1:1) |
| "8-Week DSA Bootcamp" | Class | Video + document review + chat |

## 9. Onboarding Friction Points

### Overall Friction Level: LOW

Tech professionals are digitally native. They adopt new tools quickly. The main friction is motivational, not technical.

### Friction Point Analysis

| Friction Point | Severity | Root Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| "I don't have time" | High | Engineers work long hours; side income feels like side work | Emphasize: 2 slots/week minimum = 2 hours. Frame as highest-ROI side income. |
| "I'm not sure I'm qualified" | Medium | Imposter syndrome is rampant in tech | Validate their expertise during the demo call. "If juniors DM you for advice, you're qualified." |
| "Will I get bookings?" | Medium | Fear of empty calendar (embarrassing publicly) | Offer "first session guarantee" — platform drives first booking within 2 weeks. |
| Profile completion — writing a bio | Medium | Engineers hate writing about themselves | Offer to write their bio during the demo call. Ask 3 questions, draft it live. |
| Pricing anxiety | Medium | Engineers undervalue their time | Guide explicitly: "Your employer bills $100/hr for your time. ₹2,000/session is reasonable." |
| Setting up availability | Low | Just a calendar UI | Show them during demo. Takes 2 minutes. |
| Technical setup (video, etc.) | Very low | Stream.io is browser-based | "Click the link. That's it. No downloads." |

### Onboarding Checklist for Tech Consultants

1. Profile photo + bio (offer to write for them)
2. At least 1 service type configured (recommend starting with 1:1 consultations)
3. Pricing set (guide to market benchmarks above)
4. Availability: minimum 2 slots/week
5. First session booked (platform-assisted)
6. Post-session review collected

## 10. Digital Adoption Barriers

### Overall Assessment: ALMOST NONE

The tech/engineering vertical has the **lowest digital adoption barriers** of any professional vertical in India. Engineers live online.

### Minor Barriers

| Barrier | Affected Segment | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small social media audience | Engineers active on GitHub/open-source but not LinkedIn/Twitter | Low | Leverage GitHub profile + technical blog as alternative discovery. Platform SEO drives inbound. |
| Slower internet (Tier 2-3 cities) | Engineers in smaller cities working remotely | Low | Stream.io supports adaptive bitrate. Ensure platform works on moderate bandwidth (~2 Mbps). |
| Camera shyness | Engineers who prefer text communication | Low | Emphasize that document review and chat features work without video. But 1:1 sessions need video. |

### Non-Barriers (Confirmed)

| Factor | Status |
|---|---|
| Language | English is the default for Indian tech professionals. Hindi not required for this vertical. |
| Payment methods | UPI is universal. Credit/debit cards common. No payment friction. |
| Platform trust | Engineers evaluate tools rationally. A clean UI + working video demo is sufficient. |
| App vs. web | Web-first is fine. Engineers don't need a mobile app for this use case. |

## 12. Founding Cohort Plan

### Target

**30 consultants** — the largest founding cohort of any vertical. Tech is our launch vertical and must have critical mass.

### Sourcing Breakdown

| Source Channel | Target Count | Effort Level | Conversion Rate (Expected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn outreach | 10 | High (personalized DMs) | ~15% of outreach messages |
| Twitter/X DMs | 5 | Medium | ~10% of outreach messages |
| GitHub / Hashnode / Dev.to | 5 | Medium | ~12% of outreach messages |
| TopMate migration | 5 | Medium (identify active tech consultants) | ~20% (already validated the model) |
| Conference speakers | 3 | Low (warm introductions) | ~25% (high credibility match) |
| Personal network | 2 | Very low | ~50%+ |
| **Total** | **30** | | |

### Timeline

| Week | Activity | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Build prospect lists across all channels. Score using ICP matrix (Section 2). | 150+ qualified prospects identified |
| Week 2 | Begin outreach — LinkedIn (batch 1), Twitter (batch 1), personal network (all). | 80 messages sent, 20 responses |
| Week 3 | Continue outreach — GitHub/Hashnode, TopMate migration, conference speakers. Follow up on Week 2 non-responders. | 120 more messages sent, 15 demo calls booked |
| Week 4 | Close remaining sign-ups. Onboard all confirmed consultants (profile, pricing, availability). | 30 sign-ups, 20 profiles fully completed |

### Founding Member Offer

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Commission | 0% for first 3 months (100% of earnings kept) |
| Founding Member badge | Permanent badge on consultant profile |
| Direct product input | Monthly feedback call, feature request priority |
| Priority support | Direct WhatsApp/Slack channel with founding team |
| Early SEO advantage | Profile pages indexed before competition floods the platform |

### Success Criteria

| Metric | Target | Measurement Period |
|---|---|---|
| Consultant sign-ups | 30 | End of Week 4 |
| Active consultants (2+ sessions/week) | 20 | End of Month 1 |
| Total sessions conducted | 100 | End of Month 1 |
| Consultee satisfaction (post-session survey) | 4.0+ / 5.0 | End of Month 1 |
| Consultant retention (still active) | 80% (24/30) | End of Month 3 |

### Kill Criteria

> **If fewer than 10 sign-ups after 200 outreach messages, STOP and revisit:**
> 1. Is the messaging wrong? (Test alternate pain points.)
> 2. Is the ICP wrong? (Are we targeting too senior / too junior?)
> 3. Is the product not ready? (Missing a table-stakes feature?)
> 4. Is the market timing wrong? (Are we outside placement season?)
