  ---
  The #1 Blocker: You Need a Business Entity First

  Neither Razorpay nor Stripe will activate live payments without a registered business. Here are your options:

  Option A: Sole Proprietorship (Fastest)

  - Cost: Rs. 500-3,000
  - Timeline: 1-3 days
  - Needs: Personal PAN, Aadhaar, Shop & Establishment certificate
  - Downside: Unlimited liability, harder to raise investment

  Option B: LLP (Recommended for you)

  - Cost: Rs. 7,000-15,000 (govt + professional fees)
  - Timeline: 10-15 working days
  - Needs: 2 designated partners, DSC (~Rs. 1,500/partner), DIN, PAN/Aadhaar, registered office proof
  - Why this: Limited liability, lower compliance than Pvt Ltd, can own US entity later via Stripe Atlas

  Option C: Pvt Ltd (Best for fundraising)

  - Cost: Rs. 10,000-80,000 (depending on authorized capital)
  - Timeline: 3-6 weeks realistic
  - Why consider: Can issue equity, most credible for investors, Startup India benefits

  My recommendation: If you have a co-founder, go LLP now and convert to Pvt Ltd when you raise funding. If solo, consider OPC (One Person Company, Rs. 10,500-20,000).

  Critical Finding: Stripe is Invite-Only in India

  Since May 2024, you cannot sign up directly for Stripe India. You must request an invite from Stripe's sales team. This is likely NOT in your existing docs.

  To maximize approval chances:
  1. Have a GST-registered entity ready
  2. Ensure exact name matching across PAN, GST, and bank account
  3. Publish Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy on your website
  4. Describe your marketplace model and expected volumes

  GST is mandatory only above Rs. 40 lakh/year turnover. But register early because:
  - Needed for Stripe India approval
  - Claim input tax credit on Razorpay's 18% GST charges on their fees
  - Builds credibility
  - Cost: Rs. 0 (govt) + Rs. 500-5,000 (CA fees)
  - Timeline: 3-7 working days with Aadhaar authentication

  Tax Compliance You Must Build Into the Platform

  TDS under Section 194O (mandatory for marketplaces):

  - Rate: 0.1% of gross amount on consultant payouts

  - Key: This replaces 194J (professional fees), so you DON'T need to deduct at 2%

  GST TCS (Tax Collected at Source):

  - Rate: 1% on net taxable supplies
  - Consultants claim credit in their GSTR-3B

  Your commission: 18% GST applies on your platform fee
