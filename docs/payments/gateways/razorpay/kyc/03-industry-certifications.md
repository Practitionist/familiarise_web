## Overview

### Why Industry-Specific Certifications?

Beyond standard KYC documents (business registration, PAN, ID proof), certain industries require additional licenses and certifications to:

1. **Regulatory Compliance**: Meet industry-specific regulations (RBI, SEBI, FSSAI, etc.)
2. **Customer Protection**: Ensure service providers are qualified and authorized
3. **Risk Management**: Verify legitimacy and reduce fraud risk
4. **Legal Authorization**: Confirm business is legally allowed to operate in that sector

### Who Needs Additional Certifications?

If your business operates in **regulated industries** like:

- Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals
- Financial Services & Insurance
- Education & Training
- Food & Beverage
- Travel & Tourism
- Real Estate
- Legal Services
- Media & Entertainment
- Professional Services

You will likely need to provide industry-specific licenses in addition to standard KYC documents.

### Impact on KYC Approval

- ❌ **Without required certification**: KYC will be rejected
- ⏱️ **With incomplete certification**: Verification delayed pending documentation
- ✅ **With proper certification**: Smooth approval process

## Certification Categories

### 1. Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals

#### Required for:

- Pharmacies, drug stores
- Hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers
- Telemedicine platforms
- Health & wellness products
- Medical equipment sales

#### Common Certifications:

**Drug License / Pharmacy License**

- **Issued by**: State Drug Control Department
- **Purpose**: Authorize sale of medicines
- **Types**:
  - Retail License (for pharmacies)
  - Wholesale License (for distributors)
- **Renewal**: Annual/2-3 years
- **How to obtain**: Apply through State Drug Controller

**NABH / NABL Certification**

- **NABH**: National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers
- **NABL**: National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories
- **Purpose**: Quality certification for healthcare facilities
- **Issued by**: Quality Council of India
- **Renewal**: Every 3 years

**Medical Council Registration**

- **For**: Individual doctors/practitioners
- **Issued by**: Medical Council of India (MCI) / State Medical Council
- **Purpose**: License to practice medicine
- **Verification**: Online verification available on NMC website

**Clinical Establishment License**

- **For**: Clinics, hospitals, diagnostic centers
- **Issued by**: State Health Department / Local Authority
- **Purpose**: Authorization to operate medical facility
- **Requirements**: Facility inspection, qualified staff

### 2. Financial Services

#### Required for:

- NBFCs (lending, investment)
- Insurance companies and agents
- Stock brokers, mutual fund distributors
- Payment services, fintech

#### Common Certifications:

**RBI NBFC Certificate of Registration**

- **Issued by**: Reserve Bank of India
- **For**: Non-Banking Financial Companies
- **Types**:
  - Deposit-taking NBFC
  - Non-deposit-taking NBFC
  - NBFC-MFI (Microfinance)
  - NBFC-P2P (Peer-to-Peer Lending)
- **Requirements**: Minimum net owned funds ₹2 crore
- **Process**: Application through RBI's COSMOS portal
- **Timeline**: 6-12 months

**SEBI Registration**

- **Issued by**: Securities and Exchange Board of India
- **For**:
  - Stock brokers
  - Portfolio managers
  - Investment advisors
  - Merchant bankers
- **Requirements**: Capital adequacy, fit and proper criteria
- **Renewal**: Annual compliance required

**IRDAI License**

- **Issued by**: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India
- **For**:
  - Insurance companies (life, general, health)
  - Insurance brokers
  - Insurance agents
- **Requirements**: Training, certification exams
- **Verification**: Online verification on IRDAI website

**AMFI Registration (ARN)**

- **Full Form**: AMFI Registration Number
- **Issued by**: Association of Mutual Funds in India
- **For**: Mutual fund distributors
- **Requirements**: NISM certification, registration fee
- **Renewal**: Annual renewal required

**RBI Payment Aggregator License**

- **For**: Payment gateway services
- **Requirements**: Minimum capital ₹15 crore, compliance framework
- **Process**: Multi-stage approval from RBI
- **Note**: Existing aggregators had transition period

### 3. Food & Beverage

#### Required for:

- Restaurants, cloud kitchens
- Food delivery platforms
- Food manufacturing/processing
- Packaged food sales

#### Common Certifications:

**FSSAI License**

- **Full Form**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
- **Mandatory for**: All food businesses
- **Types**:
  - **Basic Registration**: Turnover < ₹12 lakh/year
  - **State License**: Turnover ₹12 lakh - ₹20 crore/year
  - **Central License**: Turnover > ₹20 crore/year OR multi-state operations
- **Validity**: 1-5 years (renewable)
- **How to obtain**: Online application at [fssai.gov.in](https://fssai.gov.in)
- **Documents required**:
  - Business proof
  - Food safety management plan
  - List of food products
  - NOC from local authority (if applicable)

**FSSAI Certificate Requirements:**

- 14-digit FSSAI license number
- Should be active/valid
- Category should match business type
- For food delivery platforms: Central License required

**State Excise License**

- **For**: Sale of alcoholic beverages
- **Issued by**: State Excise Department
- **Varies by state**: Different states have different rules
- **Types**: Retail, bar, restaurant license
- **Highly regulated**: Age restrictions, location restrictions

### 4. Travel & Tourism

#### Required for:

- Travel agencies
- Tour operators
- Hotels, resorts
- Vehicle rental services

#### Common Certifications:

**IATA License**

- **Full Form**: International Air Transport Association
- **For**: Travel agencies (especially international ticketing)
- **Purpose**: Issue airline tickets
- **Requirements**: Financial stability, trained staff, IATA BSP account
- **Verification**: IATA number verification
- **Alternative**: Tie-up with IATA-registered agency

**State Tourism License**

- **Issued by**: State Tourism Department
- **For**: Tour operators, travel agencies
- **Requirements**: Office premises, trained guides
- **Renewal**: Annual

**Hotel License**

- **Issued by**: Local Municipal Corporation / Tourism Department
- **For**: Hotels, guest houses, resorts
- **Requirements**: Fire safety NOC, health license
- **Renewal**: Annual

### 5. Education

#### Required for:

- Schools, colleges, universities
- Online education platforms
- Coaching centers
- Skill training institutes

#### Common Certifications:

**University/Board Affiliation**

- **For**: Educational institutions offering recognized degrees/certificates
- **Issued by**:
  - University (for affiliated colleges)
  - CBSE/ICSE/State Board (for schools)
  - UGC/AICTE/NCTE (for technical/professional courses)
- **Requirements**: Infrastructure, faculty qualifications
- **Verification**: Mentioned on certificates

**Ministry of Education Approval**

- **For**: Online education platforms offering degree programs
- **Required if**: Offering recognized degrees/diplomas
- **Not required if**: Only offering skill courses, tutorials

**Local Authority License**

- **For**: Coaching centers, skill training centers
- **Issued by**: Municipal Corporation / Local Authority
- **Requirements**: Fire safety, building approval

### 6. Real Estate

#### Required for:

- Real estate developers
- Real estate brokers/agents
- Property management companies

#### Common Certifications:

**RERA Registration**

- **Full Form**: Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act
- **Mandatory for**:
  - Real estate developers (for projects)
  - Real estate agents/brokers
- **Purpose**: Consumer protection, transparency
- **Types**:
  - **Project Registration**: For developers (each project)
  - **Agent Registration**: For brokers/agents
- **Issued by**: State RERA Authority
- **Validity**: Project-specific or annual (for agents)
- **How to obtain**: Apply through State RERA portal
- **Documents required**:
  - Land title documents
  - Building plan approvals
  - Financial statements
  - Company registration documents
- **Verification**: Online verification on respective State RERA website

## How to Obtain Certifications

### General Process

1. **Identify Required Certificate**
   - Check industry regulations
   - Consult with regulatory body
   - Review Razorpay requirements

2. **Prepare Prerequisites**
   - Business registration
   - Minimum capital (if required)
   - Qualified personnel
   - Office/facility setup

3. **Gather Documents**
   - Business registration documents
   - Premises proof
   - Staff qualifications
   - Financial statements

4. **Apply Online/Offline**
   - Most certifications have online portals
   - Submit application with documents
   - Pay application fees

5. **Inspection (if required)**
   - Some licenses require physical inspection
   - Facility/premises verification
   - Staff interview/verification

6. **Receive Certificate**
   - Approval notification
   - Download/collect certificate
   - Note renewal date

7. **Maintain Compliance**
   - Annual renewals
   - Regular reporting
   - Audit compliance

### Typical Timelines

| --------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| FSSAI Basic Registration          | 7 days                  |
| FSSAI State License               | 30-60 days              |
| RBI NBFC Registration             | 6-12 months             |
| SEBI Registration                 | 2-6 months              |
| RERA Registration                 | 30-90 days              |
| Professional Council Registration | 1-3 months              |
| State-specific Licenses           | 15-90 days              |

## Common Questions

### Do I need certification if I'm just starting?

**Yes**, if your industry is regulated. You cannot process payments legally without required licenses. Obtain certifications before going live.

### What if my certification is in process?

Continue using **Test Mode** until certification is approved. Do not go live without valid certifications.

### Can I use someone else's license?

**No**. Licenses are entity-specific. Using another entity's license is illegal and will result in KYC rejection.

### My business has multiple verticals. Which certifications do I need?

Provide certifications for **all** regulated verticals. For example, if you sell both medicines (need Drug License) and food (need FSSAI), provide both.

### Certification expired after KYC approval. What to do?

**Renew immediately** and update Razorpay dashboard. Expired certifications can lead to account suspension.

### Can I start with basic registration and upgrade later?

**Yes** for some certifications (like FSSAI). Start with basic registration, then upgrade as your business grows. Keep Razorpay updated.

### What if my industry is not listed?

- Check with Razorpay support if your industry needs additional certifications
- If truly unregulated, standard KYC documents are sufficient
- When in doubt, reach out to Razorpay KYC team

### How to verify if a certification is valid?

Most regulatory bodies offer online verification:

- **FSSAI**: [foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in](https://foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in)
- **MCI/NMC**: [nmc.org.in](https://www.nmc.org.in)
- **RERA**: Respective State RERA websites
- **IRDAI**: [irdai.gov.in](https://www.irdai.gov.in)
- **SEBI**: [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in)
