## Overview

Razorpay supports **12 different business types**, each with specific document requirements based on their legal structure. Selecting the correct business type is crucial for successful KYC approval.

### Why Business Type Matters

- **Document Requirements**: Different business types require different documents
- **Legal Compliance**: Must match your actual business registration
- **Tax Implications**: Affects GST and tax reporting
- **Settlement Process**: Determines account holder name requirements
- **Verification Time**: Some types require additional verification steps

### Choosing Your Business Type

Select based on:

1. Your **legal business registration** (most important)
2. **Tax registration** (GST, PAN)
3. **Business structure** as per government records

**IMPORTANT**: Choose the business type that matches your official registration. Mismatched information will result in KYC rejection.

## Detailed Requirements by Business Type

### 1. Private Limited Company

#### Description

A company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, with limited liability and owned by shareholders. Most common for startups and SMEs.

#### Examples

- Tech startups (e.g., "TechCorp Private Limited")
- E-commerce businesses
- SaaS companies
- Product companies

#### Required Documents

| Document                            | Description                                             | Format          | Notes                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Certificate of Incorporation**    | Official document from MCA proving company registration | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must show company name, date of incorporation |
| **CIN (Corporate Identity Number)** | 21-digit unique company identification number           | Text/Screenshot | Format: U12345MH2020PTC123456                 |
| **Company PAN Card**                | PAN card in company's name                              | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must match company name exactly               |
| **Cancelled Cheque**                | Cancelled cheque with bank account details visible      | JPG/PNG/PDF     | Account must be in company's name             |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof**   | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License                | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must be government-issued, current            |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**        | PAN card of the person authorized to sign               | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must match ID proof name                      |

#### Key Points

- Company name on all documents must match exactly
- Authorized signatory must be a director (as per MCA records)
- Bank account must be a current account in company's name
- CIN can be verified on MCA portal

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Using personal bank account instead of company account
- ❌ Authorized signatory not listed as director in MCA
- ❌ Name mismatch between documents
- ❌ Using old/expired ID proofs

### 2. Public Limited Company

#### Description

A company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, with shares that can be publicly traded. Larger corporations with broader ownership.

#### Examples

- Listed companies on stock exchanges
- Large corporations (e.g., "BigCorp Limited")
- Companies with public shareholders

#### Required Documents

| Document                            | Description                                             | Format          | Notes                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Certificate of Incorporation**    | Official document from MCA proving company registration | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must show company name, registration date |
| **CIN (Corporate Identity Number)** | 21-digit unique company identification number           | Text/Screenshot | Format: L12345MH2020PLC123456             |
| **Company PAN Card**                | PAN card in company's name                              | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must match legal name                     |
| **Cancelled Cheque**                | Cancelled cheque with bank account details              | JPG/PNG/PDF     | Must be company current account           |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof**   | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License                | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Government-issued, current                |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**        | PAN card of authorized person                           | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must match ID proof                       |

#### Key Points

- Same requirements as Private Limited
- CIN format differs (contains "PLC" instead of "PTC")
- May require board resolution for authorized signatory
- Additional scrutiny for compliance

### 3. Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

#### Description

A partnership where partners have limited liability. Combines benefits of partnership flexibility with company's limited liability protection.

#### Examples

- Professional services firms (CA firms, law firms)
- Consulting businesses
- Design agencies
- IT service providers

#### Required Documents

| Document                          | Description                              | Format          | Notes                                 |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Certificate of Incorporation**  | LLP incorporation certificate from MCA   | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Shows LLP name and incorporation date |
| **LLPIN**                         | LLP Identification Number (8 digits)     | Text/Screenshot | Format: AAA-1234                      |
| **LLP Deed**                      | LLP Agreement registered with MCA        | PDF             | Signed by all partners                |
| **Company PAN Card**              | PAN card in LLP's name                   | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Name must match LLP registration      |
| **Cancelled Cheque**              | Cancelled cheque with account details    | JPG/PNG/PDF     | Account in LLP's name                 |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof** | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must be a designated partner          |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**      | PAN of designated partner                | PDF/JPG/PNG     | Must match ID proof                   |

#### Key Points

- Provide ANY ONE of: Certificate/LLPIN/LLP Deed
- Authorized signatory must be a designated partner
- LLP Deed should be properly stamped and registered
- LLPIN can be verified on MCA portal

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Using partner's personal account instead of LLP account
- ❌ Unauthorized partner signing documents
- ❌ Unregistered or unstamped LLP Deed
- ❌ Name variation between Certificate and PAN

### 4. Partnership Firm

#### Description

A traditional partnership where two or more people share profits and liabilities. Can be registered or unregistered, but registration is required for KYC.

#### Examples

- Local retail shops
- Small trading businesses
- Family businesses
- Traditional businesses

#### Required Documents

| Document                          | Description                                              | Format      | Notes                                   |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Registered Partnership Deed**   | Partnership agreement registered with Registrar of Firms | PDF         | Must be stamped and registered          |
| **Company PAN Card**              | PAN card in partnership firm's name                      | PDF/JPG/PNG | Issued to firm, not individual partners |
| **Cancelled Cheque**              | Cancelled cheque with account details                    | JPG/PNG/PDF | Account in firm's name                  |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof** | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License                 | PDF/JPG/PNG | Must be a partner in the firm           |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**      | PAN of the partner                                       | PDF/JPG/PNG | Individual partner's PAN                |

#### Key Points

- Partnership Deed **must be registered** (not just notarized)
- Firm must have its own PAN (separate from partners' PANs)
- Bank account must be in firm's name
- All partners should be listed in Partnership Deed

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Submitting unregistered Partnership Deed
- ❌ Using partner's individual PAN instead of firm's PAN
- ❌ Bank account in partner's individual name
- ❌ Partnership Deed not properly stamped

### 5. Sole Proprietorship

#### Description

A business owned and operated by a single individual. No separate legal entity from the owner. Most common for freelancers and small businesses.

#### Examples

- Freelancers
- Independent consultants
- Small retail shops
- Home-based businesses
- Individual service providers

#### Required Documents

| Document                                    | Description                              | Format      | Notes                                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **MSME Certificate** OR **GST Certificate** | Government registration proof            | PDF/JPG/PNG | Must provide at least ONE            |
| **Cancelled Cheque**                        | Cancelled cheque with account details    | JPG/PNG/PDF | Can be in proprietor's personal name |
| **Proprietor PAN Card**                     | Individual PAN of business owner         | PDF/JPG/PNG | Owner's personal PAN                 |
| **Proprietor ID Proof**                     | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License | PDF/JPG/PNG | Government-issued ID                 |
| **Proprietor Address Proof**                | Utility bill/Rent agreement/Aadhar       | PDF/JPG/PNG | Proof of business address            |

#### Key Points

- **No company PAN required** (use individual PAN)
- Provide **either** MSME Certificate **OR** GST Certificate
- Bank account can be in proprietor's personal name
- Business name optional (can operate under personal name)
- Easiest to set up among all business types

#### MSME vs GST Certificate

**MSME Certificate (Udyam Registration)**

- Obtained from Ministry of MSME
- Free registration online at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/)
- Provides benefits like lower interest rates
- Recommended for manufacturing/service businesses

**GST Certificate**

- Required if annual turnover > ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states)
- Obtained from GST portal
- Mandatory for most businesses

**Which to use?**

- If you have both, provide MSME (simpler)
- If no MSME, GST certificate is acceptable
- If no GST, register for MSME (quick and free)

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Not providing any business registration proof
- ❌ Expired MSME or GST certificate
- ❌ Mismatch between business name in MSME/GST and Razorpay
- ❌ Using someone else's GST certificate

### 6. Trust

#### Description

A legal entity created for charitable, religious, educational, or social welfare purposes. Governed by Indian Trusts Act, 1882.

#### Examples

- Charitable trusts
- Educational trusts
- Religious organizations
- NGOs registered as trusts
- Welfare organizations

#### Required Documents

| Document                           | Description                              | Format      | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------- |
| **Registered Trust Deed**          | Trust deed registered with Sub-Registrar | PDF         | Must show all trustees      |
| **Trust Registration Certificate** | Certificate from Charity Commissioner    | PDF         | State-specific registration |
| **Company PAN Card**               | PAN in trust's name                      | PDF/JPG/PNG | Trust's PAN, not trustee's  |
| **Cancelled Cheque**               | Cancelled cheque with account details    | JPG/PNG/PDF | Account in trust's name     |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof**  | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License | PDF/JPG/PNG | Must be a trustee           |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**       | PAN of the trustee                       | PDF/JPG/PNG | Individual trustee's PAN    |

#### Additional Documents (If Applicable)

- **80G Certificate**: For tax-exempt donations
- **12A Registration**: Income tax exemption
- **FCRA Certificate**: For foreign funding
- **CSR Registration**: For Corporate Social Responsibility

#### Key Points

- Trust Deed must be properly registered and stamped
- Trust must have its own PAN
- Authorized signatory must be an appointed trustee
- May require additional documentation for NGO status

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Unregistered Trust Deed
- ❌ Using personal account of trustee
- ❌ Unauthorized person signing documents
- ❌ Expired 80G or 12A certificates

### 7. Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)

#### Description

A separate legal entity consisting of all persons lineally descended from a common ancestor, including their wives and unmarried daughters. Recognized under Hindu Law.

#### Examples

- Family businesses
- Ancestral property businesses
- Traditional family enterprises

#### Required Documents

| Document                          | Description                              | Format      | Notes                             |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Registered HUF Deed**           | HUF formation deed                       | PDF         | Should list Karta and coparceners |
| **Company PAN Card**              | PAN in HUF's name                        | PDF/JPG/PNG | Format: "Name of Karta (HUF)"     |
| **Cancelled Cheque**              | Cancelled cheque with account details    | JPG/PNG/PDF | Account in HUF's name             |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof** | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License | PDF/JPG/PNG | Usually the Karta (head)          |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**      | PAN of Karta                             | PDF/JPG/PNG | Karta's individual PAN            |

#### Key Points

- HUF must have separate PAN (distinct from Karta's PAN)
- Karta typically acts as authorized signatory
- Bank account must be in HUF's name
- HUF Deed should declare formation of HUF

#### Common Issues

- ❌ Using Karta's personal PAN instead of HUF PAN
- ❌ Bank account in Karta's individual name
- ❌ Missing or improperly drafted HUF Deed
- ❌ No proof of HUF registration

### 12. Individual / Unregistered Business

#### Description

Individuals operating without formal business registration. Suitable for influencers, content creators, or very small-scale operations.

#### Examples

- Social media influencers
- YouTubers
- Bloggers
- Individual consultants (no business registration)
- Freelance artists

#### Required Documents

| Document                          | Description                              | Format      | Notes                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Authorized Signatory ID Proof** | Aadhar/Voter ID/Passport/Driving License | PDF/JPG/PNG | Individual's government ID       |
| **Authorized Signatory PAN**      | PAN card                                 | PDF/JPG/PNG | Individual's PAN                 |
| **Cancelled Cheque**              | Cancelled cheque with account details    | JPG/PNG/PDF | Personal savings/current account |

#### Key Points

- **Simplest option** - only 3 documents required
- No company PAN needed (use individual PAN)
- No business registration required
- Bank account in individual's personal name
- Income taxed as individual income
- **Not suitable for registered businesses**

#### Limitations

- Limited credibility compared to registered businesses
- No legal separation between business and personal assets
- May face transaction limits
- Not suitable for high-volume businesses

#### When to Use

✅ Personal brand/influencer
✅ Very small scale operations
✅ Testing business idea
✅ Content creators

#### When NOT to Use

❌ Already have business registration
❌ Planning to scale significantly
❌ Need legal liability protection
❌ High transaction volumes expected

## Document Specifications

### General Requirements

| Specification         | Requirement                                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **File Formats**      | PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG                                    |
| **Maximum File Size** | 5MB per document                                       |
| **Resolution**        | Clear and readable (minimum 300 DPI recommended)       |
| **Color**             | Color or grayscale (black & white acceptable if clear) |
| **Orientation**       | Correct orientation (not upside down or sideways)      |
| **Completeness**      | Full document visible (no cropped edges)               |
| **Validity**          | Current/unexpired documents                            |

### ID Proof Specifications

Accepted ID proofs (any ONE):

- ✅ Aadhar Card (front and back)
- ✅ Voter ID Card
- ✅ Passport (photo page)
- ✅ Driving License (front and back)

**Requirements:**

- Must be government-issued
- Should be current/unexpired
- Photo should be clearly visible
- All text should be legible
- Signature should be visible (if applicable)

### Bank Account Proof Specifications

Accepted bank proofs (any ONE):

- ✅ Cancelled cheque with pre-printed bank details
- ✅ Bank statement (latest 3 months)
- ✅ Bank letterhead with account details

**Cancelled Cheque Requirements:**

- Cheque must have bank account number printed
- IFSC code should be visible
- Account holder name should be visible
- Write "CANCELLED" across the cheque
- Don't sign the cancelled cheque
- Ensure pre-printed details are clear

**If using Bank Statement:**

- Latest 3 months' statement
- Should show account holder name
- Account number and IFSC visible
- Bank logo/letterhead present

### PAN Card Specifications

**Requirements:**

- Clear photo (for individual PAN)
- PAN number clearly visible
- Name should match exactly with other documents
- Not expired or cancelled
- For company PAN: Company name should match registration documents

### Registration Certificate Specifications

**Requirements:**

- Official stamp/seal visible
- Registration number clearly visible
- Issuing authority mentioned
- Date of registration visible
- All pages included (if multi-page)
- Properly stamped (where applicable)

## Common Mistakes

### Document-Related Mistakes

1. **Poor Image Quality**
   - ❌ Blurry or pixelated images
   - ❌ Dark or overexposed photos
   - ✅ Use scanner or good camera in well-lit conditions

2. **Incomplete Documents**
   - ❌ Cropped edges cutting off important information
   - ❌ Missing pages of multi-page documents
   - ✅ Ensure full document is captured

3. **Wrong Format**
   - ❌ Uploading .doc, .heic, or other unsupported formats
   - ✅ Convert to PDF, JPG, or PNG

4. **Oversized Files**
   - ❌ Files larger than 5MB
   - ✅ Compress images while maintaining readability

5. **Expired Documents**
   - ❌ Using expired ID proofs or registrations
   - ✅ Ensure all documents are current

### Information Mismatch

1. **Name Variations**
   - ❌ "ABC Pvt Ltd" in one document, "ABC Private Limited" in another
   - ✅ Ensure exact name match across all documents

2. **Wrong Business Type Selection**
   - ❌ Selecting "Partnership" when actually "LLP"
   - ❌ Selecting "Sole Proprietorship" when you have Pvt Ltd
   - ✅ Choose business type matching your actual registration

3. **Personal vs Business Account**
   - ❌ Using personal bank account for company
   - ❌ Using personal PAN for company
   - ✅ Use business accounts and PANs for registered entities

4. **Unauthorized Signatory**
   - ❌ Employee signing when they're not a director/partner
   - ❌ Family member signing without proper authorization
   - ✅ Ensure signatory is officially authorized

### Registration Issues

1. **Unregistered Documents**
   - ❌ Partnership Deed that's notarized but not registered
   - ❌ Trust Deed without registration
   - ✅ Get documents officially registered with appropriate authority

2. **Missing Stamps**
   - ❌ Partnership/Trust Deed without stamp duty paid
   - ✅ Ensure proper stamp duty is paid and visible

3. **Incomplete Registration**
   - ❌ Company registered but PAN not applied
   - ❌ GST registered but certificate not downloaded
   - ✅ Complete all registration formalities before KYC

## Selection Guide

### Decision Tree

```
START: What is your business structure?

┌─ Individual working alone (no formal registration)?
│  └─> Select: Individual/Unregistered Business
│
┌─ Sole owner with MSME or GST registration?
│  └─> Select: Sole Proprietorship
│
┌─ Multiple partners in a traditional partnership?
│  ├─ Registered partnership deed?
│  │  └─> Select: Partnership Firm
│  └─ No registration?
│     └─> Register first, then select Partnership Firm
│
┌─ Multiple partners with LLP registration?
│  └─> Select: Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
│
┌─ Company registered under Companies Act?
│  ├─ Shares can be publicly traded?
│  │  └─> Select: Public Limited Company
│  ├─ For-profit private company?
│  │  └─> Select: Private Limited Company
│  └─ Non-profit (Section 8)?
│     └─> Select: Section 8 Company
│
┌─ Family business (Hindu Law)?
│  └─> Select: Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)
│
┌─ Charitable/Religious/Educational organization?
│  ├─ Registered as Trust?
│  │  └─> Select: Trust
│  └─ Registered as Section 8 Company?
│     └─> Select: Section 8 Company
│
└─ Government/Municipal/Judicial body?
   ├─ Government department/PSU?
   │  └─> Select: Government Entity
   ├─ Court/Judicial body?
   │  └─> Select: Judicial Person
   └─ Municipal/Panchayat?
      └─> Select: Local Authority
```

### Quick Selection Tips

**Choose Individual/Unregistered if:**

- You're an influencer, content creator, or freelancer
- No formal business registration
- Very small scale operations
- Want simplest setup

**Choose Sole Proprietorship if:**

- Single owner
- Have MSME or GST registration
- Small to medium business
- Want legal but simple structure

**Choose Partnership if:**

- 2+ partners sharing profits
- Have registered partnership deed
- Traditional business structure
- Partners have joint liability

**Choose LLP if:**

- Professional services (CA, lawyers, consultants)
- Want limited liability protection
- Flexible partnership structure
- Modern business approach

**Choose Private Limited if:**

- Startup or growing business
- Want to raise funding
- Need limited liability
- Planning to scale

**Choose Trust if:**

- NGO or charitable organization
- Governed by Indian Trusts Act
- Already registered as Trust

**Choose Section 8 if:**

- Non-profit company
- Charitable/educational objectives
- Want company structure with non-profit status
