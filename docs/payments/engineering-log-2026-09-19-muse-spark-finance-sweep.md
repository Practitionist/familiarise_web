# The 2026-09-19 Muse Spark finance sweep: what a four-part audit and #1564 verified, and how two PRs closed it

**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Branches:** `fix/finance-money-sweep` (PR-F) and `fix/finance-glue-docs` (PR-G) · **Scope:** `lib/payments/`, `lib/compliance/`, `lib/enterprise/`, `lib/maintenance*.ts`, `netlify/functions/cron-tick.mts`, `app/api/checkout/`, `app/api/admin/`, `app/api/cleanup/`, `app/api/organizations/`, `jobs/billing/`, `jobs/compliance/`, `prisma/sql/`, `prisma/schema.prisma` (comments only), and this documentation tree.

This entry records what the Muse Spark booking audit's four parts, together with issue #1564, claimed against the money subsystem, how many of those claims survived a second look at the code, and how the two pull requests this sweep produced closed the ones that were real. Every number below is a verdict-registry count from the plan file the two builder agents worked from, cross-checked against dev HEAD after PR #1740 and PR #1741 had already landed their own fixes; a claim that was already true on that HEAD is not counted again here as a new fix.

## What the four parts and #1564 claimed

Between them, the four Muse Spark booking-sweep parts and issue #1564 raised roughly thirty P0-severity claims and roughly one hundred and twenty P1-severity claims against the money subsystem — checkout, webhooks, refunds, payouts, the ledger, TDS withholding, GST invoicing, the DEGRADED maintenance gate, and the erasure pipeline. Re-verifying every one of those claims against dev HEAD, after the two prior trains (#1740 and #1741) had already landed their own fixes, produced four buckets. About thirty-five percent were already fixed by those earlier PRs and needed no further work. About twenty percent were either not real bugs on the code as it stands or were an intentional, documented design choice rather than a defect. About fifteen percent turned on a product or engineering decision that had not yet been made, or on a change that needs chartered-accountant sign-off before it can ship. The remaining thirty percent were legitimate, open defects, and those are what the two pull requests in this sweep fixed.

## PR-F: the money-semantics train

`fix/finance-money-sweep` carries eighteen money-semantics items, all of them changes to how a payment, a refund, a payout, or a TDS record is computed or posted. It fixed the `order.paid` webhook payload to carry the payment entity so the org-payment branch can mark an invoice PAID without degrading to a notes-only guess; made `payment.failed` a proper CAS write instead of a bare update; moved the consultant TDS period onto the payout's completion instant instead of the moment the batch job happens to run, mirroring a fix the organisation rail already had; hardened the Razorpay refund call against a zero or `NaN` amount silently promoting to a full refund, and against reaching the gateway for an order with no captured payment; ran the dispute-update and cascade paths under `withSerializableRetry`; capped both the organisation and the consumer credit note cumulatively per invoice rather than per note; made every refund-path payout clawback post its ledger entry in the same transaction as the earnings counter it updates; collapsed a double-mint window on recording purchases to a single mint; converted a batch of ad-hoc throws into the typed refusal rail; moved a handful of `recordSystemError` calls onto the transaction's own client so they survive a rollback; taught the admin whole-event refund route to notify the payer for organisation-funded seats the way the other refund doors already do; made the organisation payouts route refuse a non-Razorpay gateway outright; fixed five smaller earnings-hygiene defects; clamped the refund cascade to the payment's actual refundable balance; widened the reconciler's unjournaled-earnings check with a post-commit grace window; switched the TDS engine's default from the legacy threshold gate to pure Section 194-O; made the invoice-refund wallet-credit failure rethrow inside its transaction instead of being swallowed; capped the Stripe webhook body size; made the admin payment-recovery route actually recover a stuck payment instead of no-opping on its own idempotency guard; gated erasure behind a money-in-flight check and nulled the three consultee free-text fields the earlier scrub had missed; and added the four sidecar SQL guards — two check constraints, a credit-note XOR trigger, and the ledger immutability triggers — that the owner applies with `npm run db:sidecars` at merge time.

## PR-G: the glue and the docs

`fix/finance-glue-docs` carries eleven smaller glue items plus this documentation pass. It added the three admin money-write prefixes (`/api/admin/refunds`, `/api/admin/tds`, `/api/admin/billing-accounts`) to the DEGRADED write-block list; scoped the platform maintenance-window reads to `organizationId: null` so a per-org row can no longer be read or closed as the platform's own; sorted a maintenance twin's `503` into the ticker's `held` bucket instead of `failed`, and paged Sentry on a missing `CRON_SECRET` instead of only logging it; taught the cleanup-route error reporter to preserve a non-`Error` throw's message instead of reporting `[object Object]`; gave the overage-charge sweep twin a `?limit=` parameter and ordered the IRP candidate query oldest-first; replaced four hand-rolled `SUPPLIER_STATE_CODE ?? "KA"` reads with the same GSTIN-first, fail-closed resolver the consumer tax invoice already uses; swapped both capture doors from the cookie-cached session read to the force-fresh one the checkout POST route already used; added three distinct audit actions for payout-account writes and manual status overrides in place of one overloaded `SETTINGS_CHANGED`/`PAYOUT_INITIATED` pair; rendered the earner-facing hold date on PENDING and HELD earnings rows; corrected a schema comment that had the discount cap's condition backwards and marked two ledger-transaction kinds as reserved; and taught the checkout-success page to read a `bookingState` the verify route now derives, so a request still awaiting consultant approval no longer renders "Subscription Activated!" and a `VERIFICATION_FAILED` 500 keeps the page on its polling card instead of routing to the failure page.

## The eight owner decisions

Eight product and engineering questions came up during triage that the code alone could not answer, and the owner settled all eight before either PR was built. First, the train shipped as two unstacked pull requests rather than one, so the money-semantics changes and the glue-and-docs changes could be reviewed and merged independently. Second, the Phase 2 earnings-and-ledger write plus its `sync-payment-earnings` healer stay as they are, now backed by a reconciler grace window, and folding that journal write back into the Phase 1 transaction is tracked as a post-MVP design issue rather than attempted in this sweep. Third, the refund cascade clamps to the payment's refundable balance, which counts a dispute already `LOST` or `CHARGE_REFUNDED` against that balance the same way it counts another returned refund. Fourth, `TDS_ENGINE` defaults to Section 194-O, with the gross-base variant staying gated behind chartered-accountant sign-off before it can be turned on in production. Fifth, the four sidecar SQL guards are applied by the owner at merge time rather than through an automated migration, consistent with how every other sidecar in this codebase ships. Sixth, the admin payment-recovery route was changed to perform a real recovery rather than continuing to no-op behind its own idempotency guard. Seventh, the invoice-refund path's wallet-credit and journal write now rethrow inside their transaction instead of being swallowed, so a failure rolls back and the stuck-event sweeper re-drives it under a stable idempotency key. Eighth, the Muse Spark parent issues were closed as superseded by this sweep, with their residual, still-open items folded into two themed follow-up issues plus a short append to each closed parent recording where its residuals went, rather than left open as four separate trackers.

## Where the residuals went

Two categories of finding did not get a fix in either pull request. The Phase-1/Phase-2 fold — moving the earnings and ledger write back into the same transaction as the appointment confirmation — is a design change large enough to need its own issue rather than a line item in this sweep, and is tracked as a post-MVP tracked follow-up issue rather than under a number assigned here. The two themed residual issues that absorbed the Muse Spark parents' remaining open items are likewise tracked follow-up issues rather than numbered in this log, because the numbers belong to whichever issue the owner files them under, not to this entry.
