## What Is Enterprise on Familiarise?

Familiarise is a marketplace where experts (consultants, teachers, mentors, coaches) offer their knowledge to learners through paid 1-on-1 or group sessions.

**Enterprise** is when a whole organisation — a school, a company, a coaching institute, a professional association — wants to be part of that ecosystem.

There are two basic things an organisation can do:

| The organisation… | We call them |
|---|---|
| **Pays us** so their people can access experts | A **Buyer org** |
| **Earns from us** by bringing their own experts onto our marketplace | A **Provider org** |
| **Does both** — has internal experts AND pays for their people's learning | A **Hybrid org** |

Within Buyer and Hybrid orgs, we offer **four payment arrangements** depending on how the client wants to pay. The combination of org type + payment arrangement gives us **nine product offerings** in total.

## Offering 1 — Buyer · Pay Individually (TAG_ONLY)

### The deal in plain words
"We want our 500 employees to be able to book sessions on your platform. We'll pay for them individually through their corporate cards, but we want company-level reports on what's happening."

### Who buys this
- Large corporates with L&D (Learning & Development) budgets allocated per employee
- Companies where employees expense their own coaching
- Any org that wants visibility and a branded presence without committing budget upfront

### What they get
- A company page on Familiarise
- All employees are tagged to the company in their profiles
- HR/manager dashboard shows who booked what, with whom, how often
- No financial subsidy — each person pays normally

### How we make money
Our standard commission (10%) on every session their employees book. Multiplied by 500 employees × however many sessions per year.

### Sales talking point
"There's no upfront cost. You just register your company, invite your team, and instantly get a dashboard showing all your organisation's learning activity."

### Steps to set up
1. Sign contract / onboard org
2. Create the organisation as a Buyer org with "Tag Only" billing
3. Bulk-invite employees via email list
4. HR gets dashboard access

## Offering 2 — Buyer · Pre-loaded Credits (SEAT_PACK)

### The deal in plain words
"We want to give our 200 employees ₹10,000 each worth of consulting. We'll pay ₹20 lakhs upfront to you, and our employees book freely without seeing any price."

### Who buys this
- Companies with a fixed annual L&D budget to spend
- HR teams running structured training programmes
- Any org that wants employees to book without friction (no card, no payment)

### What they get
- A pool of "credits" loaded onto their account
- Employees book sessions at ₹0 — the credits are deducted invisibly
- When the pool runs out, employees either stop booking or the company tops it up
- Full visibility on how credits are being consumed

### How we make money
We take our commission from each session as usual. The org pre-paid, so the money is already with us. This is excellent cash flow — payment received upfront, revenue recognised over time as sessions happen.

### Sales talking point
"Think of it like a corporate prepaid card for learning. Your HR team loads ₹X, distributes it to employees, and they book without any payment friction. When the balance runs low, you top up."

### Cost estimate example
```
Company: 100 employees
Target: 2 sessions per employee per month
Average session price: ₹500
Monthly burn: 100 × 2 × ₹500 = ₹1,00,000
Suggested starting pool: ₹3,00,000 (3 months)
```

### Steps to set up
1. Sign contract / onboard org
2. Create org as Buyer + "Seat Pack" billing
3. Process the credit purchase (Razorpay link or bank transfer)
4. Credits loaded instantly on payment confirmation
5. Invite employees — they book, pool deducts automatically

## Offering 3 — Buyer · Monthly Invoice (INVOICED_MONTHLY)

### The deal in plain words
"We're a listed company. Our procurement process requires us to pay invoices at 30 days. We'll book sessions all month and pay the consolidated invoice at the end."

### Who buys this
- Large corporates and MNCs with structured procurement
- Government departments and public institutions
- Any client that says "we don't do upfront payments — we need an invoice"

### What they get
- Employees book freely all month (capped at a pre-agreed credit limit)
- At the end of the month, we generate one clean GST invoice (with itemised line items, HSN codes, GSTIN)
- They pay the invoice within agreed terms (typically 30–60 days)
- If they don't pay, overdue notices are sent automatically

### How we make money
Same commission per session. The difference is we carry 30–60 days of credit risk. This is appropriate for verified, high-value enterprise clients only. Require credit check + signed agreement.

### Sales talking point
"We work with your procurement process. One invoice per month with full GST compliance, itemised by department if needed. Just like your other SaaS vendors."

### Risk control levers
- Set a monthly credit limit (e.g., ₹10L) — bookings stop automatically if the outstanding balance hits the cap
- Automate overdue escalation (invoice flips to "Overdue" after due date)

### Steps to set up
1. Credit check + MSA (Master Services Agreement) signed
2. Create org as Buyer + "Invoiced Monthly" billing
3. Set credit limit (e.g., ₹5L/month)
4. Invite employees
5. End of month: one-click invoice generation → send → track payment

## Offering 4 — Buyer · Flat Upfront Fee (PREPAID_UNLIMITED)

### The deal in plain words
"We are a school with 1,000 students. We want them all to have unlimited access to your consultants for the next 4 months. We'll pay ₹10 lakhs and we don't want any per-session billing."

### Who buys this
- Schools and colleges
- Government education programmes
- Corporates running intensive 3–6 month upskilling programmes
- Anyone who wants to eliminate per-session admin and just buy "unlimited access for a period"

### What they get
- Every learner in their org books at ₹0 during the contract period (May–September in this example)
- No credits to manage, no invoices to approve mid-contract
- After the contract ends, normal pricing resumes

### How we make money
We collect the full ₹10L upfront. From that, we pay consultants their per-session fee every time a session happens. Our margin is the difference.

```
₹10L collected
10,000 sessions happen over 4 months
Average consultant payout per session: ₹85 (assuming ₹100 listed price, 85% to consultant)
Total paid to consultants: 10,000 × ₹85 = ₹8.5L
Platform margin: ₹10L − ₹8.5L = ₹1.5L (~15%)
```

### Sales talking point
"One payment, zero per-session admin for 4 months. Your students just log in and book — no wallets, no payments, no parent permission forms for each session."

### Steps to set up
1. Negotiate the flat fee (based on expected session volume and duration)
2. Sign contract with start and end dates
3. Create org as Buyer + "Prepaid Unlimited" billing
4. Raise a manual GST invoice for the flat fee → collect payment
5. Set contract start and end dates in the system
6. Bulk-invite students → they book freely until contract expires

## Offering 5 — Provider · Earns from Sessions

### The deal in plain words
"We are EliteCoach Academy. We have 50 expert coaches. We want to list them on your marketplace and earn money every time a learner books them."

### Who buys this
- Coaching institutes and academies
- Corporate consulting firms that want to monetise their experts
- Online education companies looking for another distribution channel
- Any organisation that has experts and wants the platform to bring them learners

### What they get
- All their coaches are listed on Familiarise under the org's brand
- Learners across the platform can discover and book them
- A share of every session fee goes to the org (typically 5%), the rest to the consultant (85%) and platform (10%)
- A payout dashboard showing monthly earnings

### How we make money
Platform takes 10% of every session. This is pure revenue with no subsidy from our side.

### Revenue split example
```
Learner books a ₹2,000 session with EliteCoach mentor
→ Platform keeps:  ₹200 (10%)
→ EliteCoach org:  ₹100 (5%)
→ Mentor earns:  ₹1,700 (85%)
```

### Sales talking point
"Bring your coaches. We bring the learners. You set the price. We handle the infrastructure — video calls, scheduling, payments, reviews. You earn on every session, and so do your coaches."

### Steps to set up
1. Sign partnership / revenue-share agreement
2. We create and verify the Provider org (admin process)
3. Invite their coaches onto the platform as "Org Consultants"
4. Coaches list their services — they're live to all learners
5. Monthly payouts to the org and to individual coaches

## Offering 9 — Hybrid · Flat Fee + Earns (PREPAID_UNLIMITED)

### The deal in plain words
"We are Delhi Public School. Our teachers will be the mentors. Our students will be the mentees. We're paying ₹10 lakhs for 4 months of unlimited sessions. Handle everything."

### Who buys this
- Schools with strong faculty who are also potential mentors
- Colleges running peer-mentorship or faculty-mentorship programmes
- Corporate L&D departments with internal coaches AND external coaching needs

### What they get
- Teachers/employees listed as earners on the platform
- Students/learners book at ₹0 for 4 months
- One clean invoice for the ₹10L
- No per-session admin for the entire contract period
- After the contract, the org can renew or switch to a different plan

### How we make money
```
₹10L collected upfront
Teachers earn their share per session (e.g., ₹85 per ₹100 session)
We pay teachers from the pool
Our margin = ₹10L − total teacher payouts over 4 months
```

If sessions don't happen (teachers aren't busy), we keep more. If sessions are dense, our margin compresses — but we've already been paid.

### The upsell path
```
Year 1: School signs ₹10L flat deal → tests the platform
Year 2: School wants more features (WhatsApp alerts, PDF reports, parent access)
       → Renews at ₹15L + optional add-ons
Year 3: School recommends us to 3 sister schools → network effect
```

### Sales talking point
"One payment. Four months. Teachers earn. Students learn. You never see another invoice until renewal."

### Steps to set up
1. Negotiate flat fee (based on student count × expected sessions × contract duration)
2. Sign contract with exact start/end dates
3. Create org as Hybrid + Prepaid Unlimited billing
4. Raise GST invoice → collect payment → mark paid
5. Set contract dates in system
6. Invite teachers as "Org Consultants" → they list their mentorship services
7. Invite students as "Org Learners" → they book freely until contract end

## Sales Objections & Responses

**"We already use Zoom / Google Meet for this."**
> "We don't replace your video tool — we add the booking, payment, scheduling, and reporting layer on top. And we include HD video calls natively so you don't need Zoom for learning sessions."

**"Why would our teachers be on a public marketplace?"**
> "They don't have to be. With a Hybrid or Provider org, your teachers only appear to your own students. External visibility is optional."

**"What if we book fewer sessions than expected?"**
> "For flat-fee deals (Prepaid Unlimited), unused sessions are your buffer — you paid for a budget, not a count. For credit-based deals (Seat Pack), unused credits roll over or we offer a top-up discount."

**"What about data privacy? We can't have student data on a third-party platform."**
> "We can sign a DPA (Data Processing Agreement). Student names, session content, and session recordings stay within your org's view only."

**"Can you invoice in our company name with our GSTIN?"**
> "Yes — all invoices are fully GST-compliant with your GSTIN, correct SAC codes, and CGST/SGST breakout. We're e-invoice ready."

## How to Close the ₹10L School Deal (Step by Step)

This is the most common high-value deal pattern. Here is exactly what you do:

1. **Discovery call** — ask: How many students? How many teachers/mentors? Over what period? What's the expected session frequency?

2. **Build the estimate**
   ```
   1,000 students × 10 sessions each = 10,000 sessions
   Average session: ₹100 listed price
   Total listed value: ₹10L
   Your ask: ₹10L flat fee (matches listed value → easy to justify)
   ```

3. **Present the Prepaid Unlimited proposal** — one payment, zero per-session admin, teachers earn, students access freely.

4. **Send GST proforma invoice** for ₹10L (you issue this from the platform manually).

5. **Collect payment** — NEFT/RTGS, cheque, or UPI for large amounts.

6. **Onboard in the system** — 20-minute setup (our team handles it).

7. **Go live** — teachers list services, students get invite links, sessions start.

8. **Monthly report** — send the school a PDF showing sessions completed, teacher utilisation, student engagement. This is the renewal conversation starter.

## Questions to Qualify a Lead Quickly

1. "Do you have your own experts, or do you want to access ours?" → tells you BUYER vs PROVIDER vs HYBRID
2. "Do you have a budget already approved, or does it need to go through procurement?" → tells you PREPAID vs INVOICED
3. "How many learners? How many sessions per month?" → tells you deal size
4. "Do you need individual student receipts or one company invoice?" → confirms INVOICED_MONTHLY vs SEAT_PACK
5. "What's your timeline — when do you want sessions to start?" → creates urgency
