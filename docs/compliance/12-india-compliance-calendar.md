# 12 — India compliance calendar

> **Status:** authoritative deadline reference. Update whenever a regulation changes.
> **Audience:** finance + tax-ops + compliance dashboard.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-02

This is the single source of truth for every recurring filing / disclosure / deadline that touches the platform. Any cron-driven artefact also points back here for its cadence.

## Recurring monthly

| Day of month | Filing | Notes | Source doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7th** | TDS deposit (challan) | Deposit prior-month TDS to govt. **March-month deductions** due 30 Apr instead. | [01](./01-tds-overview.md) |
| **10th** | GSTR-8 (TCS by ECO) | Monthly TCS return; covers prior calendar month's net taxable supplies of registered consultants. **B2C-only.** | [02](./02-gst-overview.md) |
| **11th** | GSTR-1 (outward supplies) | Tax invoices + credit notes; aligns with the credit notes from refund cascade. | [02](./02-gst-overview.md), [05](./05-refund-and-chargeback-tax-adjustments.md) |
| **20th** | GSTR-3B (summary) | Output liability + input credit summary. | [02](./02-gst-overview.md) |

## Recurring quarterly

| Quarter | Period | Form 26Q (resident) due | Form 27Q (non-resident) due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Apr–Jun | 31 Jul | 31 Jul |
| Q2 | Jul–Sep | 31 Oct | 31 Oct |
| Q3 | Oct–Dec | 31 Jan | 31 Jan |
| Q4 | Jan–Mar | **31 May** | **31 May** |

**Form 16A** to consultants: within **15 days of the return due date** (i.e. ~15 Aug, 15 Nov, 15 Feb, 15 Jun).

See [doc 04](./04-tds-quarterly-filings.md).

## Recurring annual

| Date | Filing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 31 Oct | TDS reconciliation statement (Form 26AS) | Auto-generated by IT dept from quarterly returns; verify alignment. |
| 31 Dec | Annual GST return (GSTR-9) | If aggregate turnover > ₹2 cr; consolidates the year's GSTR-1/3B/8. |
| 31 Mar | FY end | Cut-off for cross-FY refund tax adjustments — see [doc 05](./05-refund-and-chargeback-tax-adjustments.md). |

## Per-event deadlines

| Trigger | Deadline | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Personal-data breach detected | "Without delay" — operational target **72 hours** | Email DPB + affected users. Cron at `databreach-deadline-alerts` fires at T+60h and T+72h. |
| Refund initiated (cards) | **7 working days** | RBI norm; tracked via `Refund.targetCompletionDate`. |
| Refund initiated (UPI) | **5–7 days** | Same. |
| Chargeback evidence window | **7 days** from gateway notice | Razorpay / Stripe specific. |
| Grievance acknowledgement (E-Commerce Rules 4(5)) | **48 hours** | See [doc 09](./09-consumer-protection-and-grievance.md). |
| Grievance resolution | **30 days** | See [doc 09](./09-consumer-protection-and-grievance.md). |
| MSME 43B(h) — without written agreement | **15 days** from invoice date | See [doc 03](./03-msme-43b-h.md). |
| MSME 43B(h) — with written agreement | **45 days** from invoice date | See [doc 03](./03-msme-43b-h.md). |
| IRN generation | **30 days** from invoice issue (CBIC retroactive cut-off) | Enforced by `irp-uploader.ts` `issuedAt: { gte: subDays(now, 30) }`. |
| IRN cancellation | **24 hours** from generation | Past 24h, must issue credit note + new invoice. |
| Form 15CA / 15CB | **Before remittance** | Cross-border consultant payouts only. See [doc 07](./07-cross-border-flows.md). |
| Form 16A to consultant | **15 days** after quarterly return due date | See [doc 04](./04-tds-quarterly-filings.md). |
| Razorpay marketplace self-declaration | **Annually** | See [doc 10](./10-rbi-pa-and-payment-architecture.md). |

## Penalty / breach risk reference

| Breach | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Late TDS filing (Form 26Q / 27Q) | ₹200/day under Sec 234E, capped at TDS amount |
| Non-filing > 1 year | ₹10,000–₹1,00,000 under Sec 271H |
| Wrong PAN quoted | Separate Sec 271H penalty per row |
| GST TCS not collected (Sec 52) | Equal to TCS not collected (Sec 122(1)(viii)) + 18% p.a. interest (Sec 50(3)) |
| GSTR-8 non-filing | Late fee + interest |
| MSME 43B(h) breach | Expense disallowance in current FY tax computation until paid |
| DPDP breach not reported within 72h | Penalty up to ₹250 cr (Schedule, DPDP Act) |
| Consumer Protection grievance SLA breach | Damages + civil court risk via CCPA escalation |
| Cross-border 15CA/CB skipped | Sec 271-I penalty up to ₹1 lakh per non-filing |
