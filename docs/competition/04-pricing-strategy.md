## Our Pricing Strategy — Phased Approach

### Phase 0: Founding Cohort (Pre-Launch)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Commission | **0%** |
| Duration | 3 months from launch |
| Target | First 30-50 consultants (hand-picked) |
| Qualification | Invited only. Must have existing audience (1K+ social followers or equivalent) |

**Rationale:**
The first 30-50 creators determine the platform's DNA. We need high-quality, active consultants who will generate sessions, reviews, and content. Charging them 0% removes every objection. They join because there's literally no financial risk.

**Rules:**
- 0% commission for 3 calendar months from their first session
- After 3 months, they transition to Phase 1 pricing (10%)
- If they've generated 20+ sessions in those 3 months, they get a loyalty rate of 8% for the next 3 months
- The 0% period is non-renewable. No exceptions.

**Exit criteria:** 30 active creators (defined as 3+ sessions/month) OR 3 months elapsed, whichever comes first.

---

### Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard Commission | **10%** |
| Topmate Migration Rate | **5%** (for 3 months) |
| Gateway Fee (UPI) | 0% |
| Gateway Fee (Cards/Netbanking) | ~2% (passed to buyer or absorbed — TBD) |
| Gateway Fee (International/Stripe) | ~3% (passed to buyer) |

**Standard Commission: 10%**

This matches Topmate's headline rate but our effective rate is lower because of UPI. Marketing message: "Same commission, 6-8% cheaper for your clients."

**Topmate Migration Offer: 5% for 3 months**

Any creator who can demonstrate an active Topmate account (screenshot of dashboard with bookings) gets 5% commission for their first 3 months on Familiarise.

Requirements:
- Screenshot of Topmate dashboard showing at least 5 completed sessions
- Active Topmate profile link
- Agreement to deactivate Topmate profile after migrating (honor system — we can't enforce this, but we ask)

After 3 months, they transition to standard 10%.

**Revenue Impact:**
At 5% commission, we need 2x the volume to generate the same revenue. This is a customer acquisition cost, not a long-term rate. Budget for ₹0 net revenue from migration cohort for 3 months.

---

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-9)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard Commission | **10%** |
| High-Volume Rate | **8%** (for creators earning >₹50K/month) |
| Qualification | Rolling 3-month average GMV >₹50K/month |

**High-Volume Incentive:**

Creators who consistently generate >₹50K/month in bookings get a reduced 8% rate. This rewards loyalty, incentivizes growth, and prevents high-value creators from shopping for lower rates.

**Mechanics:**
- Calculated on rolling 3-month average
- Reviewed monthly
- If they drop below ₹50K/month average, they revert to 10% next month
- Communicated proactively: "Congratulations! You've earned our preferred creator rate of 8%."

**Why ₹50K/month threshold:**
- ₹50K/month × 10% = ₹5K commission to us
- ₹50K/month × 8% = ₹4K commission to us
- We sacrifice ₹1K/month to retain a high-value creator
- A high-value creator leaving costs us ₹5K/month (100% loss)
- The retention ROI is obvious

### Phase 3: Maturity (Months 10+)

| Tier | Price Range | Commission | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | ₹299 - ₹999/hr | 20% | High volume, low margin. Platform provides significant value relative to session price. |
| **Everyday** | ₹1,000 - ₹3,000/hr | 18% | Bread-and-butter segment. Balanced commission. |
| **Premium** | ₹3,000 - ₹10,000/hr | 15% | High-value sessions. Lower commission retains these creators. |
| **Luxury** | ₹10,000+/hr | 12% | Top-tier experts. Flight risk to DIY solutions. Lowest commission to keep them on platform. |

**Rationale for Inverse Tiering:**

The higher the session price, the more a creator has to lose by leaving — but also the more sophisticated they are and the more alternatives they have. A consultant charging ₹15,000/hour can afford to set up their own Calendly + Stripe + Zoom stack. A consultant charging ₹499/hour cannot.

Inverse tiering keeps luxury creators on the platform (where they generate massive GMV and attract premium seekers) while extracting more value from the high-volume budget segment (where the platform provides more relative value).

**Optional Premium Tool Subscriptions:**

In addition to commission, offer optional premium tools:

| Tool | Price | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced Analytics | ₹499/month | Cohort analysis, conversion funnels, revenue forecasting |
| Priority Support | ₹299/month | 4-hour response time, dedicated account manager |
| Featured Listing | ₹999/month | Boosted placement in marketplace search results |
| Custom Branding | ₹1,499/month | Remove Familiarise branding from booking page |

These are additive revenue streams that don't increase commission. Creators who want them pay for them. Creators who don't, don't. No pressure.

## The UPI Advantage — Detailed Analysis

This is our single biggest structural advantage over Topmate for the Indian market. It deserves a detailed breakdown.

### How Topmate's Pricing Actually Works in India

A creator on Topmate charges ₹1,000 for a session. Here's what actually happens:

| Step | Amount | Who Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Session price | ₹1,000 | Seeker |
| Stripe processing fee (2.9% + 30¢) | ~₹30 | Deducted from creator |
| Topmate commission (10%) | ₹100 | Deducted from creator |
| **Subtotal after fees** | **₹870** | Creator receives (in USD) |
| Forex conversion (USD → INR) | -3% to 5% loss | Creator absorbs on payout |
| **Actual creator payout** | **₹825 - ₹845** | Into Indian bank account |

**Effective take rate: 15.5% - 17.5%**

The creator thinks they're paying 10%. They're actually paying 15-18%. The forex loss is invisible until they check their bank statement.

### How Familiarise Pricing Works in India

Same ₹1,000 session. Here's what happens with Razorpay UPI:

| Step | Amount | Who Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Session price | ₹1,000 | Seeker |
| UPI gateway fee | ₹0 (0%) | N/A |
| Familiarise commission (10%) | ₹100 | Deducted from creator |
| **Creator payout** | **₹900** | Into Indian bank account |

**Effective take rate: 10.0%**

No forex. No Stripe fee on UPI. No hidden costs. ₹900 is ₹900.

### The Savings Per Transaction

| Monthly GMV | Creator Payout (Topmate) | Creator Payout (Familiarise) | Creator Saves |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹10,000 | ₹8,250 - ₹8,450 | ₹9,000 | ₹550 - ₹750/month |
| ₹25,000 | ₹20,625 - ₹21,125 | ₹22,500 | ₹1,375 - ₹1,875/month |
| ₹50,000 | ₹41,250 - ₹42,250 | ₹45,000 | ₹2,750 - ₹3,750/month |
| ₹1,00,000 | ₹82,500 - ₹84,500 | ₹90,000 | ₹5,500 - ₹7,500/month |
| ₹5,00,000 | ₹4,12,500 - ₹4,22,500 | ₹4,50,000 | ₹27,500 - ₹37,500/month |

**At ₹1L/month GMV, a creator saves ₹5,500-7,500/month by using Familiarise instead of Topmate.** That's ₹66,000-90,000/year. Real money.

### Marketing the UPI Advantage

This needs to be front and center in all acquisition messaging:

- Landing page hero: "Keep 90% of your earnings. Not 82%."
- Creator onboarding: "Unlike platforms that route your money through US payment processors, we use Razorpay UPI. Zero forex fees. Zero surprises."
- Comparison page: Side-by-side payout calculator. Input your monthly earnings, see the difference.
- Social media: "Indian creators lose 6-8% per transaction on platforms that use Stripe. Here's why." (Educational content, not attack ads.)

### Note on Non-UPI Transactions

Not all transactions will be UPI. Card payments through Razorpay incur ~2% gateway fees. International payments through Stripe incur ~3% + forex. The 0% advantage applies specifically to UPI transactions, which we expect to be 60-80% of Indian transactions.

Blended effective rate for Indian creators (estimate): **10% commission + ~0.6% blended gateway = ~10.6% effective.** Still significantly below Topmate's 16-18%.

## Minimum Pricing Floors

| Service Type | Minimum Price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Consultation (15 min) | ₹299 | ₹299 × 10% = ₹30 commission. Below this, platform costs exceed commission revenue per transaction. |
| Consultation (30 min) | ₹499 | Prevents "₹99 consultation" race-to-bottom that devalues the entire marketplace. |
| Consultation (1 hour) | ₹999 | Signals that this is a professional platform, not a discount bazaar. |
| Webinar (per attendee) | ₹199 | Low enough for accessible events, high enough to cover Stream.io per-session costs. |
| Subscription (monthly) | ₹999 | Recurring revenue must justify platform infrastructure costs. Sub-₹999 subscriptions churn fast and generate negligible commission. |
| Class (per session) | ₹399 | Multi-session classes require significant platform resources (scheduling, recordings, materials). Floor ensures viability. |

### Why Floors Matter

**1. Prevents Race to Bottom**
Without floors, creators compete on price. ₹99 consultations become ₹49, then ₹19, then free. The marketplace becomes a bazaar where nobody earns real money. See: Fiverr's early years, where "five dollars" became synonymous with low quality.

**2. Signals Quality**
A platform where the cheapest consultation is ₹299 communicates: "The experts here are worth paying for." A platform with ₹49 consultations communicates: "These people couldn't charge more."

**3. Commission Economics**
At 10% commission on a ₹99 session, we earn ₹9.90. After gateway processing costs, payment reconciliation overhead, and support costs, we lose money on every transaction. Floors ensure every transaction is at least break-even for the platform.

**4. Creator Confidence**
Floors give new creators permission to charge fairly. Many first-time consultants underprice out of imposter syndrome. A ₹999 floor for 1-hour consultations tells them: "This is the minimum standard. You're worth at least this."

### Floor Enforcement

- Hard floor: creators cannot set prices below the minimum in the UI
- No exceptions for promotional pricing below the floor
- Free sessions are allowed only as trial sessions (limited to 1 per seeker-creator pair, 15 minutes max)

## Revenue Model Math

### Fixed Monthly Costs

| Cost | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase (DB + Auth) | ~₹2,500 | Free tier initially, grows with usage |
| Stream.io (Video + Chat) | ₹0 → ₹36,000 | Free tier until ~₹8.5L revenue or 5+ team |
| Vercel (Hosting) | ~₹1,500 | Pro plan |
| Upstash (Redis) | ~₹500 | Rate limiting + caching |
| Novu (Notifications) | ~₹500 | Free tier initially |
| Resend (Email) | ~₹325 | Transactional email |
| Domain + DNS | ~₹200 | Annual amortized |
| Shubham (Dev) | ₹10,000 | Base salary |
| Miscellaneous | ~₹3,300 | Buffer for unexpected costs |
| **Total** | **~₹19,325/month** | Pre-Stream.io cliff |

### Break-Even Calculation

At 10% commission:

```
Break-even GMV = Fixed Costs / Commission Rate
Break-even GMV = ₹19,325 / 0.10
Break-even GMV = ₹1,93,250/month
```

But not all transactions are UPI (0% gateway). Assuming blended gateway cost of ~0.6%:

```
Net commission per ₹1,000 transaction = ₹100 - ₹6 = ₹94
Break-even transactions = ₹19,325 / ₹94 ≈ 206 transactions/month
At ₹1,000 average transaction: ₹2,06,000 GMV/month
```

**Conservative break-even: ~₹2L GMV/month, or roughly 206 transactions at ₹1,000 average.**

**Note:** Month 12+ projections assume Stream.io remains on free tier. If we hit the free tier cliff (₹8.5L revenue), add ₹36,000/month to fixed costs. New break-even at that point: ~₹5.5L GMV/month.

### The Stream.io Cliff

This is the single biggest cost risk in our model:

| Trigger | Stream.io Cost |
|---|---|
| <₹8.5L platform revenue | ₹0/month (free tier) |
| >₹8.5L platform revenue OR >5 team members OR $100K funding | ~₹36,000/month |

At ₹36,000/month additional cost, our new break-even becomes:

```
New fixed costs = ₹19,325 + ₹36,000 = ₹55,325/month
New break-even GMV = ₹55,325 / ₹0.094 ≈ ₹5,88,564/month
```

Plan: ensure we're well past ₹5.9L GMV/month before triggering Stream.io paid tier. If we hit ₹8.5L revenue, we should be doing ₹8.5L/0.10 = ₹85L GMV/month, which generates ₹8.5L commission — far above the new ₹55K break-even. The cliff is manageable if we grow into it organically.

## Why NOT to Compete on Commission Alone

This section exists because the temptation will be strong. When SuperProfile charges ₹99/month flat and Topmate drops to 8%, the instinct is to match or undercut. Resist it.

### The Commission Race to Zero

| If we charge... | Revenue at ₹10L GMV | Can we sustain? |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | ₹1,00,000 | Yes |
| 8% | ₹80,000 | Tight |
| 5% | ₹50,000 | Barely |
| 3% | ₹30,000 | No — below break-even at scale |
| 0% (SuperProfile model) | ₹0 from commission | Need subscription revenue or VC funding |

SuperProfile's ₹99/month model generates ~₹37.6L/month at 38K creators. But most of those creators are likely inactive. If 10% are active (3,800), that's ₹3.76L/month. A team of 5-10 people costs more than that. The model doesn't work without either VC subsidy or a dramatic increase in paying subscribers.

**We don't want to be in a position where we need VC money to survive.** A 10% commission at reasonable scale is profitable. A 0% commission at any scale is not.

### Compete on Value Instead

The argument we need to win is not "we're cheapest" but "we make you more money":

| Value Driver | How It Increases Creator Revenue |
|---|---|
| Integrated video | Higher session completion rate (no Zoom link failures). Fewer no-shows. |
| Marketplace discovery | Organic bookings from seekers who find the creator through platform search. New revenue the creator wouldn't get on Topmate. |
| Multi-service | A creator who adds webinars to their consultation practice increases total revenue by 40-60% (estimated). |
| Reviews & ratings | Social proof increases booking conversion rate. More bookings from the same traffic. |
| Analytics | Data-driven pricing decisions. Creators who see their demand curves can optimize pricing. |
| Scheduling automation | Less time managing calendar = more available slots = more bookings. |
| Reliable payouts | Creator confidence. No anxiety about whether they'll get paid. They invest more time on the platform. |

**If Familiarise helps a creator earn ₹1,00,000/month instead of ₹80,000/month on Topmate, they'll happily pay 10% (₹10,000) instead of Topmate's effective 17% (₹13,600).** They earn more AND pay less. That's the pitch.

## Anti-Topmate Pricing Attack — Playbook

### Scenario: Topmate Drops Commission to 5%

**Probability:** Medium. If we start gaining traction with Indian creators, Topmate could cut their commission to 5% for the Indian market.

**Our Response:**

1. **Don't panic-match.** 5% commission on Topmate is still ~8-10% effective (5% + Stripe fees + forex). Our 10% with UPI is still cheaper or comparable.

2. **Emphasize total cost, not headline rate:**
   ```
   "Topmate says 5%. Your bank statement says 8-10%.
    Familiarise says 10%. Your bank statement says 10%.
    Who's actually cheaper?"
   ```

3. **Match for high-value creators only.** If a creator earning >₹1L/month on Topmate wants to switch, offer 5% for 6 months. The lifetime value of a ₹1L/month creator justifies the acquisition cost.

4. **Double down on features Topmate can't match quickly:**
   - Integrated video (they'd need to build or license this)
   - Multi-service (fundamental architecture change for them)
   - Collaboration features (revenue splits, co-hosting)
   - Session recordings (requires video infrastructure they don't have)

### Scenario: Topmate Adds UPI/Razorpay

**Probability:** Low-Medium. This would require Topmate to add an Indian payment gateway alongside Stripe. Technically possible but operationally complex (two payment systems, reconciliation, compliance).

**Our Response:**

1. If Topmate adds UPI, our cost advantage narrows significantly. Their effective rate becomes ~12% (10% + 2% card fees, 0% UPI). Ours is 10%.
2. At that point, compete purely on product: integrated video, multi-service, marketplace, analytics.
3. The 2% difference is not enough to drive migration alone. Product quality becomes the deciding factor.

### Scenario: SuperProfile Raises Prices

**Probability:** High. ₹99/month flat is not sustainable. They will raise prices within 12-18 months — likely to ₹499-999/month or a hybrid model (low subscription + small commission).

**Our Response:**

1. Be ready with migration messaging: "SuperProfile raised prices? Come to Familiarise. 10% commission, no subscription fee. You only pay when you earn."
2. Position commission as fairer than subscription: "A subscription charges you whether you earn or not. Commission means we only make money when you make money. Our incentives are aligned."

## Pricing Communication — How to Talk About Price

### Do Say

- "You keep 90% of every rupee."
- "We only make money when you make money."
- "No forex fees. No hidden charges. What you see is what you get."
- "Same commission as Topmate, but 6-8% cheaper for Indian transactions."
- "Your first 3 months at 5% if you're switching from Topmate."

### Don't Say

- "We're the cheapest." (We're not, and this attracts price-sensitive creators who churn.)
- "Commission-free." (We're not SuperProfile and shouldn't pretend to be.)
- "Topmate is a scam." (Attack the economics, not the brand. Let creators draw their own conclusions.)
- Any specific numbers about competitor revenue or internal operations that we can't verify publicly.

### The One-Liner

For social media, pitches, and elevator conversations:

> "Familiarise: 10% commission. UPI payments. Integrated video. You keep more, you earn more."

## Summary — Pricing Principles

1. **Commission is 10%. Period.** Don't apologize for it. Don't race to lower it. 10% is fair, sustainable, and lower than most competitors' effective rates.

2. **UPI is our structural advantage.** Every piece of pricing communication should reference the forex savings. This is not a feature — it's a fundamental cost advantage that Topmate cannot easily replicate.

3. **Compete on value, not price.** The question is not "who charges less?" but "who helps you earn more?" If we deliver better conversion, better tools, and better experience, 10% is a bargain.

4. **Phase pricing to match growth.** 0% founding → 10% launch → tiered maturity. Each phase serves a strategic purpose. Don't skip phases.

5. **Floors protect the ecosystem.** Minimum prices prevent the race to bottom, signal quality, and ensure commission economics work. Enforce them without exception.

6. **Prepare for competitive responses.** Topmate will react. SuperProfile will evolve. Have the playbook ready before it happens, not after.
