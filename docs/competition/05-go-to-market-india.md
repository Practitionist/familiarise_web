# Go-To-Market Playbook — India Launch

## The Cold Start Problem

We have zero users. Every marketplace dies or thrives based on solving this. Supply (experts) must come first — seekers won't come to an empty marketplace.

The math is simple: if a consultee opens Familiarise and sees 3 consultants with empty calendars, they leave and never come back. If they see 50 active consultants with reviews, filled slots, and diverse expertise, they book. The entire GTM strategy flows from this asymmetry.

---

## Phase 0: Founding Members (Weeks 1-4)

### Objective

Personally recruit 30-50 tech consultants who will form the supply backbone of the platform at launch.

### Target Profile

| Attribute | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Role | Senior engineers, tech leads, architects, engineering managers |
| LinkedIn presence | 5K+ connections or 1K+ followers |
| Domain | System design, DSA, backend, DevOps, cloud, mobile, ML |
| Commitment | Minimum 2 sessions/month for 3 months |
| Verifiability | Public GitHub, blog, conference talks, or verifiable employer |

### Founding Member Offer

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Commission | 0% for first 3 months (100% of earnings kept) |
| Badge | "Founding Member" badge on profile — permanent |
| Priority support | Direct WhatsApp/Slack channel with founding team |
| Product input | Monthly feedback call, feature request priority |
| Early SEO advantage | Profile pages indexed before competition floods the platform |

### Outreach Strategy

**Where to find them:**
- LinkedIn search: "Senior Software Engineer" + "India" + 5K+ connections
- Twitter/X tech community: Active posters in #buildinpublic, #systemdesign, #techtwitter
- Dev communities: Hashnode, Dev.to, Indian tech Discord servers
- Conference speaker lists: JSConf India, RustConf, GopherCon India, PyCon India
- Open source contributors: Indian maintainers of popular GitHub repos

**Outreach DM Template (LinkedIn):**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been following your work on [specific thing — blog post, open source project, talk]. Really sharp insights on [topic].
>
> I'm building Familiarise — a consultation platform specifically for tech experts in India. Think Topmate, but with built-in HD video, session recordings, document review, and 4 service types (1:1s, subscriptions, webinars, classes).
>
> We're recruiting 30 founding members before launch. The deal: 0% commission for 3 months, founding member badge, and direct input on the product roadmap.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-min call this week? Happy to demo the platform.
>
> — [Your name]

**Key principles for outreach:**
- Personalize every single message. Reference their specific work.
- Never batch-send. Quality over quantity.
- Follow up once after 5 days. No more.
- If they say no, ask if they know someone who'd be interested.

## Phase 1: Niche Domination — Tech/Engineering (Months 1-3)

### Why Niche First

Trying to be everything for everyone means being nothing for nobody. Topmate is broad and shallow — they have fitness coaches next to software engineers next to astrologers. There is no curation, no expertise signal, no trust.

We go deep in tech. When someone in India thinks "I need a system design mock interview," Familiarise should be the first name. Owning one vertical completely is worth more than being mediocre across ten.

### Target Verticals

| Vertical | Demand Signal | Revenue Potential |
|---|---|---|
| System design interviews | Highest search volume, FAANG prep is massive in India | High — candidates pay ₹2,000-5,000/session |
| DSA prep / mock interviews | Evergreen demand, placement season spikes | Medium-High |
| Career mentorship | Engineers at crossroads (switch to management, change stack) | Medium |
| Resume / LinkedIn review | Perfect for document review feature (unique to us) | Medium — lower price point, higher volume |
| Mock interviews (behavioral) | Often bundled with system design | Medium |
| Startup tech advisory | CTOs, tech leads advising early-stage founders | High — premium pricing |

### Content Strategy

Every consultant creates content as part of their onboarding commitment:

1. **LinkedIn post** announcing they've joined Familiarise (template provided, but personalized)
2. **Blog post or thread** about their area of expertise, with a CTA linking to their Familiarise profile
3. **Free webinar** (hosted on Familiarise) on a high-demand topic — acts as top-of-funnel

Content multiplier: We reshare every consultant's post from the Familiarise brand account, tagging them. This creates a cross-pollination effect across their audiences.

### SEO Play

**Profile page optimization:**

Each consultant profile page is a landing page. Optimize for:
- `[Name] consultation` (e.g., "Rahul Sharma system design consultation")
- `[Skill] mentor India` (e.g., "system design mentor India")
- `[Skill] mock interview online` (e.g., "system design mock interview online")
- `best [skill] consultant` (e.g., "best DSA tutor India")

**Technical SEO:**
- Schema.org `Person` + `Service` structured data on every profile
- Rich snippets: rating, price range, availability
- Canonical URLs, proper meta descriptions, OG tags
- Server-side rendering (Next.js already handles this)
- XML sitemap auto-generated for all consultant profiles

**Content SEO:**
- `/blog` section with pillar content:
  - "How to Prepare for System Design Interviews in 2026"
  - "Top 10 DSA Patterns Every SDE-2 Should Know"
  - "Career Switch Guide: Backend to ML Engineering"
- Each pillar links to relevant consultant profiles (internal linking)

## Phase 2: Demand Generation (Months 2-4)

### Free Webinars as Top-of-Funnel

The webinar feature is not just a product — it's a growth engine.

**Playbook:**
1. Consultant hosts a free 30-min webinar on a high-demand topic (e.g., "System Design: URL Shortener")
2. Registration page collects email + profile data
3. During webinar, consultant mentions availability for 1:1 deep dives
4. Post-webinar email sequence: recording link → "Book a 1:1 with [Name]" CTA → follow-up after 3 days
5. Conversion target: 5-10% of webinar attendees book a paid session

**Webinar topics that convert (tech vertical):**
- "System Design in 45 Minutes: [Specific System]"
- "Resume Review Live: I'll Review 5 Resumes on Screen"
- "From SDE-1 to SDE-3: The Unspoken Playbook"
- "Mock Interview Live: Watch Me Crack a FAANG Round"

### Referral Program

| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| Consultant refers another consultant | ₹500 credit for both (referrer + referee) |
| Consultee refers another consultee | ₹200 credit for both |
| Consultant refers a consultee | ₹300 credit for consultant, ₹200 for consultee |

Credits are spendable on-platform only. This creates a closed-loop economy that keeps users engaged and reduces disintermediation.

### College Ambassador Program

**Target:** 10 engineering colleges in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities.

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| Ambassador count | 1-2 per college |
| Compensation | ₹100-200 per sign-up they drive (tracked via referral code) |
| Responsibilities | WhatsApp group promotion, poster placement, organizing 1 free webinar/month |
| Selection criteria | Active in college tech community, good social media presence |
| Support | Marketing materials, free premium account, direct Slack channel |

**Target colleges (initial):**
- IITs (Delhi, Bombay, Bangalore, Madras, Kanpur)
- NITs (Trichy, Warangal, Surathkal)
- BITS Pilani
- IIIT Hyderabad
- DTU, NSUT (Delhi NCR — proximity to team)

### Social Proof Loop

```
More reviews → Higher trust → More seekers → More bookings → More reviews
```

**Tactics to accelerate the loop:**
- Post-session popup: "Rate your session with [Name]" (2 clicks, not a form)
- Email reminder 24 hours after session if no review submitted
- Incentive: Leave a review = ₹50 credit (first 3 reviews only, to prime the pump)
- Display review count prominently on profile cards ("47 reviews" is more persuasive than star ratings alone)

## Phase 3: Expand Verticals (Months 4-6)

### Expansion Order (by demand and market readiness)

| Priority | Vertical | Why Now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business / startup advisory | Natural adjacency — tech founders already on platform |
| 2 | Product management | PMs frequently seek mentorship, overlap with tech audience |
| 3 | Career coaching (general) | Broader appeal, high search volume |
| 4 | Health & wellness | Emerging demand in India, telemedicine awareness post-COVID |
| 5 | Creative professionals | Design, content, video — growing freelance economy |

### Expansion Playbook (per vertical)

1. Recruit 10-15 founding consultants in the new vertical (same Phase 0 playbook)
2. Create vertical-specific landing page (SEO-optimized)
3. Host 3-5 free webinars to generate demand
4. Cross-pollinate: existing tech consultees may also need business/career advice
5. Vertical-specific content marketing (blog posts, social media)

### What NOT To Expand Into (Yet)

- Astrology / spiritual consulting (dilutes brand, attracts low-quality supply)
- Legal consulting (regulatory complexity, liability risk)
- Financial advisory (SEBI registration requirements)
- Medical consultations (telemedicine regulations, malpractice liability)

### Content Marketing

- **Blog:** 4 posts/month (2 SEO-driven, 2 thought leadership)
- **YouTube:** Monthly "Consultant Spotlight" interviews (5-10 min)
- **Podcast appearances:** Founder appears on Indian tech podcasts (free, high-signal)
- **Newsletter:** Weekly "Top Sessions This Week" + "New Consultants" to subscriber base

### B2B Partnerships

| Partner Type | Value Proposition | Revenue Model |
|---|---|---|
| Startups | Employee mentorship program | Monthly subscription per seat |
| Coding bootcamps | Post-bootcamp mentorship | Revenue share on referrals |
| HR platforms | Integration for employee development | API partnership |
| Colleges | Placement prep + career counseling | Institutional license |

### Mobile / PWA

- Month 6: Evaluate PWA vs native app based on usage data
- If 60%+ traffic is mobile (likely in India), prioritize PWA
- PWA advantages: no app store dependency, instant updates, works offline
- Native app only if PWA proves insufficient (push notifications, performance)

## Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can't recruit 30 founding consultants | Medium | Critical | Lower bar to 15, offer higher incentives, extend timeline |
| Consultants sign up but don't stay active | High | High | Weekly check-ins, gamification, public leaderboards |
| Low demand despite supply | Medium | High | Free webinars as demand gen, college ambassador push |
| Topmate copies our niche strategy | Low | Medium | Execution speed matters — they're too broad to pivot fast |
| Burn rate exceeds runway | Medium | Critical | Keep paid marketing at zero until organic proves PMF |
