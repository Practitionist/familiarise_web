## Battlecard: How to Position Against Each

### vs Topmate (the default comparison)

> _"Why not just use Topmate?"_

**Say**: "Topmate gives you a link page with Zoom. We give you a complete business platform — built-in HD video and chat, 4 service types (not just 1:1), subscriptions for recurring revenue, classes for courses, document review, team collaborations with revenue sharing, and Indian payments through UPI at 0% fees. On Topmate, you're losing 16-18% per Indian transaction to forex fees. On Familiarise, it's 10% with direct Razorpay."

**Don't say**: Anything about Topmate's payout issues (let them discover it themselves; saying it sounds desperate).

### vs SuperProfile

> _"SuperProfile is cheaper at ₹99/month flat"_

**Say**: "SuperProfile is a link-in-bio tool with payments. We're a full consultation platform with integrated video calls, scheduling, waitlists, trial sessions, and multi-week class management. If you're selling PDFs, use SuperProfile. If you're running a consulting business, you need Familiarise."

### vs Preplaced

> _"Preplaced has free trials and placement support"_

**Say**: "We also have free trials — with conversion tracking so you know your trial-to-paid rate. Preplaced focuses only on tech career mentorship. We support any domain — tech, business, health, creative. And our 10% commission vs their 20% means you keep more of what you earn."

### vs GrowthSchool

> _"GrowthSchool has big names and Sequoia backing"_

**Say**: "GrowthSchool sells cohort courses at ₹5,000-15,000. They control the pricing, content, and brand — you're essentially a contractor. On Familiarise, you own everything: your pricing, your brand, your client relationships, your recurring revenue. We're the difference between being a platform employee and being a business owner."
