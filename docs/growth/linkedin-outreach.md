## LinkedIn Limits (The Problem)

| Plan                     | InMails/Month                        | Daily Limit                   | Cost     |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | -------- |
| Free                     | 0 InMails (only message connections) | ~100 connection requests/week | Free     |
| Premium Career           | 5 InMails                            | 25/day                        | ~$30/mo  |
| Premium Business         | 15 InMails                           | 25/day                        | ~$60/mo  |
| Sales Navigator Core     | 50 InMails                           | 25/day                        | ~$100/mo |
| Sales Navigator Advanced | 100+ InMails                         | 25/day                        | ~$150/mo |

So even with Sales Navigator, you cap at ~50-100 cold InMails/month. For reaching hundreds of consultants India-wide, LinkedIn alone won't cut it.

**One hack:** InMails that get a response (accept/decline/reply within 90 days) are credited back. So high-quality, personalized messages that get replies effectively give you "unlimited" InMails.

## Recommended Outreach Strategy (For Your Budget & Stage)

### Month 1: Manual + Free Tools (~$0–25/mo)

   - Find their LinkedIn/Twitter profiles
