# Consultant dashboard information architecture: the sidebar groups and the Settings hub

**Scope:** `app/dashboard/consultant/[consultantId]/ConsultantDashboardLayout.tsx`, `lib/dashboard/nav/consultant.ts`, the `(features)/settings` hub, and the shared `DashboardShell`.

This page records how the consultant (Expert) dashboard is organised, and why. The owner locked "Expert" and "Learner" as the platform's end-user terms during this PR's review rounds; "Consultant" and "Client" no longer appear in the product surface, though the underlying routes, database enums and `consultant`/`consultee` code identifiers are unchanged.

## The shell and the sidebar

The consultant dashboard now shares one `DashboardShell` with every other dashboard tree (personal, organization, workspace and back office), rather than carrying its own chrome. The shell's sidebar begins with the `ContextSwitcher`, which lets a signed-in person move between their Expert dashboard, their Learner dashboard (if they have also bought a session), every organization they belong to, and — for admins and staff — the back office. Below the switcher, the sidebar is a static, unfiltered list of groups, because every surface on a personal dashboard belongs to the one person who owns it. `buildConsultantNav()` in `lib/dashboard/nav/consultant.ts` builds it as:

| Group              | Entries                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (none)             | Home                                                                    |
| Inbox              | Booking requests (count badge), Messages (unread badge), Collaborations |
| Calendar           | Appointments, Availability                                              |
| Sell               | 1:1 & subscriptions, Group programs                                     |
| Studio             | Recordings, Session documents, Products                                 |
| Today and Upcoming | `appointments`                                                          | The same sessions as Next session, minus the one the card already shows: today's remaining sessions first, then later days, five rows together (time, who or what, status), then "All appointments ›". Absent when nothing follows. |
| Money              | Earnings & payouts, Invite & earn                                       |
| Reputation         | Reviews                                                                 |
| (none)             | My library                                                              |
| Settings & help    | Help & support, Settings                                                |

Money holds Invite & earn (`referrals`) next to Earnings & payouts, because referral credit is money the expert earns or spends. Reputation holds Reviews alone.

Sell holds the two plan pages the expert prices and schedules: `sell/sessions` (Consultations, Subscriptions) and `sell/programs` (Webinars, Classes), each with a "+ New" type picker (see [Dashboard information architecture](information-architecture.md#sell)). Studio holds what the expert made: hosted Recordings, Session documents and `products` (Digital products, a single list with one "New digital product" action). Collaborations sits in Inbox because it is a queue of invitations to answer first and a list of co-hosted plans second; its page has a plain header with no "+ New" menu. Offering cards copy the expert's signed personal share link (`?via=<token>` / `?ref=<code>`, locking the 10% own-link platform fee), display subscription cadence and topic/module counts, and hydrate full plan metadata (`deliverables`, `prerequisite`, `material`, `sessionDurationInHours`) in the edit drawer alongside a net take-home preview. Earnings surfaces active `ConsultantFeeWaiver` banners, `Own link (10%)` and `Fee waived` badges on ledger rows, a Tax & TDS (`Form 16A`) quarterly summary with 1-click FY CSV export, and Own-Link vs Marketplace revenue split + repeat learner rate on the Analytics tab. Reviews is a dedicated destination where an expert filters by track (`1:1` vs `Group`) or star rating, replies to learners, reports abusive reviews, and shares sanitised review cards or session milestones to LinkedIn/X. Two rules shape this list: a sidebar entry must be a distinct destination, and a daily work surface is not a preference, which is why Availability stays in Calendar rather than moving into Settings.

Settings & help contains **Help & support** and **Settings** in the sidebar. Help & support has separate pages for Support requests, Evidence requests, Reports & appeals, and Product feedback. Request details retain the dashboard shell and a context panel; the shared secondary menu is hidden on detail routes. Product feedback is separate from support conversations, session ratings, and public Reviews. Settings also remains in the avatar menu as a shortcut. The header Help menu links to the same destinations and the public Help Center. The pinned header action is "View public page".

The mobile tab strip carries four tabs plus **Menu**: Home, Requests, Appointments, Messages. Menu opens a sheet with the switcher, the full nav, Settings and Sign out — Availability, Earnings and everything else not in the four tabs is reached from there, replacing the old five-tab strip that left Messages, the planner and Availability unreachable on a phone.

## Home: action first

Home answers "what do I do now?". The main column, top to bottom, is the setup checklist while it is open, **Needs you**, **Next session**, the **Today** and **Upcoming** lists, **Money** and **Latest feedback**; the sidebar holds the Requests panel, a few plain numbers, a "Share your page" row and, only when they exist, the organisation sessions and the sessions booked as a client. Nothing renders a zero or a dash as a stat: a number with nothing behind it is left out, and ratings appear only once the expert has one ("4.8 from 12 learners").

The server page (`home/page.tsx`) prefetches the dashboard payload and dehydrates it; `HomePageClient` also starts the one Requests inbox read (`useHomeRequests`, the consultation tab in priority order) at the same time, so Needs you and the Requests panel share one fetch with no queue behind the payload. `home-model.ts` holds every derivation as pure functions, and `__tests__/dashboards/expert-home.test.tsx` pins them.

| Block           | Source                                                                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Needs you       | Inbox rows, `needsYou`, `payoutSetup`, the Stream unread count                | At most five lines, one button each. Requests and make-ups with a deadline come first by deadline, then reschedule replies, requests without a clock, payout setup, unread messages, then documents, invitations and reviews. Up to three requests are named; the rest fold into one "N more requests to answer" line. A request the learner owes (a pay link, a checkout) never appears. Empty reads "You're all caught up." |
| Next session    | `appointments`                                                                | Confirmed, live, non-tentative occurrences that have not ended. Join enables from `CONSULTANT_JOIN_WINDOW_MS` (15 minutes) before the start, through the rejoin grace; before that the button reads "Join at <time>" with the time it opens.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Money           | `financialSummary`, `payoutSetup`, `sessionsDelivered`, `performanceSnapshot` | "₹X ready · next payout <date>" with an Earnings link; a second line carries sessions this month and ratings. The payout date is the Monday batch from `nextPayoutBatchAt()`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| At a glance     | `appointments`, `sessionsDelivered`, `responseRate`                           | Sessions delivered ("6 this month · 4 last month", both on the expert's own month boundaries from `homeBoundaries()`), booked time in the next seven days, replies within a day over 30 days, sessions delivered in total. A zero row is left out.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Requests panel  | The same inbox read                                                           | A 403 reads "You don't have access to these requests." with no Retry and is reported once; any other failure reads "Couldn't load requests. Try again." with Retry, never the server's words.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Latest feedback | `latestReview`                                                                | The newest live review with text, read by `readLatestReview()` in `lib/reviews-latest.ts` (one bounded `findFirst`, inside the supplementary reads): rating, a short quote, the learner's first name (none when they chose anonymity) and the date. Nothing renders until one exists.                                                                                                                                         |
| Share your page | `expertShareHref()`                                                           | The page mints the expert's signed own link on the server and passes it down, so there is no client fetch. Not minted for an inspecting admin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

The "Get started" checklist (`SetupChecklist`, the same component the organization Home uses) sits first in the main column. It shows while any of five steps is open and disappears when all five are done; there is no dismiss control and nothing is stored, so a step that regresses reappears by itself. `deriveConsultantChecklist()` in `lib/dashboard/consultant-checklist.ts` decides each step:

| Step                        | Done when                                                                                                         | Links to                |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Get your profile verified   | `verificationStatus` is `VERIFIED`                                                                                | `settings/verification` |
| Complete your profile       | Every profile field scores: description, headline, experience, domain detail, work history and an image           | `settings/profile`      |
| Set your weekly hours       | Availability rows exist for the schedule type the expert runs (weekly or custom)                                  | `availability`          |
| Publish your first offering | A discoverable 1:1, subscription, webinar or class plan, or a `PUBLISHED` digital product; a draft does not count | `sell/sessions`         |
| Finish payout setup         | `payoutRequirements()` is not blocked and asks for nothing except a GSTIN, which is only ever due later           | `settings/get-paid`     |

`getConsultantDashboard()` computes the steps from the inputs of its own profile read (`FIRST_RUN_PROFILE_SELECT` in `lib/data/consultant-first-run.ts`), so a fully set-up expert pays no extra query, and `readFirstRunSteps()` returns `null` once every step is done. The profile step reuses the weights in `lib/profiles/profile-completion.ts` minus the availability, plan and verification weights, which are their own steps; see [the onboarding reference](../onboarding/01-system-reference.md).

The Needs-you wording for payout setup, reschedule replies, make-ups, documents, invitations and reviews comes from `deriveConsultantActionItems()` in `lib/dashboard/action-items.ts`; `home-model.ts` ranks those rows and adds the request and message lines. Two rows are new and both are severity `info`: `collab-invites`, the count of pending invitations on live (unarchived) webinar and class plans, links to `collaborations` and uses the same `LIVE_WEBINAR_COLLABORATOR`/`LIVE_CLASS_COLLABORATOR` predicates (`lib/collaborators/live-plan.ts`) that page lists with; `reviews-reply`, the count of live reviews with no reply from the expert, links to `reviews?tab=needs-reply`. The review count uses `NEEDS_REPLY_WHERE` in `lib/reviews-needs-reply.ts`, the same predicate the Reviews inbox filters with.

## Documents and Recordings pages

My library (`library`) is the expert's buyer Library: the shared `LibraryPage` with the Purchases tab (`readDigitalProductLibrary()` keyed by the user), plus Session recordings and Session documents from their learner profile when they have one (see [Dashboard information architecture](information-architecture.md#library)); the old `purchases` URL is gone and answers 404. The Studio group's Recordings and Session documents entries are cross-session indexes, grouped by appointment (`LibraryBrowser`, ten sessions per page), with filters for the learner, kind (All, Consultation, Subscription, Trial and the group kinds), date and search, and for documents "Started by anyone / you / the learner" (plus "Plan materials only") and the status filters "Your turn", a waiting status, "Done" and "Shared" (one rule, `libraryBucket` in `lib/documents/thread-copy.ts`, places a thread under a filter and `libraryChip` gives its row the same words for the viewer's side: "Waiting for review" for a learner, "Waiting for learner" for an expert, both words in mixed or organisation views). Subscription headers read "Started <date> · <n> sessions" and class headers "Your batch <start> – <end> · <n> sessions" ("Batch …" for a host), with cards ordered by latest session and every date in the booking pages' display zone. Documents list one expandable row per document thread (title, "Latest: <name>", a "vN · N versions" chip, Started by, status, Updated and Open latest), sorted needs-you first; expanding it lists the versions newest first, and plan materials sit in their own table. Recordings render one session timeline per booking: a header with the plan, expert, "Open booking" and one retention line, then one row per session (Session, Recording with a state badge, Length, Play) that expands into its parts. The per-row Manage is a link to the booking's Recordings page and appears only on a session that still has a part. When different people removed parts of one session, the line names each of them, for example "Removed by LearnPro Academy and Angela Anderson", without a date. Rows link out; the work happens on two dedicated per-appointment pages reached from the appointment details page, which acts as a hub with snapshot cards for Documents, Materials and Recordings; the Materials card ("Files included with this offering.") lists every file with Open and Download and has no page of its own.

| Page                  | Route                                                                          | Purpose                                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Documents workspace   | `/dashboard/consultant/[consultantId]/appointments/[appointmentId]/documents`  | Review queue, reply, share a file, status banner per thread |
| Recording details     | `/dashboard/consultant/[consultantId]/appointments/[appointmentId]/recordings` | Sessions, parts, player, manage sheet                       |
| File viewer (new tab) | `/files/documents/[documentId]`, `/files/materials/[materialId]`               | Same-origin viewer that signs on every load                 |

Collaborators and co-hosts see only appointments they are a party to. See [session documents](../documents/README.md#pages-and-routes-per-role) and [recordings](../stream/19-recordings-lifecycle-and-access.md#the-recording-details-page).

## The Availability page

`/dashboard/consultant/[consultantId]/availability` renders `AvailabilitySection` and `AvailabilityGrid` over the same data, and the save contract is the strict whole-profile `PUT /api/user/consultants/[id]`. That logic lives once, in `settings/use-consultant-settings-form.ts`, shared with the Profile and Booking mode settings sections. Its loader error ("Couldn't load your profile") is shared with those sections, so it names the profile rather than "settings".

## The Settings hub

Settings still renders through the shared `SettingsLayout` primitive (a grouped hub leading to individual full-width forms with a Settings back link, one URL per section), now shared with the consultee, org and back-office trees rather than being consultant-specific. `SETTINGS_SECTIONS` in `settings/settings.ts` reads:

| Group          | Section                | URL                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Account        | Account                | `/settings/account`       |
| Account        | Notifications          | `/settings/notifications` |
| Public profile | Profile                | `/settings/profile`       |
| Public profile | Experience & education | `/settings/experience`    |
| Public profile | Verification           | `/settings/verification`  |
| Business       | Booking mode           | `/settings/booking`       |
| Business       | Get paid               | `/settings/get-paid`      |

Account absorbed `/profile`, `/settings/change-password` and the old Security tab, so a consultant's name, photo, phone, password, active sessions, connected accounts and account deletion live in one place shared with the consultee, workspace, and back-office dashboards. The old Security tab is folded into `account`, and `/profile` no longer exists; `accountSettingsHref` gives each viewer their own Account page (a consultant or consultee their Account section, ADMIN/STAFF the back-office Settings page, ORG_WORKSPACE `/settings/account`). Experience & education lets an expert edit work history and education after onboarding.

Booking mode is how people book the expert (instantly or by request) and whether new 1:1 bookings are open. It is named for the setting, so "Booking requests" means only the Inbox page that lists the requests (its h1 and crumb read "Booking requests"). The hub root `/settings` shows the grouped section list. The payout section is `/settings/get-paid` (`GetPaidClient.tsx` lives directly inside `settings/get-paid/`); `?tab=` is not read. A jest pin (`__tests__/dashboards/settings-hub.test.tsx`) asserts that every section has a unique URL.

The consultee (Learner) Settings hub also uses `SettingsLayout`: its sections are Account, Notifications and Learning profile (`CONSULTEE_SETTINGS_SECTIONS` in the consultee tree's own `settings.ts`), following the same one-URL-per-section pattern.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **The "This month" stat grid.** Home once opened with a 2×2 grid of Sessions, Available and two rating tiles (dashes when empty), and a milestone sentence with a share dialog. Superseded by the action-first layout above; `ThisMonthCard.tsx` was deleted, and the milestone share dialog is gone for good (Latest feedback takes its place).
- **Flat root-only library rows.** The Documents index listed one row per thread root and hid its versions. Superseded by expandable thread rows with version history (`components/dashboard/library/DocumentTables.tsx`).
- **One Offerings item.** Offerings (`offerings`) held every plan kind, digital products and Collaborations behind a sub-tab bar and a type pill, inside a Business group. Superseded by the Sell pages and the Studio group; the old URLs answer 404.
- **Products and Collaborations under Sell.** Sell once held four items: the two plan pages, Products (Digital products and a Replays tab) and Collaborations. Products moved to Studio at `products`, because a file the expert made is Studio's subject, and Collaborations moved to Inbox. The Replays tab, `ReplaysPanel`, `HostedReplays`, `listHostedReplays()` and the Recordings page's "Sell as replay" button were deleted; replay sales are closed, and the public `/explore/products?tab=replays` grid is unchanged. `sell/products` and `sell/products?tab=replays` answer 404 with no redirect.
- **"Today" and "Booking requests" settings.** The home entry was called Today although the page is not limited to today (its route is still `home`), and the Settings section for how people book was called "Booking requests", the same words as the Inbox page. They are now Home and Booking mode.
- **"Reputation & growth" and a one-item Money group.** Reviews and Invite & earn once shared a group called Reputation & growth, with Money holding only Earnings & payouts. Invite & earn moved under Money and the group is now called Reputation, with Reviews alone. Do not restore the old group name.
- **Redirect claims.** Earlier versions of this page said the old `purchases` URL, the `settings?tab=availability` deep link and the Offerings URLs redirect. No redirect exists for any of them (see [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-03-dashboard-ia-drop-legacy-redirects.md)); they answer 404.
