# ADR: Access rules after the bug hunt — operator refunds through booking routes, organisation plan edits, learner profile access

- **Status**: Accepted (owner decisions, 2026-11-13). Decisions 1 to 4 and 6 are **built** on the branch that carries this record. Decision 5 records two findings that were **not built**: one deferred, one not reachable. Decision 6 also lists three routes left as they are, with the reason.
- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [The booking bug hunt](2026-11-12-bug-hunt-booking.md), [The money doors after the bug hunt](2026-11-12-bug-hunt-money-doors.md), [Cancellation flow](../booking/08-cancellation-flow.md), [Money remedies](../support/05-money-remedies.md), [Rescheduling flow](../booking/07-rescheduling-flow.md)

## Context

The third pass of the 2026-11-12 bug hunt compared what each page allows with what the API behind it allows, outside the admin money doors. It found nine access findings, AC-1 to AC-9. The owner decided them on 2026-11-13. This record keeps the decided rule for each, what enforces it, the options not chosen and the limits that remain. Two terms first. A _party_ to a booking is its learner (the buyer who requested it) or its expert (the consultant on the plan). An _operator_ is a platform user whose role is STAFF or ADMIN.

## Decisions

1. **An operator who is not a party changes no request or trial status through the ordinary booking routes** (AC-1, decided, built).
   - **Before.** A STAFF or ADMIN user who was not a party could decline a paid 1:1 or plan request, decline a pending trial or cancel a trial through the routes the parties use. Each of those refunds the buyer in full, and none of them asks for a fresh sign-in, applies the staff refund limits or writes an operator audit row. The appointment cancel route already refused the same operator.
   - **Decided.** The request status and trial routes refuse a non-party operator exactly as the cancel route does, with 403 `PLATFORM_CANCEL_DOOR_REQUIRED` and the message "Only the learner or the expert can do this here. Operators act on bookings from the back office, so any refund is audited.", whatever status the request names: decline and approval on the request routes; decline, cancel, schedule, complete and convert on the trial route. Party identity is checked before the role, so an operator who is a party (they bought the booking, or they are its expert) keeps that party's rights. Every refund amount and tier, and every status transition, are unchanged.
   - **Enforced by** `platformCancelDoorRefusal` in `lib/booking/platform-cancel.ts`, the one check the cancel route (`app/api/appointments/[appointmentId]/cancel/route.ts`) and these handlers now share, before anything is written: every `PATCH` status on `app/api/bookings/consultations/[consultationId]/route.ts` and `app/api/bookings/subscriptions/[subscriptionId]/route.ts`, the two list routes `app/api/bookings/consultations/route.ts` and `app/api/bookings/subscriptions/route.ts` (which accept only `REJECTED`), and every `PATCH` status and `DELETE` on `app/api/trials/[trialId]/route.ts`. The first version refused only `REJECTED` there, which left a non-party operator able to cancel an unpaid pending trial or approve a request; review found it and both now refuse. A request that carries the staff door's grant (`grantPlatformCancel`) passes, as on the cancel route.
   - **Who called these routes as a non-party.** No server code calls these handlers; sweeps and moderation call the library functions directly (`stampRejectionRefundOwed`, `refundRejectedRequest`, `refundCancelledTrial`, the unpaid-trial expiry job). The screens that call them are party screens: the shared requests inbox (`components/dashboard/shared/requests/RequestsInbox.tsx`, `request-decision.ts`), the learner's appointments (`ConsulteeAppointmentsAdapter.tsx`) and the expert's appointments (`useConsultantEventActions.ts`). An operator who opens an expert's dashboard from the back office and presses Decline now gets the refusal.
   - **The staff door covers a paid pending request.** A paid pending 1:1 or plan request always has a tentative appointment, because checkout creates it with the capture (`lib/payments/checkout/handlers/consultation.ts`, `subscription.ts`). The back-office platform cancel door (`app/api/staff/appointments/[appointmentId]/cancel/route.ts`) reaches it from the booking's Ops panel, which offers Cancel booking while the booking has no sessions or a live one. The cancel route's allowed-from set (`CANCELLABLE_FROM` in `lib/booking/transitions.ts`) includes `PENDING`, and an operator's cancel is priced at the expert-side tier (`consultantInitiatedPct`, 100% under the platform defaults), so the operator gives the same full refund a decline gives, with step-up, limits and an audit row.
   - **Options not chosen.** A new staff door for trials was deferred. Adding step-up and limits inside each of the four handlers was not chosen, because it would copy the staff door into four places.
   - **Honest limits.** Staff have no door to end a paid trial until one is built; only the learner or the expert can end it. An operator who is the learner on a request can still decline it as a privileged caller and be refunded in full without the staff door, because the decided rule leaves a party's rights unchanged.

2. **Editing an organisation's webinar or class plan requires the named expert to be an ACTIVE expert member of that organisation** (AC-2, decided, built).
   - **Before.** The webinar and class plan edit routes and the plan materials routes checked only that the caller was the expert named on the plan, so an expert removed from, or suspended in, the organisation that owns the plan could still change its price, schedule, published state and materials.
   - **Decided.** For a plan whose `organizationId` is set, the caller's consultant profile needs an `ACTIVE` membership with role `EXPERT` in that organisation, the same test the organisation catalog applies when it names a plan's expert. A personal plan (no `organizationId`) is unchanged and reads no membership.
   - **Enforced by** `activeOrgExpertWhere` and `isActiveOrgExpert` in `lib/enterprise/membership-guards.ts`. The catalog POST (`app/api/organizations/[orgId]/catalog/route.ts`) now builds its named-expert check from `activeOrgExpertWhere`. `PATCH` on `app/api/bookings/webinars/crud-with-plan/route.ts` and `app/api/bookings/classes/crud-with-plan/route.ts` answers 403 `NOT_ACTIVE_ORG_EXPERT` ("Only an active expert of the organisation that owns this plan can change it.") through `orgPlanEditRefusal` before any write, topic creation included. `resolvePlanAccess` in `app/api/plans/shared/materials-handler.ts` lets the plan's expert through on an organisation plan only while active; an organisation operator holding `materials.manage.orgPlan` still manages the materials as before.
   - **Example.** Hannah Anderson is the expert on Acme's webinar plan. While her Acme membership is `ACTIVE` she edits the title and the price. Once Acme suspends or removes her, the same edit answers 403 and nothing is written; Acme's managers still manage the plan's materials.
   - **Options not chosen.** Reserving price and publish for organisation managers was not chosen, because it needs an organisation-side plan editor that does not exist. Refusing every expert edit of an organisation plan was not chosen, because the expert is the one who keeps the plan current.
   - **Honest limits.** The check is the membership alone: an active expert of an organisation that has since been deactivated can still edit. It is read before the edit's transaction, so a removal that lands during an edit is seen on the next edit.

3. **Staff read a learner's profile in the public shape, no operator edits it, and an admin's edit of a user's profile is step-up and audited** (AC-3 and AC-4, decided, built).
   - **Before.** `GET /api/user/consultees/[id]` returned the whole learner profile to every STAFF and ADMIN user, and `PATCH` let either overwrite it. `POST` created a learner profile for any user id and had no caller. On `app/api/user/[id]/route.ts`, an ADMIN could overwrite another user's profile and professional background with no fresh sign-in and no audit row.
   - **Decided, learner profile.** The owner and ADMIN read the whole profile. STAFF read the profile's `id` and `userId` and the user fields any other viewer of a profile sees: `publicProfileUserSelect` in `lib/data/consultant-public.ts`, which is the expert route's non-owner selection (`app/api/user/consultants/[id]/route.ts`), now one shared constant. The learner-only fields (about me, goals, career stage, skills, budget preference, billing state) are not in that selection, so STAFF do not see them. `PATCH` is owner-only. `POST` is deleted; learner profiles are created by `lib/profiles/ensure-consultee-profile.ts`.
   - **The operator's learner dashboard still renders.** Operators open a learner's dashboard from the back office (`viewerMayOpenProfile` in `lib/dashboard/profile-access.ts`). Its layout reads the profile through `fetchConsulteeDetails` (`lib/user.ts`) and uses only the owner's name, which the reduced read carries. The Learning profile settings page (`LearningProfileForm.tsx`) shows the form only to the learner; anyone else sees "Only the learner can see and edit their learning profile." and no save button.
   - **Decided, admin edits.** An ADMIN's `PUT` or `PATCH` of another user on `app/api/user/[id]/route.ts` needs a fresh session (`requireFreshSession`, 403 `REAUTH_REQUIRED`, as the route's `DELETE` already does) and writes one `OpsActionLog` row (`recordOpsAction` in `lib/backoffice/ops-action-log.ts`, surface `users.moderate`, actions `user.profile.update` and `user.background.update`) in the same transaction as the write. The row records the names of the fields written, not their values. The owner's own edit is unchanged. The two screens that call these routes (`AccountDetailsSection.tsx` and `LearningProfileForm.tsx`) now use `fetchWithReauth` (`lib/auth/reauth-client.ts`), so a `REAUTH_REQUIRED` answer opens the re-authentication dialog and retries once.
   - **Options not chosen.** Keeping operator access and only adding an audit row was not chosen, because it leaves STAFF able to read and change a learner's private fields. Owner-only for everything was not chosen, because it breaks the operator's view of a learner's dashboard.
   - **Honest limits.** ADMIN still reads the whole learner profile. Both screens that call `app/api/user/[id]/route.ts` act on the signed-in user, so no screen sends an admin edit of another user today; the step-up and the audit row bind the API. The audit row's reason is fixed text, because the route has no operator reason field, which is why it is written with `recordOpsAction` and not through `withOpsAction`, whose doors take a reason in the body and sit behind a back-office surface check.

4. **Three plain fixes** (AC-5, AC-8 and AC-9, decided, built).
   - **An archived 1:1 or subscription plan is hidden from everyone but its expert (AC-5).** The by-id `GET` on `app/api/plans/consultations/[consultationPlanId]/route.ts` and `app/api/plans/subscriptions/[subscriptionPlanId]/route.ts` now applies `canViewPlanDetail` (`lib/data/plan-viewable.ts`, the wiring of `isPlanViewable`), the rule the webinar and class routes apply: a draft or an archived plan answers 404 to anyone but its expert, who can still preview it. The gate is `canViewPlanById`, which reads no session for a public plan and lets a failed session lookup answer 503 `SESSION_LOOKUP_FAILED` with Retry-After, so an outage never reads as a hidden plan. Before, `isHiddenDraft` hid drafts only. The routes do not use `planDetailResponse`, because that helper gives a public plan a shared CDN cache and these GETs are also the editor's read. The subscription feasibility route still hides drafts only.
   - **Withdrawing a reschedule needs the initiator to still act for their side (AC-8).** `app/api/appointments/[appointmentId]/reschedule/withdraw/route.ts` checks `actsForRescheduleSide` (`lib/booking/reschedule-side.ts`) for the side the request was opened from, and answers 403 `NO_LONGER_A_PARTY` otherwise. The learner side is the buyer or an admin of the paying organisation; the expert side is the plan's expert or a host-organisation operator, and for a trial its own expert. This is the side check the respond route used, moved into the library so both routes share it; it is built on the same helpers whose rules the open path applies inline (`resolveEventHostActor`, `isOrgAdminOfAppointment`). The side check judges party identity before the role, so an expert whose account is STAFF or ADMIN is still the expert on respond and withdraw. A learner suspended in the organisation funding the booking is refused withdraw with 403 `MEMBERSHIP_SUSPENDED`, as open and respond already refuse them (`isSuspendedLearnerOf`). Before, the withdraw checked only that the caller had opened the request, so an operator who had since left the host organisation could still withdraw.
   - **The invitation preview's comment is true and the preview is throttled (AC-9).** The comment in `app/api/invitations/preview/route.ts` now says the token is the invitation's cuid id, which is hard to guess but not a secret-grade random value. The route applies `applyRateLimit` with `invitePreviewLimiter` (30 per minute per IP, `lib/rate-limit.ts`) before it reads anything.

5. **Two findings not built** (AC-7 deferred; AC-6 not reachable).
   - **AC-7 is deferred** with #2724 "Money doors: step-up on issuing a referral credit and on releasing an earnings hold". `PUT /api/consultant/tax-info` changes a consultant's tax declarations with no fresh sign-in. A fix needs the route to call `requireFreshSession` and the Get paid form's save (`saveTaxInfo`) to use `fetchWithReauth`, so a stale session opens the re-authentication dialog.
   - **AC-6 is left alone.** The 1:1 and subscription plan `PUT` and `DELETE` lack the organisation-governed refusal their archive `PATCH` has, but no product path creates such a plan with an `organizationId`: the plan create routes never set it and the organisation catalog offers only webinars and classes. Only the seed's organisation fixtures (`prisma/seedFiles/14c-seed-org-appointments-and-feedback.ts`) write one.

6. **The reduced read for STAFF covers the learner list and every booking read that embeds the learner** (extends decision 3, built after preview QA).
   - **Before.** Preview QA, signed in as a STAFF user who was not a party, found two more reads that returned a learner's private profile fields (about me, goals, career stage, skills, budget preference, billing state) and email: `GET /api/user/consultees` (the learner list) and `GET /api/bookings/consultations/[consultationId]`, which embeds the requester.
   - **Decided.** The decision 3 rule applies to every read of a learner by an operator. ADMIN, the owner and a party to the booking read the learner in full. A STAFF user who is not a party reads the public shape: on the learner list the same `publicProfileUserSelect` shape as the single read; on a booking read the requester's ids and public identity (id, name, image), the shape the booking list routes already return (`PROFILE_WITH_USER_SELECT`).
   - **Callers checked.** No screen calls the learner list. No screen calls `GET` on the consultation, subscription or trial detail routes, or on `GET /api/scheduling/appointments/[appointmentId]`. The trials list feeds the expert's appointments and the Sell tab's pending counts, which read only the learner's id, name and image. The scheduling appointments list feeds the expert's appointments page, which an operator can open from the back office, and that page also renders the learner's name and image only. No client code reads the email or the learner-only fields from any of these routes.
   - **Enforced by** `publicRequester` and `isStaffNonParty` in `lib/booking/list-selects.ts`, applied after the read in the consultation, subscription and trial detail GETs and in `GET /api/scheduling/appointments/[appointmentId]`, per row in `GET /api/trials`, and per row by `withPublicRequesterForStaff` (`lib/data/consultant-appointments.ts`) in `GET /api/scheduling/appointments` and in the server-rendered expert appointments page, so the operator's view of an expert's appointments carries no learner email in its hydrated data either. The learner list chooses its query by role.
   - **Left as it is, with the reason.**
     - `GET /api/dashboard/admin/approval-payments` gives the back-office Awaiting payment panel (`AwaitingPaymentPanel.tsx` under `OperatorAppointmentsClient.tsx`) each learner's name and email, and the panel shows the email so staff can chase an unpaid approval. The owner can keep it: the party's contact email stays visible to support, and this record says so. Or the owner can narrow it: drop the email and have the panel open the support case or the booking instead.
     - `GET /api/staff/moderation/reviews` returns the reviewer's email to the moderation screen, which shows only the name and image. It is a back-office moderation read of a review's author, not a learner profile read, so it was not changed here. The owner can keep the email for contacting the author, or drop it, since no screen renders it.
     - The detail `PATCH` responses of the consultation and subscription routes still embed the whole requester when a non-party operator approves a request. No screen approves through that route as an operator, and these are write answers, so they were not changed.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **A privileged role as a substitute for being a party on the decline and trial routes.** Replaced by `platformCancelDoorRefusal` in decision 1. Do not let `isPrivileged` stand in for a party on any route that moves money.
- **The plan's expert as the only test for editing an organisation plan.** Replaced by `isActiveOrgExpert` in decision 2.
- **The whole learner profile for every operator, operator edits of it, and the unused create.** Replaced by decision 3.
- **`isHiddenDraft` on the 1:1 and subscription by-id GETs, and a reschedule withdraw that checked only who opened it.** Replaced by `canViewPlanDetail` and `actsForRescheduleSide` in decision 4.
- **A learner's whole profile and email in the learner list and in booking reads for every operator.** Replaced by `publicRequester` and `withPublicRequesterForStaff` in decision 6.
