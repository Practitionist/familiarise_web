## Context

Two read-only reviews of `dev` at 39f98669f (2026-11-03) looked at booking after the megafix and the dashboard IA rework. V2A found two P0 bugs on the Requests tab: Decline on a booking being rescheduled cancelled the whole paid booking (A-01), and a consultant's own reschedule showed in their inbox as an incoming request (A-02). It also found that the multi-week subscription engine works as built but does not serve the product. A plan of "3 a week for 6 months" opens one floating 7-day cycle at a time, only after the previous cycle is delivered, so the consultant comes back about 24 times, on desktop only, and earliest-fit packs sessions onto consecutive days. Months × 4 sells 72 sessions, not 78. V2B found that almost nothing turns a finished booking into the next one, and that a trial is marked `CONVERTED` when checkout starts rather than when money is captured (R-01).

## What this pull request ships

1. **Decline versus reschedule (A-01, A-02).** Every REJECTED and APPROVED status door (the consultation and subscription list and `[id]` PATCH routes) calls `assertNotBeingMoved` (`lib/booking/request-route-guards.ts`): an open `PENDING_REVIEW` reschedule answers 409 `USE_RESCHEDULE_RESPOND`, and live released sessions (tentative `RESCHEDULED`) with no proposal answer 409 `USE_CANCEL_ENDPOINT` on decline and `PLACE_RELEASED_SESSIONS` on approve. The transition itself carries `NOT: { appointment: BEING_MOVED }` in its compare-and-set WHERE, so a proposal opened between the check and the write still loses. Deviation from the research report (which also asked to refuse a wrapper that was ever confirmed): "being moved" means an open proposal or live released sessions only, with no history predicate, because every path back to `PENDING` restores the parent through `originStatus` and a booking with neither has nothing to protect. Inbox rows for a booking being moved offer:
   - a client proposal with times: "Accept new times" (respond accept) and "Keep the original time" (respond decline);
   - a client "any time works": "Pick a new time" (the allocator) and "Keep the original time";
   - the consultant's own proposal with times: only "Withdraw my request" (reschedule withdraw), in the "Waiting on the client" bucket;
   - a consultant preference-only release: "Pick a new time" and "Withdraw my request";
   - stranded released sessions: "Pick a new time".

   The row clock is the proposal's `expiresAt`. `countRequestsToAnswer` leaves out the consultant's own open proposals with times, and `countOpenRequestsForCap` and `countActiveConsultationRequests` leave out bookings being moved (`BEING_MOVED`, `lib/data/needs-you.ts`). The allocator refuses 409 `PROPOSAL_AWAITING_CLIENT` over the consultant's own open proposal with times (`lib/booking/allocation/preference.ts`). Inbox buckets go by who acts: sessions to schedule (cycle 1 or an open plan week) are the consultant's, banded by deadline ("This week" with no clock), and the old "Waiting on them" bucket is now "Waiting on the client".

2. **Trial conversion at payment (R-01).** `convertCompletedTrial` (`lib/trials/conversion.ts`) writes `COMPLETED` → `CONVERTED` and `convertedToSubscriptionId` only when the subscription is paid: in `confirmApprovalStatus` at capture (`lib/payments/webhooks/handlers.ts`) or at a no-gateway checkout that settles at once (`skipPayment`). An abandoned checkout leaves the trial `COMPLETED`. Owner decision (2026-11-03): attribution counts a captured subscription to any plan of the same expert **within 30 days of the trial's completion** (`TRIAL_CONVERSION_WINDOW_MS`), whichever org the subscription is billed under: the expert earned it either way. The conversion time is the subscription's first captured payment.
3. **One lead-time contract.** `leadMsFor(purpose, { settled })` in `lib/booking/lead-time.ts`: book 15 minutes; request and allocate 15 minutes settled and 2 hours unpaid; reschedule 24 hours. `isTooSoonFor(purpose, ctx, startMs, nowMs)` is the one boundary (a start at or before now plus the lead is too soon); `assertRequestLeadFits` (request-for-approval refuses a priced request inside 2 hours with 409 `REQUEST_TOO_SOON`), `assertUnpaidApprovalFits`, the reschedule route and slot validation all call it. The grid route takes `?purpose=book|request|allocate|reschedule&unpaid=1`, marks a cell `tooSoon` when its start is at or before now plus the lead (the writers' boundary), returns a top-level `leadMs` and folds it into the ETag; clients re-apply it between polls (`isTooSoonAt`). `TimePickerPolicy.lead` drives it (allocate unpaid from `AllocationRequest.settled`; both reschedule policies 24 hours), `MobileDayListPicker` gets the same lead as `minLeadHours`, and the expert page's `windowsForDay` takes it (a priced REQUEST-mode plan uses 2 hours). `isBookableWindow` requires `bookingStatus` `available`, so a partly booked window is never offered to a buyer.
4. **Grid cells on the UTC lattice (B-01).** `processAvailabilitySlots` no longer splits blocks at the viewer's midnight; `breakDownSlotsByDuration` snaps window starts to the absolute UTC half-hour and keys each window by the viewer day of its own start, so :45 zones such as Kathmandu and Eucla keep every atom. `splitSlotsByDay` is deleted. `dayRangeBounds` returns an exclusive end (the next day's first instant), so the last half-hour of a window is painted.
5. **Subscription pacing, multi-week Phase 0 (C-01).** `subscriptionEntitlement` uses fixed plan weeks from the plan start's zone-day in `schedulingTimezone` (week k is `[day0 + 7k, day0 + 7k + 7)`). The open week is the current plan week, or the next one once the current one is full, never further; `nextBatch` is `sessionsPerWeek` minus the sessions held in that week, bounded by what remains; delivery no longer gates the next week. `cycle.count` is the plan weeks to plan end and `cycle.ordinal` the zero-based plan week; `planWeekNumber`, `planWeekStartsAt` and `rescheduleWindow` are exported. Copy reads "Week N of W" in `subscriptionCycleHeading` and the grid footer (`GridQuota.weekNumber`/`weekCount`), and cells before the open week paint "Limit reached". The subscription auto search first tries to keep a free day between sessions (the preference sweep included), then packs, so spacing never costs a placement. Known gap, left for a follow-up issue: the auto search matches a custom availability row on its start day only, so one custom row that spans several days offers only its first day (daily rows are unaffected). The consultee's cycle-done notice says their latest sessions are done and claims no week number (it can fire mid-week), and the booking card reads "Week done · the next N to be scheduled." for either viewer, never "the next 0".
6. **Consultant reminders (C-02).** `nudgeUnplacedPlanWeeks` (`lib/booking/week-nudge.ts`) runs inside the `expire-stale-requests` job. For `APPROVED` plans with a confirmed session and no move in flight, when the current plan week has sessions to place, the consultant is reminded from 09:00 on the week's first day in their own zone, then the next day and the third, only between 08:00 and 21:00 local and outside their quiet hours. One bell (`NEW_BOOKING_REQUEST` workflow with a `weekSummary`) and one email (`SUBSCRIPTION_WEEK_NUDGE`) a day per consultant list every plan due, deduped per consultant and local day; plans are read by keyset on id from a start that moves one read budget (1,000 plans) every hour and wraps (`rotationOffset`), so every plan is reached however many there are. The consultant's zone is their user's `timezone`, falling back to the plan's `schedulingTimezone`. A next week opened early is never nagged. The reminder links to the consultant's allocate page (`/dashboard/consultant/{id}/requests/{plan}/allocate`); there is no org-scoped allocate page, so org-hosted plans use the same link.
7. **Top-up retry (C-03).** The `reconcile-occurrence-availability` top-up is due when availability changed since the last attempt or the last attempt is at least 6 hours old (`TOP_UP_RETRY_MS`); changed calendars go first, then the oldest attempt first, still capped at 25 events and 60 seconds a run. Owner decision (2026-11-03): it places open weeks on its own **only for plans the consultant scheduled with Auto**. The choice is a stable machine token (`schedule:auto` / `schedule:by-hand`, `SCHEDULE_REASON` in `lib/booking/allocation/schedule-choice.ts`, parsed strictly, shown to people through `SCHEDULE_REASON_COPY`) in the `reason` of the allocation's `BookingStatusHistory` row, no schema change. Only the plan's first scheduling records it, derived on the server from what the plan held before the run; a later week (including the one-tap Auto), an operator's run, the sweep, or re-placing a released session never changes it. **Plans scheduled before this release have no token and count as by hand** (consultant consent, no backfill): they lose the old availability-edit top-up and get the week reminders instead; every other plan gets the reminders plus a one-tap "Auto-schedule this week" (a manual auto allocation for the current week) on the allocate page the reminder links to.
8. **Mobile allocate (C-12).** On a phone the allocate page shows "Auto-schedule this week" (`MobileAutoSchedule`: auto mode, `topUp` for a plan that already holds sessions) under the desktop notice.
9. **Subscription reschedule picker (B-03).** It clamps to `rescheduleWindow`, the allocator's window with the moved sessions released, so picks the auto-confirm would drop are not offered.
10. **Cancel (A-07, A-08).** A released session whose original time passed with no replacement quotes at full notice, unless the consultee released it (the recorded notice floor then applies). The consultant's cancel dialog (`viewerRole="consultant"`) says "{client} gets a full refund of ₹X. Cancelling a paid booking counts against your reliability.", with "Keep booking" and "Cancel and refund"; the consultee's confirm button is "Cancel booking".

## Decision 1: multi-week allocation is fixed plan weeks plus a Regular time ([#2561](https://github.com/Practitionist/familiarise_web/issues/2561))

- **Model.** A plan has fixed plan weeks and a consultant-set **Regular time** (for example Mon, Wed and Fri at 18:00). The system keeps the next **4 weeks** of real sessions booked and rolls the horizon forward weekly. Each week shows a **conflict list** for the consultant to fix.
- **Catch-up.** Missed or unplaced weeks are caught up from plan slack. Anything that cannot fit is refunded per session at plan end. A consultant-caused miss counts toward reliability after 2 in 90 days.
- **Partial placement is always allowed**, with at most 12 live rows per plan, and it is refund-neutral because refunds use the per-session tiers.
- **Who places.** The host places by default. The consultee proposes times (requested mode); self-pick inside the expert's Regular time comes later and only if the expert opts in.
- **Plan length.** A new nullable `durationInWeeks` column, so 26 × 3 = 78 sessions. Months stay as a label; existing plans keep months, with no backfill.
- **No soft reserve.** Beyond the 4-week horizon other buyers can book the Regular time. A clash met by the roll-forward goes on that week's conflict list.
- **Naming.** `schedulingPeriodStartsAt` becomes `planStartsAt` through `@map` (no database change). `schedulingPeriodEndsAt` is deprecated and dropped at the reset window. The allocator's window becomes `selectableFrom` and `selectableUntil`. Copy reads "Week 3 of 26".
- **Phases.** Phase 0 is in this pull request. Phase 1 adds the schema (`durationInWeeks`, a Regular-time pattern, the `planStartsAt` map, horizon bookkeeping). Phase 2 adds the roll-forward job in `lib/cron/cleanup-registry.ts` with a Postgres lease and fair rotation, the conflict list and catch-up. Phase 3 adds expert opt-in self-pick. Phases 1 to 3 are **(planned)**.

## Decision 2: retention phase 1 is same-expert first, cross-expert on-site only ([#2562](https://github.com/Practitionist/familiarise_web/issues/2562))

- **"More from {expert}"** (the same expert) appears everywhere: rebook, renew, and the upsell to a subscription or class.
- **Cross-expert "Similar plans"** appears on-site only (plan and profile pages, Home), and only after the learner's appointment with their expert has ended.
- A learner who arrived through an expert's own link (`OWN_LINK`) never gets another expert's plans by email. Org-sponsored learners see only their org's catalog.
- **Phase 2**, cross-expert email to marketplace-sourced learners with marketing consent, is decided after phase-1 data shows the effect on expert link sharing and churn.
- **Email provider.** Stay on Resend Free until launch. Move to Pro before the first marketing batch or when real volume needs it, because Free stops at 100 emails a day. The reminder is in the Resend guardrails and in Step 5 of the [pre-production checklist](../notifications/05-pre-production-checklist.md).

## Decision 3: attendance keeps today's evidence-based outcomes ([#2563](https://github.com/Practitionist/familiarise_web/issues/2563))

Outcomes stay as `lib/booking/session-outcome.ts` derives them from Stream presence: `HOST_ABSENT` and `CUT_SHORT` are voided and strike the expert, `LEARNER_ABSENT` is a forfeit, and `NOBODY_JOINED` and `INCONCLUSIVE` are parked for ops with no money moved. Lateness marks, consecutive-miss pauses and a both-absent auto-reschedule are a P2 policy for later **(planned)**.

## Consequences

- An inbox Decline can no longer end a confirmed booking that is being rescheduled; ending it goes through the cancel route.
- Trial-conversion counts include only paid conversions.
- Until Phase 1 lands, plans still sell months × 4 sessions and the consultant still places each week, but weeks open on the calendar and the consultant is reminded.
- Cross-expert recommendations by email stay off until the owner decides phase 2.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **Floating 7-day cycles gated on delivery.** Replaced by calendar plan weeks in Phase 0 and by the rolling 4-week horizon in later phases.
- **Decline as an implicit cancel.** A decline during a reschedule is refused; the cancel route owns ending a booking.
- **Conversion stamped at checkout start.** Moved to capture.
- **The #2456 rename of the scheduling-period columns.** Superseded by the `planStartsAt` map and the deprecation of `schedulingPeriodEndsAt`.
