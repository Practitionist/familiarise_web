# ADR: One status vocabulary for bookings, the appointment detail page, and demand guidance for experts

- **Status**: Accepted (owner decisions, 2026-11-12, 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14). Everything below is **built** on the branch `feat/train1-booking-states` unless it says otherwise.
- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [The booking follow-up train](2026-11-12-booking-follow-up-train.md) (decisions 4 to 7), [One target lifecycle per product](2026-11-13-product-lifecycles.md) (decision 5), [The appointment detail page](../dashboard/appointment-detail-page.md), [Booking state machines](../booking/18-state-machines.md)

## Context

Before this change a booking could read three different ways on one screen. The detail page, the learner's list, the expert's home, the Requests inbox, the organisation pages and the back office each had their own label map, and they disagreed: a pay-first checkout that was waiting for payment read "Requested … pay after the expert approves", a booking with an open reschedule read "Requested" (a reschedule puts the stored status back to `PENDING`) and offered Approve and Decline buttons that the server refuses, a seat whose payment had lapsed still read "Registered" with a Join button, and "Completed" appeared as soon as the clock passed the session, before anyone knew whether it had happened.

The stored statuses stay as they are (decision 1 of the lifecycle record). What changed is how they are shown.

## Worked example: one 1:1, three moments

Charlotte books a ₹1,000 1:1 with Hannah, who books instantly.

No Remind: the hold is Charlotte's own |

## Decisions

1. **Seven words and one reason line (owner, 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-14).** Every badge a buyer, expert, organisation or back-office user sees for a booking is one of Requested, Awaiting payment, Confirmed (Registered for a group seat), Being moved, Completed, Cancelled and Refunded. A booking that ended without its session reads Cancelled with one plain line saying why and by whom: "The expert declined this request.", "The expert did not answer in 48 hours.", "Payment was not completed in time.", "You withdrew this request." ("Charlotte withdrew…" for the other side), "Cancelled by you on 12 Nov.". A landed refund reads Refunded with "₹500.00 refunded on 13 Nov.". A paid plan waiting for its first times reads Confirmed with "Paid. Your expert is arranging times (within 48 hours).". "Approved" appears only as a timeline step, and only when the history shows the expert approving a request. A host looking at their own webinar or class keeps its event words (Draft, Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled).
   - **Enforced by** `bookingStatus` in `lib/appointments/booking-status.ts`, which every surface calls: `deriveBookingPresentation` (`lib/dashboard/money-state.ts`), the list chip `appointmentStatusChip` (`lib/appointments/occurrences.ts`), both home pages, the Requests inbox labels, the organisation list, detail and payer views, the operator appointments list and the back-office case page. The reason is read from the booking's lifecycle edges, which the detail read now selects with the side that wrote each one (`readAppointmentDetail`). The guard `__tests__/labels/no-raw-enums-in-customer-copy.test.ts` runs every kind, stored status, source and viewer through the function and fails on any other word.
   - **Options not chosen.** Nine words, keeping Declined and Expired as badges: two more words for states a reader treats the same way, with the difference already carried by the reason line. Seven words with no reason line: a cancelled booking would not say who ended it or why.

2. **A pay-first hold awaits payment, and its Pay works.** A direct-checkout hold, a held seat and an unpaid trial read Awaiting payment with their deadline and a Pay button that opens the right page (the trial's checkout page from `AppointmentVM.trialId`; a seat's or a 1:1's held order). A reschedule of an unpaid trial hold answers `PAY_LINK_LIVE` ("still waiting for payment; pay or cancel it first") instead of "already cancelled or completed"; no rule changed. **Enforced by** `stageOf` in `booking-status.ts`, `bookingPayHref` (`lib/appointments/trial-checkout-href.ts`) and the reschedule route's `payLinkLiveError`.

3. **Being moved, with only the buttons the server accepts.** While a reschedule is open every viewer reads Being moved with the proposal and the original time. The proposer may withdraw (on the proposal card); the other party may accept or decline in the banner through the respond route; an operator or a suspended organisation member sees no button. **Enforced by** `rescheduleAnswersFor` and `rescheduleAnswerRequest` (`lib/appointments/reschedule-answer.ts`), whose bodies the respond route parses with the shared `RescheduleRespondSchema` (`schemas/appointments.ts`). `__tests__/booking-algorithm/reschedule-answer-contract.test.ts` checks, for each viewer, that what each button sends is accepted by its route's schema and identity check, because two production-blocking bugs this month came from untested request bodies.

4. **An unpaid, lapsed seat is not Registered and has no Join.** The learner's list and detail page read their own seat status and payment; a held seat whose window closed reads Cancelled ("Payment was not completed in time.") and the Join gate refuses it (`isUnpaidSeat` in `lib/appointments/status.ts`). No access rule changed.

5. **The detail page: one banner, the Payment card under Help, one zone.** One amber banner sits at the top of the page whenever the viewer must act (pay, accept or decline new times, approve a request, place plan sessions), with the deadline and the primary button (`NeedsYouCallout`). The right rail reads Details, Resources, Help, Payment, Timeline. Every deadline and time on the page, including the Payment card's "link valid until", is in the viewer's profile zone. Withdrawing, cancelling or paying refreshes the page. Cancelled class sessions stay in the schedule with their state ("Cancelled by the host · make-up owed", "· make-up Tue 18 Nov", "· refunded instead"), and the host schedules a make-up from that row in a dialog (`SingleTimePicker`, purpose `makeup`) instead of a picker stacked above the list. The Recordings card no longer says "This session was cancelled." for a booking that is only being moved. The expert can cancel a scheduled trial from its appointment page: the trial cancel door (`DELETE /api/trials/[trialId]`) always admitted the trial's expert, with a full refund and a strike, but the page keyed the action on the list row and never offered it; what it sends is the shared `TrialCancelBodySchema`, pinned by `__tests__/booking/trial-expert-cancel-contract.test.ts`.
   - **Options not chosen.** A banner for payment only, and a Pay button on the Payment card with no banner (decision 5 of the follow-up train).

6. **Completed follows the outcome, not the clock.** A booking reads Completed, moves to the Past tab and invites a review once each session's outcome is recorded (`isOccurrenceHeld`: delivered, parked for a human, voided or an attendance outcome) or, for the learner, once the server's rating gate (`rateable` from `useSessionFeedback`) says they attended. **Enforced by** `allOccurrencesHeld` in `deriveBucket` (`lib/appointments/bucket.ts`) and `statusFacts` in `money-state.ts`.

7. **After leaving an event, back to the list (owner, 2026-11-13).** A successful seat leave shows its confirmation, drops the detail query and goes to the Appointments list. No access rule changed (`authorizeAppointment` still refuses a released seat). **Option not chosen:** a read-only "You left this event" page, which would widen who may read a booking.

8. **Seats and charges.** The host and co-hosts are not counted as seats on the detail page; a seat that cost nothing reads "Free", not "Paid" (`seatPaymentBadge` in `lib/appointments/seat-payments.ts`, also on the roster page); "Problem with this charge" shows only when the viewer was charged (a succeeded payment above zero), otherwise "Problem with this booking".

9. **The learner's Appointments list.** "My alerts" is a compact strip right under the next appointment (`AppointmentsShell`'s `afterHero` slot), the "need action" count opens its tab, and every empty state is worded for the learner or the expert.

10. **Demand guidance, with no new capacity control (owner, 2026-11-13).** A "You're in demand" banner on the expert's Requests page and home appears only when a load signal fires and names the signals in plain words, with the actions that fit: Pause new bookings and Review requests first (the existing booking-settings PATCH with its compare-and-set), Limit plan clients (a link to the field), and Add more hours (a link to availability) when learners are waiting. "Dismiss for 7 days" is kept in the browser per expert and a new kind of signal shows the banner again. A paused expert sees the existing paused notice instead. Settings → Booking says under each control what it does and shows its live number ("6 open requests now", "3 of 5 active plan clients").

- **The signals and their thresholds are judgement defaults**, named in `lib/booking/demand-signals.ts`: open requests at or above 75 % of the cap (5 or more with no cap), a request lapsing within 12 hours, a paid plan unplaced for more than 24 hours, the next 14 days at or above 80 % booked, and at least one learner waiting for a filled time.
- **The read.** `GET /api/user/consultants/[id]/demand` (owner only, `no-store`, parsed by `expertDemandSchema`) runs `readExpertDemand` (`lib/data/expert-demand.ts`): the profile, two cap counts, the nearest request expiry, the unplaced paid plans, the published and booked time of the next 14 days (`readFeasibilitySpans`) and the distinct waiting learners, eight statements in all, the profile read included. Nothing here is public: an expert's load and reliability are never shown to buyers.
- **Options not chosen.** Descriptions in Settings only, with no banner; deadline alerts by email or bell (a notifications decision); hover info icons, which are invisible on touch and fail accessibility guidance.

## Honest limits

- A session whose outcome is never recorded stays out of Past and Completed. Outcomes come from the call webhook and the attendance jobs; while the production job fleet is paused, a session nobody joined keeps reading Confirmed.
- The list surfaces read fewer facts than the detail page (no history), so their Cancelled rows carry no reason line; the detail page carries it.
- The "Instant booking" description in Settings still mentions requests for a held time; the time-picker pull request changes that rule and its words.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- `APPOINTMENT_STATUS_BADGE`, `TRIAL_STATUS_BADGE`, `eventUnionStatusBadge`, `BOOKING_LABEL`, the organisation list's `STATUS` map and the operator list's `humanizeEnum` status: replaced by `bookingStatus`.
- "Payment lapsed", "Awaiting schedule", "Declined", "Withdrawn", "Expired", "Approved" and "Pending" as badges: replaced by the seven words and the reason line.
- "Requested (you) / Approved (expert)" on every 1:1 timeline: a pay-first booking starts "Booked", and "Approved" needs the approval edge.
- A trial Pay button keyed on the `trial-` prefix of the row id, and an expert trial cancel keyed on the list row's source (so it never showed on the appointment page): both read `AppointmentVM.trialId`.
- Make-up pickers stacked inline above a class's schedule: replaced by the row's "Schedule make-up" dialog.
