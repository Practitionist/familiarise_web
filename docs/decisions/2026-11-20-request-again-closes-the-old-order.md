# ADR: "Request again" closes the old order first

- **Status**: Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-20. Ships in the pull request that adds this record.
- **Date**: 2026-11-20
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [The reconciler re-reads closed orders](2026-11-21-reconciler-rereads-closed-orders.md), [The money line says only what the rows prove](2026-11-20-money-line-says-only-what-is-provable.md), [An approved request's unpaid order returns what its checkout consumed](2026-11-20-unpaid-approval-orders-return-what-checkout-consumed.md), [Booking lifecycle](../booking/06-booking-lifecycle.md), [Approval payments](../finance/10-money-in/05-approval-payments.md#3-time-and-expiry)

## Context

A booking whose pay window has closed while its gateway order is still `PENDING` can still be confirmed. The expiry sweep has not claimed the order yet (and the job fleet is paused before launch), so a late capture goes through `confirmExistingAppointment` (`lib/payments/webhooks/handlers.ts`), which confirms the booking while its time is free. The booking page says so: "The payment window closed on … If your payment still goes through, the booking is confirmed while the time can still be booked; otherwise the money is refunded automatically."

The same booking sits in the learner's Cancelled list, where the detail page offers "Book again". A learner who followed it and booked a second time could end up with two confirmed bookings if the first payment then landed. The first design kept "Request again" as it was and, when the late capture confirmed the original, cancelled the new request on the server. That needed a column linking the new request to the original, and the schema is frozen. The owner chose instead to close the old order before the new request starts. That needs no schema change, and the old booking can no longer be confirmed behind the learner's back.

## Worked example

Charlotte Brown's ₹1,000.00 (100,000 paise) consultation with Hannah Anderson was approved. She opened the pay link at 10:00 with ₹200.00 (20,000 paise) of referral credit and a 10% code, and her organisation's seat counted one engagement. The window closed at 10:15. At 10:20 she opens the booking and clicks **Book again**.

1. A dialog asks: "Close your earlier payment link? Your earlier payment link will be closed, and a payment that reaches it after this is refunded automatically. If it was already paid, that booking is kept and no new request starts." She confirms.
2. `POST /api/bookings/<appointment>/request-again` asks Razorpay whether the order holds a live payment. It holds none, so one transaction runs. The order moves `PENDING → EXPIRED`. Her ₹200.00 credit, the code use and the seat's engagement come back. The request moves `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT → EXPIRED` with the history reason `REQUESTED_AGAIN`, and the held time is released. The route answers `{ "closed": 1 }`, and only then does the page open Hannah's booking page.
3. Her bank captures the payment at 10:21, after the click and its re-check, and the webhook arrives at 10:25. (Had the capture landed in the instant between the gateway check and the commit, the re-check after the commit would have found it and refunded it at once.) It lands on the `EXPIRED` order. `handlePaymentSuccess` records it as `SUCCEEDED` with "Auto-refund pending", confirms nothing and refunds it in full through `refundBookingPayment`. If that refund call fails, `retry-auto-refunds` re-drives it.
4. Had the webhook arrived at 10:19, before her click, the capture would have confirmed the booking. The route then answers `409 ALREADY_PAID`: "Your earlier payment went through, so that booking is confirmed. No new request was started." Nothing is closed, and the page stays on the confirmed booking.
5. Had the bank captured at 10:14 with the webhook never arriving, Razorpay would answer that the order holds a live payment. The route answers `409 PAYMENT_IN_PROGRESS` and closes nothing, so the order stays `PENDING` and `reconcile-payment-status` finds the capture and confirms the booking (or refunds it if the time is no longer free).

## Decisions

1. **The control decides at the click, from the rows it shows.** `RequestAgainButton` (`components/booking/RequestAgainButton.tsx`) is the one control behind "Book again" on the learner's booking page and on the pending-checkout page. It receives the learner's own payment rows (the detail reader scopes a group booking's rows to the viewer). The booking page files a booking in the same tab as the list, because both pass `deriveBucket` the input `bucketFactsOf` (`lib/appointments/list-status-facts.ts`) builds, so a lapsed pay-first hold or approval is Cancelled on its page too and shows "Book again" there. When, at the click, `openOrderPastWindow` (`lib/booking/request-again-rules.ts`) finds an order still `PENDING` whose `expiresAt` has passed, it shows the warning and calls the route. Only `{ closed }` lets it navigate. While any order is still `PENDING` the control is a button with no `href`, so opening it in a new tab cannot skip the close; with none it is the plain link it was before. A busy, unavailable or lost booking lock (423 `APPOINTMENT_BUSY`, 503 `BOOKING_LOCK_UNAVAILABLE`, 409 `LOCK_LOST`) shows the server's own retry sentence and changes nothing.
2. **One transaction closes the caller's own order.** `closeOrderForRequestAgain` (`lib/booking/request-again.ts`) runs under the appointment lock in one Serializable transaction with the usual retry, for the caller's own booking only: the request's requester, the trial's learner, or a seat holder on a webinar or class (anyone else gets `404 NOT_FOUND`, read before the lock so the answer never depends on whether the booking is locked). Under the lock, before the transaction, it asks the gateway about each of the caller's `PENDING` orders through `cancelPaymentIntent`, the abandoned-payment sweep's own check: a live payment answers `409 PAYMENT_IN_PROGRESS` and an unproven answer `503 GATEWAY_UNCHECKED`, and either closes nothing. Inside the transaction:
   1. It reads the booking and the caller's `PENDING` orders.
   2. With no `PENDING` order, it answers `ALREADY_PAID` if a capture of the caller's is kept for the booking (`keptCaptureWhere`: no auto-refund or owed-refund marker, no open or lost dispute) and its refunds do not cover its amount, and `{ closed: 0 }` otherwise. A partial goodwill refund therefore still answers `ALREADY_PAID`.
   3. An order whose window is still open (or has no `expiresAt`) is refused with `409 PAY_WINDOW_OPEN`.
   4. `expirePendingPaymentsInTx(tx, { appointmentId, userId, reason })` claims each order by compare-and-swap and returns its credit, code use and engagement. The payment goes first, which is the capture's own lock order (payment, then booking).
   5. The booking takes its lapsed state through the writer its own sweep uses, each carrying the money predicate in its `WHERE`:
      - An approved request goes through `lapseApprovedRequest` with the new reason `REQUESTED_AGAIN`, which maps to `EXPIRED`.
      - A pay-first hold (`PENDING` with `DIRECT_CHECKOUT`, and no open move) ends as the abandoned-payment pass ends it: the request goes `PENDING → EXPIRED`, its occurrences are cancelled and tombstoned, its `HELD` seats are cancelled and the appointment is soft-deleted.
      - A paid trial still `AWAITING_PAYMENT` moves to `CANCELLED`, as `expire-unpaid-trials` does, and its held call is tombstoned (`softCancelTrialAppointmentInTx`) after the waiting learners' notices are staged.
      - A webinar or class seat releases only the caller's `HELD` seat and stages the seat-opened notices.
   6. A booking in any other status with such an order (for example a confirmed plan, or a request back in `PENDING` under an open move) is refused with `409 NOT_CLOSABLE`, as is an order the gateway was not asked about.
3. **A capture that won first closes nothing.** If the booking's compare-and-swap misses after the orders expired, the transaction is rolled back by throwing, so the expiries and give-backs never commit. The answer is `ALREADY_PAID` when `holdsCapture` finds a capture of the caller's kept for the booking that its refunds do not cover, and `NOT_CLOSABLE` otherwise. The control then shows the refusal, refreshes the page and does not navigate.
4. **The gateway is asked before the close and once more after it.** Before, as the abandoned-payment sweep asks it, because a Razorpay "cancel" only reads the order: an order closed while the gateway held a capture would leave the reconciler's `PENDING` cohort with the money still at the gateway. After the commit, the owner decided on 2026-11-20 to close the remaining window: each order the click closed is read again with `readRazorpayOrderHolding`, and a capture found there (one that landed between the check and the commit) goes through `handlePaymentSuccess`, whose `EXPIRED` arm records it and refunds it through `refundBookingPayment`. A later webhook or reconcile run for the same capture finds the row `SUCCEEDED` and changes nothing more. A re-check the gateway cannot answer never undoes the close; it is reported once per click (`REQUEST_AGAIN_RECHECK_FAILED`), and a capture it missed still reaches the same refund when its webhook arrives. If that webhook never arrives either, `reconcile-payment-status` reads the order again on later runs for 72 hours after the close, and once more after them, and sends a capture it finds through the same refund ([the reconciler re-reads closed orders](2026-11-21-reconciler-rereads-closed-orders.md)). The report's order ids are needed for a refund by hand while the job fleet is paused, or for a capture that lands after the order's final read.
5. **The warning is true as written.** "A payment that reaches it after this is refunded automatically" holds because a capture on an `EXPIRED` order takes the `captured_after_release` arm of `handlePaymentSuccess` and is refunded through the booking refund door, and `retry-auto-refunds` re-drives a refund that failed. "If it was already paid, that booking is kept" holds because a capture that committed first answers `ALREADY_PAID` and one the gateway holds answers `PAYMENT_IN_PROGRESS`. Before the click the booking page still names both outcomes, because the order is still `PENDING`.
6. **A second click changes nothing.** The order is already `EXPIRED`, so the route answers `{ closed: 0 }` and nothing is returned twice. A refunded late capture does not count as "already paid", so a click after it still continues.
7. **The Home widget does not nudge a request the learner closed.** The lapsed pay-link list (`toLapsedPayLinks`) skips an `EXPIRED` edge whose reason is `REQUESTED_AGAIN`, because the learner has already requested again. It also skips a request whose expert's public page would 404 (the expert is not `VERIFIED`, or the profile is deleted), because its "Request again" links to that page; `readLapsedPayLinks` (`lib/data/lapsed-pay-links.ts`) selects `publicProfileFacts` and asks `isPublicExpertProfile` (`lib/profiles/public-profile.ts`), handing the pure half only a boolean, so the status never reaches the client. The owner asked for this on 2026-11-20. The booking page and both appointment lists read the closed booking's reason line from the same facts (its `EXPIRED` history edge and `unpaidLinkSeen` in `lib/appointments/list-status-facts.ts`): "You requested a new booking." for the learner and "<learner> requested a new booking." for the expert, while a hold or approval that lapsed on its own reads "Payment was not completed in time."
8. **No schema change and no notice to the learner.** The click is the learner's own act, so no "request expired" email or bell is sent. Anyone waiting on the released time, including a paid trial's, still hears it is free.

## Consequences

- The Home lapsed-link row and its "Request again" action item are unchanged links. Both list only requests whose order its sweep has already expired: `expireLapsedPayLink` and `lapseApprovedRequest` expire the order in the same transaction as the request, so there is never an open order left to close. The pending-checkout page shows "Book again" only once its order is `EXPIRED` or `FAILED`, so its control never asks either.
- `__tests__/db/booking/request-again-closes-old-order.db.test.ts` pins the transaction on Postgres: one return of what the checkout consumed, the refund of a later capture, the refusal after a capture that won first, a click racing a capture, a second click, an open window, a stranger, and the hold, trial and seat arms. `__tests__/booking/request-again-control.test.tsx` and `__tests__/booking/request-again-route.test.ts` pin the control, what it sends against the route's schema, and the route's answers.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **"Book again" as a plain link while an order could still confirm the old booking.** Replaced by decision 1.
- **A refund by hand as the only recovery for a capture whose re-check and webhook both failed.** Superseded on 2026-11-21 by the reconciler's 72-hour re-read of closed orders; the hand refund remains only while the job fleet is paused.
- **Cancelling the new request when the old payment lands.** It needed a column linking the two requests (the frozen schema has none: `Subscription.renewedFromSubscriptionId` means a renewal, `Payment.parentPaymentId` an overage side-charge). It was not built.
