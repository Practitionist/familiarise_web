# ADR: An expert who is not verified cannot be booked

- **Status**: Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21. Ships in the pull request that adds this record.
- **Date**: 2026-11-21
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: issue #2925 "[booking][P1] An unverified expert can still get sessions", [Verification lifecycle](../onboarding/04-verification-lifecycle.md), [Booking system HLD](../start-here/01-booking-system-hld.md), [Approval payments](../finance/10-money-in/05-approval-payments.md), [Public pages and discovery](../enterprise/30-programs-and-lifecycle/05-public-pages-and-discovery.md)

## Context

An expert is verified when platform staff have checked their identity and credentials (`ConsultantProfile.verificationStatus = VERIFIED`; the other values are `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, `UNDER_REVIEW` and `REJECTED`). Until this change only direct checkout refused an unverified expert (`EXPERT_NOT_VERIFIED`, 409). A free or paid trial, a 1:1 request, its approval pay-link and a trial pay-link re-mint never asked, so a learner who reached an unverified expert's plan could book a real session with them. The public plan list APIs, the plan pages, the webinar and class listing behind `/explore/programs` and an organisation's member catalog all listed those plans, and an expert invited by an organisation starts as `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, so the catalog was a real way in.

When moderation rejected a verified expert (`PROFILE_UNVERIFIED`, `unverifyProfiles` in `lib/moderation/side-effects.ts`), their plans stayed listed, their approved-but-unpaid links stayed payable, and their cached public profile kept offering trials and requests for up to five minutes because the action route purged only the review surfaces.

## Worked example

Priya Nair signs up as a career coach and publishes a 1:1 consultation at ₹1,500 and a subscription with a free 30-minute trial. Her profile is `PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

- Before: Rahul Verma opened her subscription plan from a shared link, booked the free trial and got a confirmed session. Her plans showed in `GET /api/plans/consultations` and on her organisation's member catalog.
- Now: Priya still sees and edits both plans in her planner and opens their pages as a preview. Rahul gets a 404 on the plan page, does not see the plans in any list, and a hand-built `POST /api/trials` answers 409 `EXPERT_NOT_VERIFIED` ("This trial isn't available to book until the expert is verified.") with no trial, appointment or payment written. Her organisation's admin sees "Waiting for verification" against her on the Members page.
- Staff verify Priya. Every list and page shows her plans again on its next read; nothing was unpublished, so nothing has to be republished.
- Months later, moderation rejects Priya. Anil, who paid ₹1,500 for next Tuesday, keeps his session, and Priya's payout follows the normal holds. Kavya bought Priya's subscription last week and is waiting for her sessions to be placed; her plan is paid, so it is kept. Right after the reject commits, once the gateway confirms Meera's order holds no payment, her request, which Priya approved yesterday and Meera has not paid, expires and its ₹1,770 order is expired with it; the referral credit Meera's checkout spent comes back. Rohan's request, which Priya has not answered, is declined, and Sneha's unpaid ₹200 trial hold is released. Meera and Rohan each read "Priya Nair isn't taking bookings right now, so your request was closed. No payment was taken." If Meera's bank captures the old order an hour later, the capture is refunded and her booking stays expired. Opening her old link shows "This expert isn't taking bookings right now. No payment was taken.", with no offer to request again.

## Decisions

1. **Every booking door refuses an unverified expert with one check.** `assertExpertVerified` (`lib/booking/booking-rule-error.ts`) throws `BookingRuleError("EXPERT_NOT_VERIFIED")` with the copy checkout already used. It is called by checkout's pricing (`calculateAmountAndValidate`), checkout's in-lock re-read (`verifyPlanExistsInsideLock` in `lib/payments/checkout/locks.ts`, so an expert rejected between pricing and the lock is refused before any gateway order), `POST /api/trials` (free and paid), `POST /api/scheduling/request-for-approval`, and the pay-link price (`calculateAmount` in `lib/payments/operations/approval-payment.ts`, both the consultation and the trial arm, which every new mint and every re-mint runs). The approval routes answer the pay-link refusal as a 409 with the code (`approvalMintConflict`), and neither the mint nor the trial re-mint reports it to Sentry.
2. **The trial and request doors also refuse what checkout refuses.** `assertPlanOnSale` (same file) refuses an archived plan (`PLAN_ARCHIVED`) and a plan kept to an organisation the buyer is not booking for (`PLAN_NOT_AVAILABLE_TO_ORG`); checkout's pricing now calls it too, so the three doors share one rule. A soft-deleted expert's plan answers 404 "plan not found" at both doors, as checkout answers.
3. **An unverified expert's plans are shown to that expert only.** The rule is `isPublicExpertProfile` and its `where` form `publicExpertProfileWhere` (`lib/profiles/public-profile.ts`): verified and not deleted. It filters `oneOnOnePlanListWhere` (the 1:1 and subscription list APIs) for everyone but the owner, `buildPlanWhereClause` (the webinar and class list APIs behind `/explore/programs`), `liveConsultantWhere` in `lib/data/explore-programs.ts` (curated sections and topic counts), and `getOrgMemberCatalog`. The plan page gate `isPlanViewable` now takes the owner's facts as a required field, so every caller selects them: the four detail readers in `lib/data/plan-details.ts`, the 1:1 and subscription by-id APIs, the webinar and class by-id APIs, and the event waitlist. The owner preview arm runs first, so the expert opens their own pages. Experts may still create and publish plans while they wait, so they are bookable the moment they are verified.
4. **A rejection stops new sales and honours what is booked.** The verification filter stops new sales, and decision 6 ends the unpaid ones already open; plan rows are not unpublished or archived, so a re-verified expert's plans come back by themselves. Paid future sessions run and payouts are unchanged. Suspend or ban stays the tool for cancelling and refunding everything. The moderation action route calls `purgeExpertSurfaces` with the rejected profile's id after the commit (`unverifiedConsultantProfileId` on the transactional result).
5. **An organisation's invited experts are verified first.** The member catalog lists only verified experts' plans. The admin's Members page derives its badge from the same rule (`memberExpertOf` in `lib/data/org-members-query.ts` sends a boolean, never the raw status) and reads "Waiting for verification" (`expertVerificationLabel` in `lib/labels/org-labels.ts`).
6. **A reject ends every unpaid sale right after it commits** (owner decision, 2026-11-21; the owner confirmed on 2026-11-21 that the ending runs after the commit, not inside the reject's transaction). The moderation transaction only moves the profile to `REJECTED` and ends the collaborations, so it stays small. Phase 2 then runs `endUnverifiedExpertSales` (`lib/booking/unverified-expert-notices.ts`) the way Request again closes an order. First it asks the gateway about every open order of the expert's unpaid sales (`cancelPaymentIntent`). A live payment keeps its sale open for reconciliation to confirm, and a gateway that cannot answer closes nothing for that sale. Then one Serializable transaction (`endUnpaidSalesInTx` in `lib/booking/unverified-expert-sales.ts`) ends every sale whose orders were all cleared, each through a CAS in an existing helper. It records the history reason `EXPERT_UNAVAILABLE`, a neutral key because learners read their booking history; staff see the cause on the moderation action. After that commit, each closed order is read once more (`readRazorpayOrderHolding`), and a capture found goes through `handlePaymentSuccess`, whose `EXPIRED` arm refunds it even if no webhook ever arrives. The held-time and learner notices are staged afterwards. Anything left unchecked, unread or unnoticed is reported once per reject (`REJECT_SALES_UNSETTLED`). Every sale writer (the trial and request doors, the checkout commit on every attempt, both pay-link claims and the unpaid-approval guard) re-reads the expert's standing under a share lock (`assertExpertStandsInTx` in `lib/booking/expert-standing.ts`). A reject's profile write therefore waits for an in-flight write, and the ending that follows sees what that write committed. The counts on the action summary are `payLinksClosed`, `requestsDeclined`, `trialHoldsReleased`, `seatsReleased` and `salesKept`. If the whole ending step fails, the action records `unpaidSales: "failed"`, and the moderation enforcement retry sweep (`retryUnpaidSalesEnding` in `scripts/cleanup/retry-moderation-enforcement.ts`) re-runs it until it settles, within the sweep's shared attempt and age budget (owner decision, 2026-11-21). Every ending is a CAS, so a re-run is safe, and the sweep skips an expert who has been verified again. A failed gateway read inside an otherwise finished ending is reported once here. The durable path is the payment reconciler, which re-reads orders closed in the last 72 hours and refunds any capture through the late-capture path, keyed on the Razorpay payment id (owner decision, 2026-11-21; built in its own pull request).
7. **Paid requests are kept** (owner decision, 2026-11-21; confirmed again on 2026-11-21 that a paid pending request stays placeable). A request with a captured payment that the expert has not approved yet, such as a bought subscription waiting for its sessions, is not declined; the approval guard below lets it be placed, and its payout follows the normal holds.
8. **What the learner reads is neutral** (owner decision, 2026-11-21). The notice for a closed request or pay link is "<expert> isn't taking bookings right now, so your request was closed. No payment was taken." (`closedRequestNotice` in `lib/booking/closed-sale-copy.ts`), and the pay page reads "This expert isn't taking bookings right now. No payment was taken." Neither says the expert was rejected or gives a reason. When a real gateway order existed for the sale, both end "If you were charged, the money is refunded automatically." instead (owner-confirmed on 2026-11-21), because a capture may have landed; the money copy follows the finance rule that promises no charge only when no order ever existed. A capture recorded on such a closed booking shows the same unpayable page, never the paid redirect.
9. **An unpaid request can no longer be approved for an unverified expert** (owner decision, 2026-11-21). `assertUnpaidApprovalVerified` (`lib/booking/approval-verification.ts`) runs before the approval write in both detail PATCH routes (`app/api/bookings/{consultations,subscriptions}/[id]/route.ts`) and in allocation's `updateEventStatus`, which every allocate route reaches, the organisation's included. It refuses with 409 `EXPERT_NOT_VERIFIED` unless the booking is already `APPROVED` or carries a successful payment. A free request has no payment and is refused too.
10. **The free remedy rebook after a platform incident is left alone.** `rebookIncidentRemedy` (`lib/booking/incident-rebook.ts`) replaces a session the learner already paid for, which is an existing booking the rejection honours.

Review on 2026-11-21 also brought these surfaces in line with decision 3:

- The subscription feasibility read (`GET /api/plans/subscriptions/[id]/feasibility`) applies the by-id read's `canViewPlanDetail`, so it answers 404 to anyone but the expert for a plan they may not open, and no longer reveals the expert's time zone.
- The trial eligibility check without a plan id offers a trial only when a plan the reader can see has one, so `isEligible` agrees with `plansWithTrialEnabled`.
- The planner shows neither the published chip nor a share link until the owner's standing has loaded (`useOfferingsListed` in `hooks/useVerificationStatus.ts`), so "Published" never flashes before "Live once verified".
- The by-id webinar and class plan responses are shared for at most 60 seconds with no stale window, so a plan a reject hides stops being served within a minute (owner decision, 2026-11-21; `planDetailResponse` in `lib/api/plans/detail-response.ts`).

- Trial eligibility (`GET /api/trials/check-eligibility`) lists and reads plans with the public filter for anyone but the owner.
- The sitemap (`listIndexableEventPlanIds`), the waitlist's seat-opened invitations and joins (`eventLive` in `lib/booking/event-waitlist.ts`), and the `/explore/programs` hero counts (`getProgramCounts`) use the same filter.
- `purgeExpertSurfaces` also purges the `programs` cache.
- The webinar and class plan lists show an unverified owner their own plans and answer every expert-filtered read `private, no-store`, as the 1:1 lists do.
- The shared unfiltered 1:1 and subscription lists key their CDN entry on every query they read.
- A hidden plan's checkout says "This plan isn't available to book right now."
- An unverified expert's planner marks a published offering "Live once verified" and offers no share link.
- An unpaid group seat on the expert's own webinar or class is released at the reject, with the event and its paid seats kept.

### What a reject does to each state

| State before the reject                                                                                                                                                         | After                                                                                                                                          | Money                                                                                                                                                          | Learner's notice                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1:1 or subscription `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT`, nothing captured                                                                                                                | `EXPIRED` through `lapseApprovedRequest` (reason `EXPERT_UNAVAILABLE`); tentative holds released                                               | every `PENDING` order expired by CAS through `expirePendingPaymentsInTx`; credit, code use and engagement returned; real gateway orders cancelled after commit | the closed-request notice (`closedRequestNotice`, staged through `stageRequestEndedNotice`) |
| 1:1 or subscription `PENDING`, nothing captured (a request, a free request, or a checkout still open)                                                                           | `REJECTED` (CAS from `PENDING`, never on a booking being moved); holds, seats and open proposals released through `releaseDeclinedRequestHold` | same order expiry                                                                                                                                              | the same closed-request notice                                                              |
| Trial `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, not paid                                                                                                                                              | `CANCELLED` through `transitionTrial`; its call tombstoned (`softCancelTrialAppointmentInTx`)                                                  | same order expiry                                                                                                                                              | the trial-cancelled bell and email                                                          |
| A `HELD` seat on the expert's own webinar or class, not paid                                                                                                                    | `CANCELLED` through `releaseParticipant`; the event, its sessions and its paid seats stay                                                      | that buyer's open order expired the same way                                                                                                                   | the closed-request notice                                                                   |
| Anything with a `SUCCEEDED` payment (a paid session, a bought plan awaiting its sessions, a paid trial), or an order the gateway reports a live payment on or cannot answer for | unchanged                                                                                                                                      | unchanged; payouts follow the normal holds                                                                                                                     | none                                                                                        |
| A free trial already `SCHEDULED`                                                                                                                                                | unchanged                                                                                                                                      | none                                                                                                                                                           | none                                                                                        |

A capture that lands later on an expired order takes the capture handler's `captured_after_release` arm: the booking stays ended and the money is refunded through `refundBookingPayment`. The pay page (`resolvePayPage`) finds the order dead and the request no longer payable. When the booking's expert is not verified or is deleted it answers with `EXPERT_UNAVAILABLE_MESSAGE` ("This expert isn't taking bookings right now. No payment was taken.") and no "request again" line; otherwise it keeps the general message.

## Evidence

- Marketplaces that sell on a provider's credentials keep that provider unlisted and unbookable until a person approves them: Practo ([guiding principles](https://www.practo.com/company/guiding-principles)), Preply ([register to teach](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4175138-register-to-teach-on-preply)), Clarity.fm ([expert FAQ](https://clarity.fm/help/topics/5/expert-faqs)), Urban Company (background checks at onboarding), and Airbnb, whose listing "won't be published until you finish" verification ([help](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1237)).
- Open listing with a badge (Topmate, UrbanPro) is the long-tail model, and even Topmate gates legal, medical, financial and mental-health experts ([expert policies](https://topmate.io/expert-policies)).
- The RBI's 2025 Master Direction on payment aggregators requires merchant due diligence and that a marketplace "does not accept payments for a seller not onboarded" ([RBI](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896), paragraph 13).
- Platforms that sell coaching under their own brand vet every coach (BetterUp; CoachHub, at about 5% acceptance). Organisation-vouched publishing exists only on tools for an organisation's own people (LinkedIn Learning, Udemy Business, mentoring platforms). Familiarise sells sessions under its own name and collects and pays out the money, so verify-first fits; an organisation that needs speed gets faster review of the experts it invites.
- Not verified in the research: the Fiverr, MentorCruise, Udemy and Upwork pages returned 403 and are cited from search summaries, and whether the e-commerce "fall-back liability" amendment was notified.

## Consequences

- Seeded and test experts must be `VERIFIED` to take bookings; the database tier's `seedHost` now seeds them so.
- The reject's transaction grows with the expert's open sales: each ending costs about ten statements, so an expert with dozens of unpaid requests makes a long moderation action, inside its 30-second budget.
- The topic chips' program counts (`GET /api/topics?withProgramCount=true`) and the language facet on `/explore/programs` still count every plan; they carry counts and names, not plans.
