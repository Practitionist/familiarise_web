# ADR: Trial and public booking surfaces — free checkout, private strikes, free cancels without a refund record, and two trial lengths

- **Status**: Accepted (owner decisions 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13). Decisions 1 to 14 are **built** on the branch that carries this record (`feat/train2-single-time-picker`); decisions 10 to 14 are the time pickers.
- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [The booking follow-up train](2026-11-12-booking-follow-up-train.md) (decisions 2, 8 and 9), [Scheduling surfaces and feasibility](2026-11-09-scheduling-surfaces-and-feasibility.md) (decision 17), [Cancellation flow](../booking/08-cancellation-flow.md), [Trial sessions](../booking/09-trials.md), [Checkout and payment integration](../booking/10-checkout-payment-integration.md), the tracking issue [#2720](https://github.com/Practitionist/familiarise_web/issues/2720) (train 2)

## Context

The review of the scheduling surfaces pull request found a family of older defects on the surfaces a buyer meets first: the expert's public profile, the checkout page and the cancel of a free booking. A ₹0 checkout asked the buyer to "Pay with Razorpay", offered an organisation payer that was then refused, and promised a refund in 5–7 working days. Checkout printed the plan level as a database value ("BEGINNER") and the time in the device's zone with seconds. The profile printed "Reliability: N expert cancellations or no-shows" to every visitor, although the owner had decided that strikes are private. Cancelling a free booking wrote a zero-rupee `Refund` row, sent a "refund on its way, ₹0.00" email and told the buyer their referral credits were restored when none had been spent. This record holds what the first half of the train 2 pull request built about those surfaces, with the options the owner turned down.

## Worked example

Hannah Anderson offers a free 30-minute consultation. Charlotte Brown books Monday 10:00 IST. Olivia Anderson books Hannah's ₹500 consultation the same day and pays it entirely with ₹590 of referral credit (₹500 plus 18 % GST), so her payment is also ₹0 at the gateway.

- **Checkout.** Charlotte's checkout shows the level as "All levels", the time as "10:00 AM - 10:30 AM (IST)" in her profile zone, and one "Confirm booking" button. It shows no payer choice, no billing state, no referral-credit switch, no card block and no refund-timing sentence, and its "Includes" row appears only if Hannah listed something. Olivia's checkout is a paid one: she turns on her credits, so the payer, billing-state and payment blocks stay.
- **Hannah cancels both on Sunday.** Her cancel dialog says, before she confirms, "Cancelling a confirmed booking, free or paid, is recorded on your reliability record. Only you and our team see it." For Charlotte it adds "Nothing was paid for this, so there is nothing to refund." Each cancel records one strike.
- **What is written and sent.** Charlotte's cancel writes no `Refund` row, no refund marker, no refund bell and no refund email; her cancellation email has no refund line. Olivia's cancel writes the zero-rupee `Refund` row that records the restoration of her ₹590 credit, and she is told her credits are back.
- **Where the strikes show.** Hannah's Settings → Booking page shows "2 recorded in the last 90 days" with each cause and date. Her public profile shows nothing about them.

## Decisions

1. **Checkout shows labels and the buyer's own zone** (decided, built). Every plan checkout prints the level through `planLevelLabel` (`lib/labels/plan-labels.ts`). The consultation checkout prints its date and its short start and end times with `formatInViewerZone` and the short zone name from `zoneLabel` (`lib/time/viewer-zone.ts`), in the zone `useViewerZone` resolves from the buyer's profile; the subscription checkout prints its start and first-cycle dates the same way. **Enforced by** the four adapters under `app/checkout/plans/adapters/`; `__tests__/labels/no-raw-enums-in-customer-copy.test.ts` pins that none of them interpolates the level or calls `toLocaleDateString` or `toLocaleTimeString`.

2. **A free order confirms with one button** (decided, built).
   - **When an order is free.** The server learns that an order costs nothing only when it answers the click (`isZeroAmountOrder` in `lib/payments/checkout/zero-amount.ts`, reported as `isZeroAmountPayment`). The page therefore derives it from the same priced total it shows: the total is zero and no referral credit was applied (`isFree` in `PlanCheckoutShell`). The server stays the authority; if it answered with a gateway order, the same button would open the gateway.
   - **What changes.** One "Confirm booking" button replaces the payment card. The payer selector, the billing-state select, the referral-credit switch and the plan footer (the refund-timing note, the cancellation ladder, the FX and EMI hints) are hidden, and the request carries no organisation and no credits. A paid order keeps the existing rules: choosing an organisation still forces credits off.
   - **"Includes".** The row lists what the expert entered (`whatsIncluded`) and facts the plan's own fields state (recording, certificate, session counts), through `planIncludes` (`app/checkout/plans/adapters/shared.ts`), and is hidden when empty. The invented lines ("Personalized guidance", "Q&A session", "Learning materials") are gone.
   - **Enforced by** `app/checkout/plans/components/PlanCheckoutShell.tsx`; `__tests__/payments/checkout-shell-smoke.test.tsx` pins a free consultation.
   - **Options considered.** Asking the server whether the order is free before the click was rejected: it would be a second pricing read of the same numbers the page already shows. Keeping the payer selector on a free order was rejected because the organisation option is refused for a ₹0 order anyway.

3. **The pay button answers its first click** (decided, built). The cause, found in code: `handleCheckout` in `app/checkout/components/RazorpayCheckout.tsx` awaited the page's pre-check (`onBeforeCheckout`: the billing-state check and, for a webinar, a fresh seat read with `cache: "no-store"`) before it showed any busy state, and nothing stopped a second click from starting a second run. A slow pre-check therefore looked like an ignored click, and a missing billing state showed its error in the other column. The button is now busy from the click, a ref guards re-entry, and a blocked billing state takes focus (`useBillingState` in `app/checkout/components/BillingStateSelect.tsx`). **Enforced by** those two files; `__tests__/payments/checkout-dismiss-cancel.test.tsx` pins the busy state and the single run.

4. **The trial checkout card sits centred under the site header** (decided, built). `/checkout/plans/trial/[trialId]` spans both columns of the checkout grid (`app/checkout/plans/layout.tsx`), and `isChromeHidden` keeps the site header and footer on that route while the two-column checkouts keep their own shell (`CHROME_SHOWN_PREFIXES` in `lib/navigation/public-chrome.ts`).

5. **Strikes are private** (decided 2026-11-12, built).
   - The public profile no longer reads or prints strikes: the reliability line in `ProfileHeader`, its threading through the profile page and `ExpertProfileClient`, and `showsReliabilityNotice` are deleted. The public readers (`lib/data/consultant-detail.ts`, `lib/data/consultant-public.ts`) carry no strike field.
   - The expert sees their own record on Settings → Booking (`ReliabilityCard`): the count in the last 90 days and the ten latest strikes with their cause and date, read by `readOwnStrikes` (`lib/booking/expert-reliability.ts`) through the owner-only `GET /api/user/consultants/[id]/strikes`. Strikes on a session inside a declared incident are dropped there as they are everywhere.
   - Staff keep `ExpertStrikesCard`; an expert at `EXPERT_STRIKE_THRESHOLD` (3 in 90 days) is marked "needs review".
   - **Options considered** (from decision 17 of the scheduling surfaces record). Softer public wording was rejected. Moving the line into the Reviews section was the owner's first choice and was reversed the same day: reviews and ratings are the public signal.

6. **An expert's cancel of a free confirmed booking records a strike** (decided 2026-11-12, built). The cancel route strikes an expert-side cancel (`isExpertSideCancel`) of a 1:1 booking that has a paid payment or is confirmed (`APPROVED` or `SCHEDULED`, which covers a free request the expert approved with no payment at all), and of a webinar or class with a confirmed seat or a seat held under a SUCCEEDED payment (`hasConfirmedSeat`). A platform cancel through the audited door strikes nobody, and a strike on a session inside a declared incident is still dropped when strikes are read. The expert's cancel dialog says so before the click (`EXPERT_CANCEL_RELIABILITY_LINE` in `lib/booking/expert-reliability-rules.ts`). **Enforced by** `app/api/appointments/[appointmentId]/cancel/route.ts`; `__tests__/payments/cancel-route-refund.test.ts` pins a free booking with no payment. **Options considered.** Dropping the strike for free bookings was rejected (decision 9 of the booking follow-up train).

7. **A genuinely free cancel writes no zero-rupee refund record and sends no refund notice** (decided 2026-11-12, built).
   - **Which bookings.** A payment is free when it carries a `free_` intent, a zero amount and a zero list price (`Payment.originalAmount`, the price before discounts, credits and tax): `isPriceFreePayment` in `lib/payments/funding-rail.ts`. A credit-funded booking keeps today's record, because its refund is the restoration of the credits. A booking discounted to zero keeps it too, because its refund door nets the earnings recorded on the list price.
   - **What each door did before, and does now.**

     | Door                                                                                                            | Before                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Now                                                                                                                                                  |
     | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
     | 1:1 cancel, buyer or expert (`cancelRefundOwed` in the cancel route)                                            | The quote read the free payment as credit-funded and restored it "in full": a refund marker, then a ₹0 `SUCCEEDED` `Refund` (`source: free-credit`), the seat moved to `REFUNDED`, a refund bell and a `REFUND_PROCESSED` email for ₹0.00, and "Your credits have been restored." in the cancellation email | No marker and no `Refund`; the answer is `NOTHING_REFUNDABLE` (`POLICY_ZERO` at a 0 % tier, as before) and the cancellation email has no refund line |
     | Whole-event cancel (`planEventSeats` in `lib/payments/operations/event-refunds.ts`)                             | Every free seat got a marker and a ₹0 `Refund` with the same notices; its attendee was emailed "Your credits have been restored."                                                                                                                                                                           | A free seat is settled with nothing owed; its attendee is in the free group of the cancellation email (`eventAttendeesByRail`)                       |
     | Seat leave and organiser removal (`seatRefundOwed` in `lib/booking/seat-leave.ts`, `refundRemovedAttendeeSeat`) | A marker, a ₹0 `Refund` and the refund notices                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | No marker; the removal answers zero refunded on no rail                                                                                              |
     | Trial cancel (`DELETE /api/trials/[trialId]`)                                                                   | A free trial carries no payment, so nothing was written or sent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Unchanged; the trial cancel preview now also answers `freeBooking`                                                                                   |

   - **Readers checked before building.** Earnings release (`scripts/earnings/release-earnings.ts`) holds an earning back only for a PENDING refund or a refund marker, so a missing SUCCEEDED ₹0 row changes nothing. On 2026-11-13 the shared database held 175 `free_` payments, all with a zero list price: none had a ledger transaction or a credit note, and every one of their ₹0 earnings rows was still PENDING whether or not a ₹0 refund existed, so the refund door moved no money and no ledger line for them. The consumer-invoice healer skips a zero-amount payment with no credit; referral vesting needs a completed session, which a free cancel restoring "in full" never had. The buyer's payments list now shows a cancelled free booking as paid ₹0 rather than refunded.
   - **Enforced by** the doors above; `__tests__/payments/cancel-route-refund.test.ts` pins the 1:1 door. The expert's cancel screen and the trial cancel preview read the `freeBooking` flag the way the learner's dialog does.
   - **Options considered.** Keeping the records and fixing only the wording was rejected (decision 9 of the booking follow-up train).

8. **Archived and organisation-only events leave the expert's public list** (decided, built). `getConsultantDetail` reads webinar and class plans through `eventPlanDiscoverableWhere()` (`lib/api/plans/visibility.ts`), as the explore readers do. Each class plan carries its next scheduled batch (no new column), so its card says "Starts 24 Nov" through `programFacts` (`lib/explore/program-facts.ts`). **Enforced by** `lib/data/consultant-detail.ts`; `__tests__/explore/expert-profile-public-plans.test.ts` pins the filter.

9. **A trial is 30 or 60 minutes** (owner decision 2026-11-13, built). `TRIAL_DURATION_MINUTES` (`schemas/trial-length.ts`) is checked on write by `SubscriptionPlanSchema`, which the plans POST and PUT routes parse, with the message "A trial is 30 or 60 minutes." The editor offers two radio buttons (`minutesChoice` in `components/offerings/editor/OfferingFields.tsx`). A plan saved earlier with another length (45 minutes exists in practice data) keeps reading and booking at it, because booking reads the stored value; the editor names it and asks for 30 or 60 on the next save. **Options considered.** Any multiple of 30 up to 120 was not chosen; the owner chose the two lengths trials are sold at.

## Time pickers

10. **One single-time picker replaces the pill wall, the trial pickers and the One-time dialog's calendar** (owner decision 2026-11-12, built).
    - **What was built.** `SingleTimePicker` (`components/scheduling/SingleTimePicker.tsx`) picks one time for one session: a month grid (`MonthGrid`, exported for a date-only choice) and the chosen day's times, two panes from the `md` breakpoint and stacked below it, in a dark and a light theme from one token map (`single-time-theme.ts`). It is controlled; each caller owns its button. It reads only the shared month read (`useAvailabilityMonth`, which gained the moving event and the one-shot cache bypass) and the shared window rules (`windowsForDay`, `isBookableWindow`). The option states (`free`, `taken`, `contended`, `outside-hours`, `too-soon`) and the month summary come from `lib/scheduling/single-session-options.ts`; each caller passes its writer's purpose, so the lead is `leadMsFor` or `eventLeadMs` and never restated. A pick that a later read shows gone is cleared and the pane says "That time was just taken — pick another."
    - **Who uses it.** Dark, buyer: the One-time dialog (`ConsultationPricingToggle`) and the trial step. Light, buyer: the learner's trial reschedule, the expert's "Propose a new trial time" and the organisation's manual allocation (`AllocateOrgBookingDialog`). Light, host: the webinar start and the class's first session in the editor (`SessionTimeField`), the re-time dialog and the class make-up. The class editor's `datetime-local` start and `SingleSessionPicker` are deleted.
    - **Host and buyer.** A host sees free and booked ("Booked") times and reveals the remaining half-hour starts, "Outside your hours", with a switch. A buyer sees free times, and the times already taken behind a disclosure (decision 14). A buyer's pick carries the availability row it was cut from, which checkout's URL and the request door's body name (`lib/booking/picked-time.ts`).
    - **Enforced by** the components above; `__tests__/scheduling/single-session-options.test.ts` and `__tests__/scheduling/single-time-picker.test.tsx` pin the states, the taken list, the keyboard grid and the host switch; `__tests__/booking/picked-time-contract.test.ts` pins that a weekly and a custom pick parse at checkout, at the request door and at the trial door.
    - **Options considered** (from decision 2 of the booking follow-up train). Replacing only the pill wall, keeping all eight pickers, building it inside the scheduling surfaces pull request, listing every half hour with a label, and listing only free times for a host were rejected there; one matching picker per host surface was superseded the same day.

11. **Trial time-picking is step two of the mentorship dialog** (owner decision 2026-11-12, built). "Book Trial" opens the dark mentorship dialog on its trial step (`MentorshipTrialStep`), with Back to the start-date step; the start-date step offers "Book Trial" too and picks its date on `MonthGrid` instead of a native date input. The step carries the eligibility check, `POST /api/trials` and `invalidateAvailability` from the deleted `TrialBookingModal`. **Options considered.** Keeping the separate light modal was rejected (issue train 2): it overflowed on a phone and named no zone.

12. **"Book Now" books its own card's plan** (built). `handleBookNowClick` makes the card's plan the active one and opens that card's dialog, so the request and the stashed purchase intent carry it. `__tests__/explore/selection-just-taken.test.tsx` pins the second card.

13. **A held 1:1 time on an instant-booking expert is taken, never an unpaid request** (owner decision 2026-11-13, built). `consultationCtaFor` reads `bookingMode` alone, and `POST /api/scheduling/request-for-approval` refuses an expert whose `bookingMode` is not `REQUEST` with 409 `REQUEST_NOT_OFFERED` ("This expert takes instant bookings. Pick a free time and continue to checkout."), after the pause check. Before building, every caller of the door and every writer of an unpaid `PENDING` consultation was checked: the explore dialog is the door's only caller ("Request again" links lead back to it), and checkout and the legacy capture creator write paid holds. **Enforced by** the route; `__tests__/booking-algorithm/consultant-request-gate.test.ts` pins it. **Options considered.** Keeping the request arm for a contended time was rejected: it turned a contended instant booking into an unpaid request the expert then had to approve.

14. **A buyer can wait on any taken time** (owner decision 2026-11-13, built; replaces "Notify me" only after a refusal).
    - **What the buyer sees.** Free times show by default. Below them, "Show N taken times" (open: "Hide taken times", a native button with `aria-expanded`) lists the day's taken times greyed, in time order, each with "Notify me" (`NotifyWhenFreeButton`). A time the learner already waits on reads "Waiting" with "Withdraw". The cap of three stays the server's rule (`BACKUP_INTEREST_CAP`); its refusal shows under the button, and the list carries "You can wait on up to 3 times." "Taken" means a lattice time inside the expert's published hours that is booked or held (`takenTimes` in `lib/scheduling/single-session-options.ts`); a time that is too soon, past or outside hours is not listed. It applies to instant-booking and review-first experts alike; the host's picker is unchanged.
    - **Signed out, and refused.** A signed-out visitor's "Notify me" stashes the time (`waitOn` in `utils/purchase-intent.ts`), signs in and reopens the dialog on that day with the list open. When the request door refuses a pick as held, or a poll shows it taken, the pick clears, "That time was just taken — pick another." shows and the list opens.
    - **Nothing changes on the server.** `POST /api/scheduling/backup-interest` and `registerBackupInterest` already accept any future window. Waiters already hear when a time frees: the cancel route stages notices for a cancelled 1:1, confirmed or not (`stageNoticesForAppointmentHolds` with `includeConfirmed`), and the abandoned-checkout sweep, order reuse, abandon and request lapses do it for a lapsed hold.
    - **Enforced by** `components/scheduling/SingleTimePicker.tsx` and `components/booking/NotifyWhenFreeButton.tsx`; `__tests__/scheduling/single-time-picker.test.tsx`, `__tests__/explore/selection-just-taken.test.tsx` and `__tests__/growth/notify-toggle-wrappers.test.tsx` pin the disclosure, the refusal and the button's states.
    - **Options not chosen.** Listing only held times, with a new grid field telling a hold from a confirmed booking: rejected, a confirmed booking frees on a cancel too. An expert setting that lets buyers request a taken time: rejected, a request on a taken time is a booking the expert would have to refuse. An expert opt-out of the list: rejected, waiting reserves nothing. Building it as before (Notify only after a refusal): superseded by this decision.

## Honest limits

- **`TrialScheduleCalendar` remains** behind the expert's "accept a trial" dialog (`TrialAcceptDialog`), which is being removed in a separate pull request; the file goes with it.
- **The editor's picker does not free the event's own session.** Editing a published webinar's start shows its own session as booked, as the old pill wall did.
- **Leaving a free seat still quotes "refunds ₹0.00".** The seat-leave preview (`quoteSeatLeave`) was not changed; the leave itself writes nothing.
- **An all-free event reports "already refunded" to the admin refund door.** `refundWholeEventPayments` counts a free seat as settled, so `alreadyRefunded` is true when every seat is free.
- **A trial with a `free_` ₹0 payment and a zero list price** would still restore through the trial door (`lib/trials/cancellation.ts` was not changed). It cannot occur today: a free trial carries no payment.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **The public reliability line** (`ProfileHeader`'s "Reliability: N expert cancellations or no-shows", `showsReliabilityNotice`). Deleted; strikes show to the expert and to staff only.
- **Striking only a paid booking** (`paidPayment !== null` or a paid live seat). Replaced by decision 6.
- **A ₹0 "credit restoration" for a booking that spent no credit.** Replaced by decision 7.
- **The "Pay with Razorpay" card and the payer, billing and refund blocks on a ₹0 checkout, and the invented "Includes" lines.** Replaced by decision 2.
- **Trial lengths from 15 to 120 minutes in 15-minute steps.** Replaced by decision 9.
- **`SingleSessionPicker`, `TrialSlotPicker`, `TrialBookingModal`, the One-time dialog's own calendar and `SlotList`, and the editor's and the organisation's `datetime-local` boxes.** Replaced by decisions 10 and 11.
- **The INSTANT "contended → request" arm of `consultationCtaFor`.** Replaced by decision 13.
- **"Notify me" only after a refusal, and the "N times already taken" line** (`takenTimesLine`). Replaced by decision 14.
