# ADR: Job freshness is derived from the schedule, and an external monitor polls it

- **Status**: Accepted on 2026-11-02 (option 1 of issue #2546). Ships in the pull request that adds this record. The Better Stack monitor is an owner step.
- **Date**: 2026-11-02
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [Better Stack setup guide](../maintenance/00-betterstack-setup.md#independent-capability-freshness-probe) (the runbook), [Job machinery catalog](../maintenance/11-job-machinery-catalog.md), [Observability system design](../observability/00-system-design.md), [The ticker skips targets that have no work](2026-11-01-ticker-skip-idle-targets.md), [Observability gaps and launch posture](2026-11-01-observability-gaps-and-launch-posture.md), [Money go-live checklist](../finance/70-operations/07-go-live-checklist.md)

## Context: absence of a signal was not a signal

Every alert the platform had fired when something ran and failed. Nothing fired when something did not run. Fourteen scheduled housekeeping, compliance and money jobs stopped for two to six weeks in October 2026 with no alert:

- **They ran only on GitHub Actions**, whose budget was exhausted from about 10-17. GitHub refused the jobs, so nothing ran, nothing was billed, and `notify-ops-failure.sh` was never reached.
- **The move to the Netlify ticker reached production late.** It merged on 10-31 and shipped on 11-02, after a held release and an out-of-memory production build.
- **The absence probe did not cover them.** `/api/health/jobs` read a hand-maintained list of about 40 completion SLOs in `lib/cron/operational-health.ts`. None of the daily housekeeping or compliance jobs (`cleanup-auth-tokens`, `prune-audit-logs`, `consent-retention-sweeper`, `databreach-deadline-alerts` and others) were on it. The ticker's failure streak judges only targets the ticker calls, so a job that never starts leaves no row and no signal.

A refused CI job, a target removed from the schedule, a job renamed so the probe watches the old name, a host switched off and a deploy that never lands all look like "all quiet".

## Decision

1. **Coverage by construction.** `OPERATIONAL_COMPLETION_REQUIREMENTS` is computed from `lib/cron/schedule.ts`: one requirement for every target in `TARGETS` whose core writes a run row (`jobNameFor(target) !== null`), under that run-row name. _Superseded in part by [2026-11-02-reliable-jobs-digest-and-release-lag.md](2026-11-02-reliable-jobs-digest-and-release-lag.md): every target now writes a run row, `jobNameFor` never returns `null`, and `checkJobCoverage` enforces it at build time._ Adding a target adds its absence check; renaming a run row through `jobNameFor` moves the check with it. The hand list is deleted.
2. **The budget comes from the slot gap.** `maxSlotGapMinutes(target)` returns the longest gap between two consecutive due slots, read from the target's interval, offset and calendar window over an 800-day span (two of every quarterly window). Then `maxAge = gap + min(gap, 6 h) + 30 min`: one missed slot is tolerated, but never more than six hours of one, and 30 minutes cover a late tick, a background finish and the run's own duration. A windowed job gets its real calendar gap: the weekly payout batch about 7 days, the monthly accruals about 30 days, the quarterly TDS draft about 92 days. A test walks `dueTargets()` tick by tick over nine months and requires the computed gap to equal the observed one for every target.
3. **Tiers by a small explicit set.** `WATCHED_TARGETS` names the eleven housekeeping targets (job-history, event and audit-log pruning, webhook archiving, onboarding-draft and collaborator-invite expiry, unverified-user purge, verification sweep, document and digital-product storage reconciles, the admin run-request backstop). A late run of one of those only delays cleanup, so it is `watched`: the console shows it red and the probe neither lists it nor fails on it. Every other target is money, compliance or user-facing and is `paging`. A new target pages by default; demoting it is a deliberate edit with a reason.
4. **Evidence that a job ran.** For each paging job the probe reads, only as far back as that job's own budget, the newest `COMPLETED` row, `FAILED` row with outcome `partial`, idle skip on a tick claim row, or config skip (`CANCELLED`, outcome `skipped`, reason ending " not configured"). A config skip counts because the job was scheduled and reached; the missing setting is a configuration finding, listed per job as `lastConfigSkipAt` and in `configSkips`, not an absence. Without this, the three GSTIN jobs that skip while `PLATFORM_GSTIN` is unset would hold the probe at 503 until GST registration.
5. **One external dead-man's switch.** A Better Stack uptime monitor outside the app polls `https://familiarisenow.com/api/health/jobs` every 5 minutes with `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`, alerting on any non-2xx status, with the keyword `"healthy":false` as a belt. It pages when a job goes stale, when the ticker stops, when a deploy breaks the route and when the app is down, even when Sentry and Netlify are quiet. It costs nothing on the free Better Stack plan. The owner creates it from the runbook.
6. **Status semantics are kept.** The route already answered 503 when a paging job was overdue, 401 for a wrong bearer and 503 when history or the environment label is missing. That is what a plain uptime monitor needs, so the codes are unchanged: 200 means every paging job ran within budget.
7. **No overrides today.** No target needs a per-job exception. Two targets are not judged because their cores write no run row: `sentry-ingest-canary` is itself an out-of-band alarm (email and Better Stack), and `stream-control-plane-drift` reports drift through its own alert. _Superseded in part by [2026-11-02-reliable-jobs-digest-and-release-lag.md](2026-11-02-reliable-jobs-digest-and-release-lag.md): both now take a fail-open lease, write run rows and are judged as paging targets; the "no overrides" rule stands._ An override is allowed only for a job whose healthy run can leave no evidence, with a one-line reason.

## Consequences

- **The console uses the same budget.** The System Jobs Run metrics card (`assessMissedWindow` in `lib/backoffice/job-run-metrics.ts`) judges every ticker job by `freshnessBudgetMinutes` plus, for a windowed job, its latest due window. Its separate "ticker interval" rule (two slots plus a tick) is deleted.
- **Sixteen money jobs now page.** They were `watched` pending seven clean production days. Production rows on 2026-11-02 showed every one of them completing inside its new budget.
- **Budgets changed.** The old 15-minute paging budgets were 45 minutes and are now 60; the Stream lifecycle jobs moved from 27 to 28.5 hours; retained-order invoice recovery from 12 to 8.5 hours. The generated [cron jobs reference](../maintenance/04-cron-jobs-reference.md) prints every budget on its "Freshness" line.
- **The probe read is bounded per job.** One `groupBy` with a per-job `startedAt` bound, plus the idle-skip read back to the longest paging budget (the quarterly TDS draft). The newest completed run of every job survives the prune, and other runs are kept 180 days, so the longest budget is within retention.
- **First-run expectations at deploy.** A paging job with no production-tagged run inside its budget reads `overdue` until its next slot: `cleanup-auth-tokens` until 03:00 UTC on 2026-11-03, `consent-retention-sweeper` until Sunday 2026-11-08, and `tds-26q-draft-export` until 2027-01-05, because its last run on 2026-10-05 predates environment tagging. The owner can instead run each once from the System Jobs page before enabling the monitor; the TDS draft is idempotent (`alreadyReported`) and the consent sweeper is count-only on the ticker.

## Worked example: a daily job stops

`cleanup-auth-tokens` (target `auth-tokens`) runs once a day at 03:00 UTC. Its longest gap is 1,440 minutes, so its budget is 1,440 + 360 + 30 = 1,830 minutes (30.5 hours). It last finishes at 03:00:20 UTC on 3 November. On 4 November the 03:00 run is refused by the platform and leaves no row. At 09:30:20 UTC on 4 November the run is older than 30.5 hours, `/api/health/jobs` answers 503 with that job `overdue`, and within five minutes the Better Stack monitor raises an incident by email. Under the old hand list this job had no requirement at all and would have stayed silent for weeks.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **A hand-maintained completion-SLO list** (`OPERATIONAL_COMPLETION_REQUIREMENTS` as about 40 literals) with money SLOs parked as `watched` until seven clean production days. It drifted from the schedule and missed every daily housekeeping and compliance job. Do not add a literal `maxAgeMinutes` or a second job list.
- **The console's "two slots plus a tick" rule** for jobs without an SLO. Every ticker job now has a budget.
