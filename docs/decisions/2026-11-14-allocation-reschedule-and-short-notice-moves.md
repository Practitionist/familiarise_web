# ADR: Allocation and reschedule correctness, and agreed short-notice moves

- **Status**: Accepted (owner decisions, 2026-11-14). What the branch `feat/train4-allocation-reschedule` builds is marked **built** below; decision 4 is **planned**.
- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [Rescheduling flow](../booking/07-rescheduling-flow.md), [Booking state machines](../booking/18-state-machines.md), [Booking API reference](../booking/04-api-reference.md), [One target lifecycle per product](2026-11-13-product-lifecycles.md), [Booking follow-up train](2026-11-12-booking-follow-up-train.md) (decision 3), [Scheduling surfaces and feasibility](2026-11-09-scheduling-surfaces-and-feasibility.md)

## Context

A 1:1 booking moves in two ways. The **allocate** door (`PATCH /api/bookings/{consultations,subscriptions}/[id]/allocate`, handled by `handleAllocate` in `lib/scheduling/allocate-route.ts`, and the organisation door `POST /api/organizations/[orgId]/appointments/[appointmentId]/allocate`) places times on a booking. The **reschedule** door (`POST /api/appointments/[appointmentId]/reschedule`) releases a confirmed session and opens the booking's one `RescheduleRequest`; the other side then accepts or declines proposed times (`.../reschedule/respond`), the initiator may withdraw (`.../reschedule/withdraw`), and an unanswered request expires and puts the original time back (`scripts/appointments/expire-reschedule-proposals.ts`, every 15 minutes).

What was verified on `dev` at `f8ed6923f` before building:

- The manual allocate mode re-placed a consultation that already had a confirmed session unless the client sent `initialAllocation: true`. The auto and use-requested modes refused, the expert's own dialog did not send the flag, so a manual allocate deleted the confirmed session and wrote a new one with no question to the learner. The approval's history row carried no actor, and staff or a host-org operator left no operator row.
- An expert could release a paid session with no proposed times ("any time works") and then place it themselves: the allocator closed the request as answered.
- Every move closer than 24 hours, from or to, was refused: `isTooSoonFor("reschedule", ...)` in the route's release loop and its proposed-times check. The allocator re-placed a released session with its ordinary lead (15 minutes once paid), so the expert could put a learner's released session 11 hours ahead, where the learner could then neither move nor return it.
- A consultee proposal auto-confirmed whenever the allocator accepted it, whatever the notice.
- A proposal expired at the earlier of 72 hours and 24 hours before the earliest released session (`computeProposalExpiry` in `lib/booking/reschedule-proposals.ts`), and was refused when that was already past.

## Decisions

1. **Allocate on a 1:1 that already has a confirmed session is refused by the server** with 409 `ALREADY_ALLOCATED` and "This booking already has a time. Use Reschedule to move it.", in every mode and whatever the client sends. The actor is recorded on every approval and placement.
   - **Built.** `mustBeUnscheduled` (`lib/booking/allocation/guards.ts`) arms the confirmed-session guard for every consultation, before the locks and again inside the transaction behind the per-event advisory lock (`guardAllocationInTx`); `assertNoConfirmedSlots` carries the sentence. A consultation whose session is released (a reschedule being placed) has no confirmed session, so the accept and auto-confirm paths pass. A plan (subscription) with confirmed sessions is not re-planned by an allocate: it appends its next cycle (`isSubscriptionTopUp`), so it needs no refusal. The actor rides `AllocationRequest.actorUserId` into `approveByMoney` and the webinar and class transitions (`lib/booking/allocation/event-data.ts`), so the `BookingStatusHistory` row names who placed the times; the allocate route passes the signed-in user, the organisation door its operator, an accept the accepter and an auto-confirm the proposer. Staff write one `OpsActionLog` row (surface `appointments.manage`, action `booking.allocate`) and a host-org operator one `OrgAuditLog` row (`APPOINTMENT_ALLOCATED_FOR_ORG`), both inside the allocation's transaction (`operatorAuditFor` in `lib/scheduling/allocate-route.ts`). Tests: `__tests__/booking-algorithm/initial-allocation-guard.test.ts`, `__tests__/booking/allocate-host-actor.test.ts`.
   - Options not chosen: converting an allocate on a scheduled 1:1 into a reschedule, which would move a session from a door that asks nobody; keeping the silent move.

2. **An expert who moves a paid, confirmed session proposes times and the learner accepts or declines.** After an expert's release with no proposed times, the expert's own placement of that session is refused; with no answer the original time is restored by the existing expiry.
   - **Built.** `resolveConsumedPreferenceRequests` (`lib/booking/allocation/preference.ts`) reads the open requests on the released sessions before it closes any; an expert-initiated request with no times on a booking with a `SUCCEEDED` payment answers 409 `PROPOSE_TIMES_REQUIRED` ("Propose new times so your client can accept one.") and rolls the placement back. The expert's reschedule page no longer offers "Any time works" (`rescheduleConsultantPolicy`). Test: the bare-release case in `__tests__/booking-algorithm/preference-scored-allocation.test.ts`.
   - Options not chosen: tell-only (the expert moves the session and the learner is told), with or without a free exit for the learner.

3. **Agreed moves at any time.** A new time less than 24 hours away always needs the other side's explicit acceptance, and with it a move is allowed until 15 minutes before the session. Cancel tiers, refund amounts, strikes and no-show outcomes are unchanged, and there is no support step.
   - **Built.** The checks that moved from "24 hours or refuse" to "inside 24 hours needs the other side's acceptance":

     | Check                                                                                     | Before                                                                        | Now                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
     | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
     | Release of a session less than 24 hours away (reschedule route)                           | 409 `RESCHEDULE_WINDOW` for everyone                                          | A party to a consultation or plan may release it **with proposed times**; with no times it is 409 `RESCHEDULE_WINDOW` ("This session starts within 24 hours. To move it, propose new times; the other person has to accept one at least 15 minutes before the session starts."). A session whose join window is open, a trial and a platform operator's release keep the 24-hour refusal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
     | A proposed time less than 24 hours away (reschedule route)                                | 400 `PROPOSAL_TOO_SOON` at 24 hours                                           | A party's proposed time must be at least the lead its acceptance needs: 15 minutes, or 2 hours while the booking is unpaid (`leadMsFor("allocate", { settled })`, settlement read in the release transaction); a trial and an operator keep 24 hours.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
     | Auto-confirm of a learner's proposal (`tryAutoConfirmProposal`)                           | Confirmed whenever the allocator accepted it                                  | Never when the released session or any proposed time is less than 24 hours away (`needsOtherSideAcceptance`); the answer is `NEEDS_ACCEPTANCE` and the expert has to accept. Decided again inside the transaction that closes `AUTO_ACCEPTED`, on that transaction's clock, so a lock wait cannot carry a move inside 24 hours unaccepted. 24 hours or more ahead, unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
     | Proposal expiry (`computeProposalExpiry`)                                                 | The earlier of 72 hours and 24 hours before the session; none inside 24 hours | Unchanged with 24 hours or more of notice; a proposal opened inside 24 hours expires when the session's join window opens, 15 minutes before the start (`SHORT_NOTICE_ANSWER_BY_MS`), stored as the request's `expiresAt` when it is created; inside those 15 minutes none can be opened (400 `PROPOSAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`). The expiry job reads that field and is unchanged; the join gate (`resolveMeetingAccess`) and the detail read (`GET /api/appointments/[id]`) end a lapsed proposal and restore the booking the moment it lapses (`settleLapsedProposal` in `lib/booking/reschedule-lapse.ts`), and the appointment lists render it as restored from that moment (`withLapsedMoveRestored`, read-time only), so the original opens to both sides exactly as it would have, early window included. |
     | Accept after the deadline (`acceptProposal`)                                              | Refused with `PROPOSAL_EXPIRED`                                               | Unchanged: a late acceptance is refused, so inside 24 hours nobody accepts once the join window is open.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
     | Placement of a released session without an accepted proposal (allocator, manual and auto) | The allocate lead (15 minutes paid, 2 hours unpaid)                           | 24 hours (`replacementLeadMs`); a manual pick under 24 hours away is 409 `NEEDS_ACCEPTANCE` (`assertReplacementNotice`). An accepted proposal and a first placement keep the allocate lead. The expert's allocate grid paints released sessions with the same 24-hour lead (`allocatePolicy({ replacing: true })`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

     Kept as they were: the live pay-link refusal (`PAY_LINK_LIVE`), the one open request per booking, the reschedule caps, the appointment lock and the plan-week rule. The screens follow in decision 3's UI work: both reschedule pages list sessions inside 24 hours, offer times from 15 minutes ahead, hide "Any time works" for a session inside 24 hours, and say the other person must accept at least 15 minutes before the session starts.

   - **Worked examples.** Priya Anderson has a paid one-hour consultation with Hannah Anderson on Thursday at 18:00 IST.
     - _24 hours or more, learner._ On Tuesday at 10:00 Priya proposes Friday 11:00, inside Hannah's hours with both calendars free. It confirms itself: the session moves to Friday 11:00.
     - _24 hours or more, expert._ On Tuesday at 10:00 Hannah proposes Friday 11:00. Priya sees Accept and Decline with the deadline (Wednesday 18:00, 24 hours before the session); Accept moves it, Decline or silence keeps Thursday 18:00.
     - _Less than 24 hours, learner._ On Thursday at 09:00 Priya proposes Saturday 10:00. It does not confirm itself; Hannah has until Thursday 17:45, when the join window opens, to accept. If she accepts at 17:00 the session moves to Saturday; if she has not answered by 17:45 the proposal expires, Thursday 18:00 stands and both can join from 17:45, and Priya's ordinary cancel tiers apply from there.
     - _Less than 24 hours, expert._ On Thursday at 09:00 Hannah, unwell, proposes Thursday 20:00 or Friday 19:00 (one of them). Priya must accept by 17:45. If she does not, the session stands at 18:00; Hannah who cannot come cancels, which refunds Priya in full and records a strike as today.
     - _A new time under 24 hours away, more than 24 hours before the session._ On Monday at 12:00 Priya proposes Tuesday 09:00 for her Thursday session. Because the new time is less than 24 hours away it waits for Hannah's acceptance.
     - _A re-placed time under 24 hours away._ Priya releases Thursday's session on Monday with "Any time works". Hannah tries to place it on Tuesday 09:00, 21 hours ahead: refused with `NEEDS_ACCEPTANCE`. She places it on Wednesday 12:00 (48 hours ahead) and it is confirmed.
   - Options not chosen: strict 24 hours, which leaves an agreed emergency move to support; agreed moves plus one free expert move a month.
   - **The cut-off (owner, 2026-11-14): decided.** An unanswered short-notice proposal lapses when the session's join window opens, 15 minutes before the start (`SHORT_NOTICE_ANSWER_BY_MS` in `lib/booking/reschedule-proposals.ts`, stored as the request's `expiresAt`). The other side must accept at least 15 minutes before the start, and no proposal can be opened once the join window is open (400 `PROPOSAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`). Example: for a session at 6:00 pm, a move can be proposed and accepted until 5:45 pm; at 5:45 pm an unanswered proposal lapses and the original stands; from 5:45 to 6:00 both can join early, as they could with no proposal. Option not chosen: lapsing at the start time, which keeps the early join window closed while a proposal is open.

4. **The booking stays confirmed while a reschedule is open, built with the job work.** Today a whole-booking release moves the parent to `PENDING` with `requestedAt` refreshed, and the restore moves it back. Keeping the parent confirmed removes that flip and its repairs, but changes the inbox sources, the request caps, `assertNotBeingMoved`, the staff display and four job files under `scripts/appointments/`. **Planned**, with the infra job work (issue #2763); nothing in this branch changes it.

5. **A late same-key request retry replays the original (owner, 2026-11-14).** A request sent again with the same `Idempotency-Key` after the expert has re-placed the requested time replays the original 201 instead of answering 422; the same key with another plan, time or organisation still answers 422 `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`.
   - **Nothing already stored records what the learner submitted.** `Consultation` keeps the plan, the key and the agreed price, but no time. The request's occurrences hold the requested window only until the expert places the session: the allocator's manual and auto modes delete those rows and write new ones (`deleteExistingAppointments` in `lib/booking/allocation/commit/release-tentative.ts`), and a later reschedule replaces them again. `BookingStatusHistory` stores statuses, actor and reason, with no metadata column. `RescheduleRequest` describes moves, not the request. `IdempotencyRecord` is not used by this route, which keys on `Consultation.requestIdempotencyKey`.
   - **Built: one column, `Consultation.requestFingerprint` (`varchar(64)`, nullable, no backfill).** The request door writes the sha-256 of the submitted plan, start, end and organisation (`requestFingerprintOf` in `app/api/scheduling/request-for-approval/route.ts`) in the transaction that creates the request, and the replay compares the retry's fingerprint with it, never with the booking's current times. A fingerprint rather than two timestamps, because the replay only ever needs equality, it also covers the plan and organisation the 422 must catch, and it is the shape `Payment.requestFingerprint` already uses for checkout. A row created before the column has a null fingerprint and keeps today's comparison with its held times. Tests: the stored-request cases in `__tests__/booking-algorithm/consultant-request-gate.test.ts`.
   - Options not chosen: answering 422 after a re-placement (the recommended option, which needs no column); two timestamp columns.
   - **Owner step.** The column is additive, and `db:push` stays blocked while the four enum swaps are carried, so it is applied on its own, as `scripts/ci/check-db-schema-required.ts` prints: `ALTER TABLE "Consultation" ADD COLUMN "requestFingerprint" VARCHAR(64);`, then that check is re-run and a full-row consultation read smoke-tested.

6. **An unanswered request past its deadline is left to the expiry job (owner, 2026-11-15).** A request whose answer deadline has passed keeps reading as it is stored until `expireStaleRequests` (`scripts/appointments/expire-stale-requests.ts`) ends it on its next run; no surface derives the ending at read time and no read ends it.
   - **Worked example.** On Monday at 10:00 Priya sends an unpaid request for a 1:1 to Hannah, whose consultations are review-first. Hannah does not answer. From Wednesday at 10:00, 48 hours later, Priya's list, Home and the booking page still read "Requested" until the job's next run; the job then expires it, and every surface reads "Cancelled" with the existing line "The expert did not answer in 48 hours."
   - Options not chosen: a read-time rule for 1:1 requests only, which would put the 48-hour rule in two places while plans follow 30 days and a paid plan follows a refund path; ending the request on a page view, which writes on a read and, for a paid plan, would start a refund from a page load.
   - **A lapsed move is different.** A move's deadline is the whole rule: once `expiresAt` has passed the original time stands, whatever else is true. So the lists, Home and the Requests inbox derive it at read time (`withLapsedMoveRestored`, with the parent at the request's `originStatus`), the request counts leave it out (`lapsedMoveWhere` in `lib/data/needs-you.ts`), and the detail read, the join gate and the room settle it. A request's deadline is only one of the expiry job's conditions (its plan type, payment, live proposal and released sessions decide the rest), so only the job decides it.

7. **An availability change never moves a booking; the bookings outside the new hours are offered for reschedule or cancel only after the hours are saved (owner, 2026-11-15).** The "Save these hours?" dialog lists them with no links; after "Save anyway" succeeds it says "Hours saved" and lists them again with a Reschedule and a Cancel link each (`AvailabilitySaveConfirm`). A link before the save would leave the page with the new hours unsaved (the page has no unsaved-changes guard), and the reschedule picker reads the saved hours, so it would still show the old ones.
   - Options not chosen: links that save and then navigate, which turn one click into a save the expert did not confirm; a list with no links at all, which leaves the expert to find each booking again.

## The other corrections in this branch

- **Restore keeps an unpaid hold tentative.** The release records each released session's tentativeness in `RescheduleRequest.releasedSessions` (`tentative: true`, `releasedSessionsOf`), and `restoreRescheduledBooking` writes it back: an unpaid hold released and withdrawn, declined or expired returns tentative, a confirmed session returns firm. A request opened before the field existed restores firm, as before.
- **An expert's proposal is checked when it is made.** After the release commits, the route asks the allocator to check the proposed times exactly as the learner's Accept would (`checkOnly`), including the plan week and the expert's own hours; a refusal withdraws the request, so the booking keeps its time, and answers 409 with the reason.
- **Checkout idempotency.** The request door (`POST /api/scheduling/request-for-approval`) answers 422 `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` when a reused `Idempotency-Key` carries another plan or time, instead of replaying the first request. Checkout (`POST /api/checkout`) answers a same-key double submit with the first attempt's replay before it answers `SLOT_TAKEN` or a busy account; the lock order, the single confirmation path and the `tx`-only reads are unchanged. A checkout `clientIdempotencyKey` reused with different priced inputs still starts a new attempt, as before.
- **Typed answers.** An archived plan at checkout is 409 `PLAN_ARCHIVED`, a registered business code, so it is not reported as an error. Malformed JSON on the booking, plan, trial, checkout, request and respond routes is 400 `MALFORMED_JSON` from one reader (`readJsonBody` in `lib/api/parse.ts`).
- **Events.** A webinar or class draft with no time cannot be published (400 `EVENT_TIME_INVALID`); the organisation catalog holds webinars to the 30-minute grid (`sittingDurationInHoursSchema`); a class's stored scheduling period starts at its first live session and reaches at least its last after a re-plan or a series move (`followClassSessions`).
- **Reschedule pages** build their title only for the viewer who owns the profile in the URL (`viewerOwnsPersonalProfile`), so a signed-in non-party learns no plan or expert name.
- **Availability under live bookings.** Before saving, the settings form asks the server which upcoming bookings the new hours would newly leave outside (`previewNewlyUncovered`, a read-only arm of the settings PUT) and how many upcoming custom dates a weekly save removes, and shows a confirm that lists each booking; the bookings keep their times. A held booking outside the new hours can still be approved, because the allocator keeps a time the booking already holds for every approver (`heldSlotStarts`), and paying for it confirms the hold without an availability check.
- **Notices and the grid.** Each placement batch of a plan stages its own booked notice (the outbox key names the placed sessions), so weeks two onward are not deduplicated away; a returned (cancelled) session is not painted "This booking".
- **The manual grid and a learner's requested time.** The grid refused every cell of the booking under 24 hours away with "too soon to move", including a pending request's own hold; the hold is now pickable up to the allocate lead, and only a confirmed session of the booking keeps the 24-hour guard.
- **The organisation allocation dialog** passes the learner to the picker, and the availability read falls back to the expert's calendar alone when the server refuses the learner's (403).

## Honest limits

- The acceptance deadline for a short-notice move is the join window's opening (15 minutes before the start), not the start itself, so that the original session stands for the whole join window. While the proposal is open the session is released (tentative); decision 4 keeps it confirmed instead.
- Not run in a browser or against a database; see the pull request for the preview cases.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **`initialAllocation` as the only guard for a scheduled consultation.** The server now decides from the stored sessions.
- **The 24-hour refusal for every 1:1 move.** Inside 24 hours a move needs the other side's acceptance instead.
- **A restore that always wrote a firm session.** It now writes back what the release found.
