# ADR: One target lifecycle per product — request mode enforced, dead states removed

- **Status**: Accepted (owner decisions, 2026-11-13). What the branch `feat/booking-lifecycle-target` builds is marked **built** below; the rest is **planned** for Train 1 or Train 2 of the booking session.
- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Author**: Platform engineering
- **Related**: [Booking state machines](../booking/18-state-machines.md), [Booking lifecycle](../booking/06-booking-lifecycle.md), [Cancellation flow](../booking/08-cancellation-flow.md), [Trials](../booking/09-trials.md), [Subscription demand bounds](2026-11-13-subscription-demand-bounds.md), [Booking follow-up train](2026-11-12-booking-follow-up-train.md) (decision 9), [Tax compliance and pricing levers](2026-11-09-tax-compliance-and-pricing-levers.md)

## Context

The platform sells four booking products: a 1:1 consultation, a multi-week 1:1 plan (a subscription), a webinar or class seat, and a trial of a plan. Each has its own guarded state machine in `lib/booking/transitions.ts`, and the four had drifted apart in ways nobody had decided. Some stored statuses had no writer at all, an expert's "Requests reviewed first" switch was honoured only by the buttons, and the docs described paths the code no longer takes.

What was verified on `dev` at `22dd05834` before building:

- Only a 1:1 can be a request. The learner sends a time unpaid (`POST /api/scheduling/request-for-approval`), the expert has 48 hours (`PENDING_CONSULTATION_EXPIRATION_HOURS`), approval moves it to `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT` and mints a pay link valid 24 hours (`APPROVAL_PAYMENT_EXPIRATION_HOURS`), and the capture confirms it. Two things send a learner down that path: the expert's `ConsultantProfile.bookingMode = REQUEST`, or a contended time under `INSTANT` (`consultationCtaFor` in `lib/booking/booking-mode.ts`).
- `bookingMode` was read only by the booking buttons and the settings page. Nothing in checkout read it, so a direct `POST /api/checkout` bought a 1:1 from a review-first expert without the review.
- No code moves a consultation from `APPROVED` to `SCHEDULED`. The only writer of `AppointmentStatus.SCHEDULED` is the incident rebook (`reopenBooking` in `lib/booking/incident-rebook.ts`), which reopens a `COMPLETED` consultation whose session a declared platform incident voided.
- Nothing sets a webinar or class to `IN_PROGRESS`. The one door that could write it was the `crud-with-plan` create body, which parsed `status` with the whole enum.
- No live writer puts a plan in `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT`: the detail PATCH accepts only `APPROVED` and `REJECTED`, and allocation refuses an unpaid plan with `SUBSCRIPTION_UNPAID`.
- `bookTrialInTx` (`lib/trials/book-trial.ts`) creates a trial `PENDING` and moves it in the same transaction to `SCHEDULED` (free) or `AWAITING_PAYMENT` (paid). The expert's accept of a timeless `PENDING` trial, the decline to `REJECTED` and the 48-hour `TRIAL_UNANSWERED` sweep arm serve legacy rows only.

## How the four products differ today

|                              | 1:1 consultation                                                                                                                         | Plan (subscription)                                                                                         | Webinar or class seat                                                              | Trial                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stored status                | `AppointmentStatus` (`Consultation.status`)                                                                                              | `AppointmentStatus` (`Subscription.status`)                                                                 | `WebinarStatus` / `ClassStatus`, plus one `AppointmentParticipant` per buyer       | `TrialStatus`                                                                    |
| Can be a request             | Yes, when the expert reviews requests first or the time is contended                                                                     | No: paid at checkout                                                                                        | No                                                                                 | No                                                                               |
| Pay window                   | 30 minutes for a direct checkout (`DIRECT_CHECKOUT_HOLD_MS`); 24 hours for an approved request's pay link                                | 30 minutes (checkout hold)                                                                                  | 15 minutes per seat (`SEAT_CHECKOUT_HOLD_MS`)                                      | Until 15 minutes before the start (`payWindowEnd`)                               |
| Expert's first move          | None for a direct booking; approve or decline a request within 48 hours                                                                  | Place cycle 1 within 48 hours of the capture (`PAID_UNALLOCATED_HOURS`) or the plan expires and is refunded | Publish (`DRAFT → SCHEDULED`)                                                      | None: the learner picks the time                                                 |
| Learner cancels              | Refund ladder (`PLATFORM_DEFAULT_TIERS`): 24 hours or more 100 %, 2 hours or more 50 %, under 2 hours 0 %; frozen in the last 15 minutes | Per tier over the plan's `sessionsTotal` (`lib/booking/plan-refund.ts`)                                     | Full refund outside the host's refund window (default 24 hours), refused inside it | The same ladder as a 1:1                                                         |
| Expert cancels or no-shows   | 100 % refund; an expert no-show is cancelled and refunded by `detect-consultant-no-shows`                                                | The session returns to the plan                                                                             | Every seat is refunded; a class session gets a 14-day make-up window               | 100 % on cancel; a paid trial's expert no-show waits for a human (`NEEDS_HUMAN`) |
| Earnings hold after delivery | 48 hours                                                                                                                                 | 168 hours per cycle                                                                                         | 48 hours (webinar), 24 hours (class)                                               | 168 hours (paid trial)                                                           |

The hold values come from `PAYOUT_CONSTANTS.HOLD_PERIOD_HOURS` through `holdHoursFor` (`lib/payments/payouts/earnings-hold.ts`). Why the products differ where they do (no 50 % tier for events, the three pay windows, the four holds) is not recorded anywhere and is reviewed in Train 5.

## Worked example: a ₹1,000 1:1

Charlotte books a one-hour 1:1 with Hannah listed at ₹1,000 (100,000 paise) while the platform is not GST-registered, so `determineTax` adds no GST and Charlotte pays ₹1,000. The platform fee is a percentage of the list price stamped on `Payment.platformFeeBps` from the approved `PlatformFeeSchedule`; the live rate is a value kept in Notion (Familiarise / Finance and Pricing / Pricing Strategy), and the example uses the code's fallback of 2,000 bps (`FALLBACK_BPS` in `lib/payments/pricing/platform-fee.ts`): a fee of ₹200 and an expert share of ₹800.

| Exit                                              | Charlotte gets back | Hannah earns                                   | The platform keeps                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The session happens, or Charlotte does not show   | ₹0                  | ₹800, released 48 hours after the session ends | ₹200, less the gateway fee                                                                            |
| Charlotte cancels 24 hours or more ahead          | ₹1,000              | ₹0                                             | Nothing, and it pays the gateway fee: about ₹24 at 2.36 %, which Razorpay does not return on a refund |
| Charlotte cancels between 2 and 24 hours ahead    | ₹500                | ₹400                                           | ₹100 less the gateway fee (`applyRefundCascade` reverses the share and the fee pro rata)              |
| Charlotte cancels under 2 hours ahead             | ₹0                  | ₹800                                           | ₹200 less the gateway fee                                                                             |
| Hannah cancels, or never joins (120-minute grace) | ₹1,000              | ₹0                                             | Nothing, and it pays the gateway fee                                                                  |

If Hannah reviews requests first, Charlotte cannot reach checkout: she sends the time as a request, Hannah has 48 hours to approve it, and the pay link then has 24 hours. An approved request that is never paid ends `EXPIRED` and no money moves; a paid one follows the table above.

## Decisions

1. **Lifecycle depth: keep the four guarded state machines and their stored names, and delete the stored values nothing writes before the schema freeze.** Every screen will read the seven shared words (Requested, Awaiting payment, Confirmed or Registered for a seat, Being moved, Completed, Cancelled, Refunded) from one function. Options not chosen: renaming the stored values to the shared words, which would be a several-hundred-file diff through money code; and changing the screens only, which would freeze the dead values into the schema.
   - **Built in this PR.** `IN_PROGRESS` is removed from `WebinarStatus` and `ClassStatus`, and the `crud-with-plan` create body now accepts only `DRAFT` or `SCHEDULED` (`EventEditorStatusSchema`). The plan's edges into and out of `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT` are removed from `SUBSCRIPTION_ALLOWED_FROM` and the status from `LIVE_SUBSCRIPTION_STATUSES`; the enum value stays because consultations use it. The plan-only readers of that status are deleted: the capture webhook's plan arm, the plan detail PATCH's "approval already in progress" answer, the plan remind and withdraw-approval routes (`remindApprovedPayment` and `withdrawApproval` now serve consultations only), and the Requests inbox's plan "awaiting payment" source and chip, which listed rows whose Remind and Withdraw called those routes.
   - **Built in this PR: the trial's "awaiting the expert" arm.** `bookTrialInTx` creates the trial directly as `SCHEDULED` (free) or `AWAITING_PAYMENT` (paid) with one creation history row, and `TrialStatus.PENDING` and `TrialStatus.REJECTED` are deleted with everything only they reached: the trial PATCH's accept and decline branches, the PENDING entries in `TRIAL_ALLOWED_FROM` and the pay-link payable set, the capture webhook's paid-at-request arm, the `TRIAL_UNANSWERED` arm of `scripts/trials/expire-unpaid-trials.ts` (the unpaid-at-deadline arm stays), `TrialAcceptDialog` with the inbox's "Pick a time" and "Decline" trial actions, and the `TRIAL_REJECTED` email kind. `Trial.status` loses its `PENDING` default, so the creator always states it. No live code path commits a trial in `PENDING`: `bookTrialInTx` is the only trial creator and moved the row out of `PENDING` in the same transaction.
   - **Built in this PR: `AppointmentStatus.SCHEDULED` removed, with a rebook rule per kind (owner, 2026-11-13).** Its only writer was the incident rebook, which reopened a `COMPLETED` consultation to `SCHEDULED`, and the free rebook admitted a consultation only while `SCHEDULED` or `COMPLETED`, so a confirmed (`APPROVED`) consultation whose session an incident voided could not take it. A voided one-to-one is now rebookable while its booking is confirmed or completed, judged per kind by `isRebookableParent` (`lib/booking/incident-keys.ts`): a consultation while `APPROVED` or `COMPLETED`, a trial while `SCHEDULED` or `COMPLETED`. The rebook leaves a consultation `APPROVED` (from `COMPLETED` it writes `APPROVED`; from `APPROVED` it writes nothing), so a rebooked session voided again can be rebooked again. The open remedy row still gates who may rebook (`assertOpenFor` and the refund-marker check), so nothing else widens. Options not chosen: keeping `SCHEDULED` as the reopened state, which keeps a stored value for one rare path; and completed-only, which leaves a confirmed consultation voided before it completed with the refund as its only remedy.
   - **Planned (Train 1).** The one function that maps every stored status to the seven words.

2. **Requests: instant pay-first is the default for every product, and "Requests reviewed first" stays as one expert switch, for 1:1s only, enforced by the checkout server.** Plans and trials get no request: the trial and the expert's 48-hour placement window are the fit check, and the plan-client limit handles flooding. Options not chosen: no requests anywhere (the Topmate shape); and one switch across 1:1s, plans and trials, dropped because a request gate answers "do I want this client", not "I have too many", and it would rebuild the plan request that the subscription demand bounds deleted.
   - **Built in this PR.** `calculateAmountAndValidate` (`lib/payments/checkout/pricing.ts`) refuses a consultation checkout for an expert whose `bookingMode` is `REQUEST` with 409 `REQUEST_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` ("This expert reviews requests before you pay. Send a request for this time instead. You have not been charged."), before any gateway order, on every rail: card, zero-amount and organisation-funded. Plans, trials, webinars and classes are unaffected. An approved request's pay link is priced by `createApprovalPaymentIntent` (`lib/payments/operations/approval-payment.ts`) and never passes through checkout pricing, so it still pays. The client toast is titled "This Expert Reviews Requests First" (`ErrorTypes.REQUEST_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` in the toast map). Tests: `__tests__/booking/request-mode-blocks-direct-checkout.test.ts` and the pay-link pin in `__tests__/payments/approval-payment-appointment-link.test.ts`.

3. **A contended 1:1 time on an instant-booking expert shows as taken with "Notify me"** (the existing `WindowBackupInterest` record), not as an unpaid request, so the request door exists only for review-first experts. Options not chosen: keeping the contended request; deferring it to Train 4. **Planned (Train 2, the single time picker).**

4. **Trial lengths are 30 or 60 minutes only, validated on write.** A plan with another length keeps working until the expert next edits it. Options not chosen: any multiple of 30 minutes; 15-minute steps. **Planned (Train 2).**

5. **Capacity guidance adds no new capacity control.** A contextual "You're in demand" banner on the expert's Requests page and home appears only when load signals fire (open requests against `maxOpenRequests`, the nearest request expiry, paid plans still unplaced, the share of the next two weeks booked), with Pause, Review-first and Limit-plan-clients actions and a 7-day dismiss, plus a one-line description and a live count under each control in Settings → Booking. Options not chosen: descriptions only; deadline alerts now; hover info icons, which are invisible on touch and fail accessibility guidance. **Planned (Train 1).**

6. **Stale docs and headers are fixed now; every other finding is recorded for its train.** **Built in this PR**: the state-machine reference, the lifecycle, cancellation and trial docs, the header comments of `scripts/earnings/release-earnings.ts` and `scripts/trials/expire-unpaid-trials.ts`, and the booking skill.

7. **A genuinely free cancel writes no ₹0 Refund row and sends no refund notice**, while a credit-funded booking keeps its record. This was already decided as decision 9 of the [booking follow-up train](2026-11-12-booking-follow-up-train.md). **Planned (Train 2)**, after tracing today's writes.

## Honest limits

- **This PR merges without a push.** The database is shared with production, whose deployed code still filters on `IN_PROGRESS` and creates trials as `PENDING`, so the four enum swaps (`ClassStatus`, `WebinarStatus`, `RequestStatus`, `TrialStatus`) wait in `knownDivergences` in `prisma/sql/known-drift.json` and the preflight refuses a push. Until the reconcile push that follows the `dev` → `prod` promotion, the database keeps the extra enum values and the `Trial.status` default of `PENDING`. The new code must never meet a row holding a removed value: on 2026-11-13 no row held one (four webinars and three classes moved from `IN_PROGRESS` to `SCHEDULED`, two trials to `CANCELLED`, and no consultation held `SCHEDULED` or plan `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT`), and the released code writes none. See `docs/prisma/README.md`, "The legacy drift currently carried".
- Plan readers of `APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT` that match no rows still exist: the plan arms of the lapsed pay-link and pay-link reminder passes in `scripts/payments/cleanup-abandoned-payments.ts` and of `expirePaymentPendingRequests` in `scripts/appointments/expire-stale-requests.ts` (with `lapseApprovedRequest`'s plan kind), the plan source of the admin and consultee awaiting-payment dashboards, and the plan arm of `buildDeadHoldFilter`, kept in step with its JavaScript twin. Removing them changes the sweeps' result counters and the dashboards' response shapes, so they are left for the infra money-jobs PR.
- The trial-refunded workflow's description in `lib/novu/templates/b2c.ts` still says it fires when a paid trial is declined or not answered within 48 hours; it is left unchanged to avoid template drift before a Novu sync.

## Deprecated & Superseded Approaches

- **A booking mode honoured only by the buttons.** Checkout now refuses a direct 1:1 for a review-first expert.
- **`SCHEDULED` as a request status.** A rebooked consultation used to reopen to `SCHEDULED`, and only a `SCHEDULED` or `COMPLETED` consultation could take the free rebook; it now stays `APPROVED`, and the rebook rule is per kind.
- **`IN_PROGRESS` on webinars and classes.** A published event stays `SCHEDULED` until it completes; the "In progress" learner chip that read it is gone.
- **Plan pay links and their remind and withdraw doors.** A plan is paid at checkout; do not add the plan routes back.
- **A trial that waits on the expert.** The `PENDING` trial, its accept dialog, its decline to `REJECTED` and the 48-hour `TRIAL_UNANSWERED` refund are gone; the learner's pick books the trial.
