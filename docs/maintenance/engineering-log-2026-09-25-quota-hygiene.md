# Engineering log — 2026-09-25 — Redis/Sentry quota hygiene

The single shared Upstash Redis database hit its 500,000-command monthly cap twice in September 2026 (2026-09-21 and again on 2026-09-25), and the second incident's own uncaught Redis-error captures in `readMaintenancePhase()` and `cleanupRoute()`'s catch-all burned roughly the entire monthly Sentry error budget within about 25 hours; issue #1822 closes both gaps with one small PR: a shared throttled-capture helper reused across the three uncaught paths, `CronLockUnavailableError` answering a quiet 503 instead of an unthrottled capture, `reconcile-payment-status` and `reconcile-orphaned-confirmations` moving off the every-tick slot onto the same fifteen-minute cadence as their siblings, a shared cached maintenance-phase read and a cron-scoped health-check cache so a tick's many targets share one Redis command instead of paying for it once per target, a three-minute edge maintenance cache, an `/api/health` field that reports a Redis quota failure by name, and an optional daily Upstash usage alarm at 70%/90% of the plan cap. The owner separately upgraded the shared Upstash database to pay-as-you-go and split preview/branch-deploy traffic onto its own database, so this PR's job was entirely to stop one outage from becoming two.
