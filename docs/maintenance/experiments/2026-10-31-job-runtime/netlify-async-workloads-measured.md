## Verdict for the booking/finance use

- Latency is fine for fire-and-forget work only when warm (about 0.7-1.2 s). Anything delayed, retried, slept or multi-step is quantised to a 60 s tick with 1-2 hops, so a 30 s timer fires 80-124 s later.
- Retry backoff is much coarser than documented and ends in a dead letter after 5 attempts, readable in Blobs.
- Delivery is at-least-once with concurrent duplicate invocations. Steps were not reliably memoised in this lab; any handler must be idempotent by DB key.
- Standard workloads are unusable for work over about 30 s. Use the background type for long steps (14 min step OK).
- `send` is not transactional: an outbox row is needed for both orphan and ghost cases.
- Retries and step transitions are the weak spot; a money flow should not rely on step memoisation.
