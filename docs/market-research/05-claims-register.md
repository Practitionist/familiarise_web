source and confidence.** A decision with no row here is a guess.

## Confidence key

**VERIFIED** read from a primary source (gazette, vendor page, code) ·
**CLAIM** vendor or secondary marketing · **UNVERIFIED** could not confirm ·
**OPEN** genuinely unsettled, with the competing positions listed.

## Maintaining this register

1. **Add a row when a decision is made.** A decision with no row is a guess.
2. **Re-verify rows in section A and F on a schedule**, not on demand — they are
   the fastest-moving.
3. **When a row is wrong, correct it here and note what was wrong.** The
   13-vs-14 November date and the Cube-vs-CubeAPM confusion are both already
   recorded; that is the point.
4. **Do not delete OPEN rows.** An unresolved question that quietly disappears
   becomes a confidently wrong assumption six months later.
