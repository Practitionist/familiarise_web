### 1.3 Buckets, urgency, paging

- Bucket rule (`requests-inbox-state.ts:171-189`): anything with `kind === next-cycle`, a `checkoutHold`, or booking state ≠ REQUESTED → **"Waiting on them"**; REQUESTED rows band by deadline (<24 h → Answer today, <7 d → This week, else Later); `requestedStartPassed` or a null deadline → Answer today. Stranded reschedules are forced to Answer today (`requests-inbox.ts:425-435`).
- Bucket hints: "Under a day left on the hold…", "The next step is the other side's — a payment or a new cycle." (`labels.ts:48-62`).
- Deadline (`requests-inbox.ts:328-346,445-453`): min(request hold clock, first _tentative SCHEDULED_ start − lead time). Hold clock = live pay-link `expiresAt` ?? `requestedAt + 48 h` (consultation) / `+30 d` (subscription) (`requests-inbox.ts:445-449,572-580`). Paid plans awaiting cycle 1 take `nextAction.deadline` (capture + 48 h) (`requests-inbox.ts:420-424`). **The open reschedule proposal's own `expiresAt` is never read.**
- Server "Answer today" chip uses a different predicate from the bucket: `requestedAt` older than hold−24 h OR a tentative SCHEDULED slot within 24 h + lead (`requests-inbox.ts:716-738`).
- Paging: `new`/`old` over a single source → SQL keyset cursor; everything else → in-memory ranked window of 200 with "Showing the 200 most urgent of N — pick a filter to see the rest" (`requests-inbox-state.ts:48-54`, `requests-inbox.ts:1033-1117`, `labels.ts:96-101`). Priority = bucket rank, then money desc, then deadline (`requests-inbox-state.ts:222-226`).
- Deadline sort and cursor sorts render flat (no bucket headers) (`RequestsInbox.tsx:691,836`).

Error card "Couldn't load requests" + Retry (`RequestsInbox.tsx:787-803`). Freshness: "N new · Refresh" badge on tab return (`RequestsInbox.tsx:232-281`).

### 1.5 Server rules the actions hit

- Detail PATCH (`app/api/bookings/consultations/[consultationId]/route.ts:179-600`): consultant-or-privileged only (`lib/booking/request-route-guards.ts:124-185`); APPROVED with payment flips to APPROVED and confirms tentative SCHEDULED rows (`route.ts:374-427`), without payment → APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT after `assertUnpaidApprovalFits` (`route.ts:428-476`); REJECTED → `transitionConsultationRequest` (PENDING|APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT → REJECTED), `releaseDeclinedRequestHold` cancels **every** occurrence on the wrapper, declines open reschedules, expires PENDING payments (`request-route-guards.ts:191-220`), then `stampRejectionRefundOwed` + `refundRejectedRequest` (`route.ts:509-516,577-586`). Both refuse while a live checkout hold exists (`route.ts:363-368,481-487`, `request-cohort.ts:73-86`).
- A whole-booking reschedule moves the parent to PENDING and **resets `requestedAt = now`** (`app/api/appointments/[appointmentId]/reschedule/route.ts:641-680`); a per-session subscription reschedule leaves it APPROVED (`route.ts:654-670`). Released rows become `RESCHEDULED` + `isTentative:true` (`route.ts:578-590`).
- The presentation layer has **no "reschedule pending" state**: a PENDING parent is REQUESTED ("Paid; <consultant> is asked to approve next.") (`lib/dashboard/money-state.ts:477-484`) and its consultant next action is APPROVE_OR_DECLINE (`money-state.ts:713-715`).

### 2.2 Reschedule rules (server)

- Gate: only confirmed `SCHEDULED` rows ≥ `RESCHEDULE_MIN_LEAD_HOURS = 24` ahead may be released by a party; proposed times must be ≥24 h out (`reschedule/route.ts:423-470`, `lib/booking/reschedule-proposals.ts:26-48`).
- One open request per booking (`openForAppointmentId`), 409 `RESCHEDULE_ALREADY_OPEN` (`reschedule/route.ts:472-485`). APPROVED_PENDING_PAYMENT with a live pay link → 409 `PAY_LINK_LIVE` (`…:502-520`).
- Caps: expert `prior.total >= 2` per **booking** → 409 `EXPERT_RESCHEDULE_CAP` "…cancel instead — the client gets a full refund." (`reschedule/route.ts:527-537`, `lib/booking/reschedule-caps.ts:15`); consultee 2 moves per **session ordinal** → 422 `MAX_RESCHEDULES_EXCEEDED` (`reschedule/route.ts:539-552`, `reschedule-caps.ts:44-52`). Withdrawn and restored requests don't count (`lib/booking/released-sessions.ts:42-67`).
- Expiry = min(earliest released start − 24 h, now + 72 h) (`reschedule-proposals.ts:82-104`). Lapse → EXPIRED + **restore** original times (`scripts/appointments/expire-reschedule-proposals.ts:1-21`); if the original time was taken → slots stay released ("stranded"). AUTO_ACCEPTED is only written at creation, when a consultee's times are free (`lib/booking/transitions.ts:577-596`, `reschedule-proposals.ts:114-118`).
  - Doc drift: HLD diagram says `PENDING_REVIEW → AUTO_ACCEPTED : Window lapses without reply -> auto-confirmed` (`docs/start-here/01-booking-system-hld.md:314`) — the code never does that.
- Preference-only ("Any time works") requests are closed ACCEPTED when the allocator places a replacement; a times-bearing proposal answered by placing **different** times on the grid is closed **DECLINED** while the booking moves (`lib/booking/allocation/preference.ts:79-140`).
- Withdraw restores, but if the original time was taken → 409 `ORIGINAL_TIME_TAKEN` "…can't be withdrawn. Agree a new time instead." (`app/api/appointments/[appointmentId]/reschedule/withdraw/route.ts:88-97`, `lib/booking/reschedule-withdraw.ts:99-101`). There is no counter-round (`reschedule-proposals.ts:402`).
- Consultee picker hint: "Green means your consultant is free — choosing one of those confirms straight away. Anything else is sent to them to approve." submit "Request this time" (`components/scheduling/time-picker-policy.ts:180-195`); consultant: "Whatever you propose is sent to the consultee to accept." submit "Propose this time" (`…:198-214`). "Any time works" submits immediately, no confirm (`components/scheduling/TimePicker.tsx:515-525`).
- Detail card (`components/appointments/detail/RescheduleProposalCard.tsx`): headings by role (`:166-181`), "Needs an answer by <time zone>. If nobody answers by then, the request expires and the original times are restored wherever they are still free." (`:224-236`), Accept only when times exist (`:259-270`), Decline confirm "You are only turning down these times — you are not cancelling the booking." (`:289-303`), initiator Withdraw with **no confirm** (`:247-256`).
- Where a proposal surfaces: consultant Today "reschedule replies" = consultee-initiated PENDING_REVIEW on **personal** 1:1 only (`lib/data/consultant-needs-you.ts:59-76`); Requests inbox only when the parent re-entered PENDING (whole-booking); consultee Home lists expert proposals awaiting their answer (`app/dashboard/consultee/[consulteeId]/(features)/home/HomeTab.tsx:383-406`); list rows show "Review reschedule request" (`ConsulteeAppointmentsAdapter.tsx:420-434`); "Needs rescheduling" label only when **no** live row remains (`lib/appointments/normalize.ts:152-161`).

### 2.3 Cancel rules (server + dialog)

- Window: refused from 15 min before a confirmed session to its end (`SESSION_IN_PROGRESS`), and once delivered with nothing owed (`SESSION_ALREADY_DELIVERED`); a RESCHEDULED row counts as owed (`lib/booking/session-window.ts:28-84`).
- Quote: notice = min(hours to next live session, shortest notice the consultee gave on an earlier release of that ordinal) (`lib/booking/cancellation-scope.ts:252-283,340-359`); a RESCHEDULED row is "next live" regardless of whether its original start already passed (`cancellation-scope.ts:268-270`); null/negative notice → `-1` → lowest tier (`lib/payments/operations/cancellation-policy.ts:467-476`). Consultant-initiated → `isConsultantInitiated` tier (full) + `recordExpertStrike` (`cancel/route.ts:835,1086`). Open proposals are declined on cancel (`cancel/route.ts:1143`).
- Quote binding: 409 `REFUND_QUOTE_CHANGED` when `expectedRefundPaise` differs; **a click with no figure is unbound** ("N-1 client", `cancel/route.ts:213-229`).
- One dialog for both sides: `CancelConfirmationDialog` (consultee and consultant: `ConsultantAppointmentsAdapter.tsx:38,468-479`). Preview drives "Checking what you'd get back…", refusal sentence in red (confirm disabled), "You paid ₹X. At this notice the cancellation policy returns Y % of it.", rail sentence (`components/appointments/consultee/CancelConfirmationDialog.tsx:204-300`); preview failure → "We couldn't load the refund estimate just now — the booking's cancellation policy still applies." and **Confirm stays enabled** (`:218-229,355-363`). Buttons "Keep Appointment" / "Yes, Cancel" (`:352-375`).
- List affordances: consultee "Cancel booking"/"Cancel request"/"Cancel trial"/"Leave event" offered for any non-terminal booking without reading the window (`ConsulteeAppointmentsAdapter.tsx:448-511`); consultant "Cancel booking" for any non-trial non-terminal booking (`ConsultantAppointmentsAdapter.tsx:376-433`). The dialog's preview is what reveals the freeze.

### 2.4 Return to plan, re-time, notices

- Return: `returnBlock` CAP_REACHED / RESCHEDULE_OPEN / WINDOW_CLOSED with copy (`session-return-rules.ts:22-43`); confirm "This session goes back into your plan to book again. Your plan is extended by 7 days. You can do this twice per plan." (`:45-46`); helper text "Can't make a session? Return it to your plan at least 24 hours ahead (n of 2 used)." (`components/appointments/detail/SubscriptionReturnControls.tsx:117-119`).
- Re-time: dialog "Everyone enrolled is told and keeps their seat. Anyone who can't make the new time can leave with a full refund." (`components/appointments/RetimeDialog.tsx:98-101`); free leave until min(now + 7 d, new start) (`lib/booking/retime-leave.ts:19,36-39`); attendees get bell + email with `leaveBy` and a `clash` flag (`event-retime.ts:218-266`); consultant toast names the leave deadline (`app/dashboard/consultant/[consultantId]/(features)/appointments/components/useConsultantEventActions.ts:264`). No consultee component reads `retimeBy` (grep: only the consultant toast and API route reference it).

## 4. Design options

### D1 — Reschedule negotiation (who decides the new time)

| Option                     | What changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Pros                                                                            | Cons                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| a. Status quo + guardrails | Fix A-01/A-02 only                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Small                                                                           | Consultant can still override/impose (A-06); withdraw dead end (A-09)                                                                    |
| b. One counter-round       | Counterparty may answer with "Suggest other times" → closes current request `DECLINED(COUNTERED)` and opens a new request from the other side in the same tx (one-open rule kept)                                                        | Matches how people actually negotiate; removes "place different times silently" | Two-sided state to render; cap accounting must not double-count (count releases, not requests — already the case via `releasedSessions`) |
| c. Consent-only placement  | Allocator placement on a booking with an open/preference-only request _from the consultee_ closes it (that's consent: "any time works"); placement after a _consultant_ release creates a consultant proposal that the consultee accepts | Keeps consultee's "any time works" fast path; ends unilateral expert moves      | Consultant "Any time works" becomes "release now, propose later" (2 steps)                                                               |

**Recommendation: b + c.** Consultee "Any time works" stays auto-placing (they consented). Every consultant-originated time goes to the consultee as a proposal (AUTO_ACCEPTED never for consultant, as `mayAutoConfirm` already intends, `reschedule-proposals.ts:114-118`). Counter = close+open in one transaction, linked by `supersedesId` (§5). UI: proposal card gains "Suggest other times" (opens picker prefilled with the released sessions) next to Accept / Keep original.

Screen states for the proposal card (both sides):

1. _They asked, times named:_ "<Name> asked to move Tue 4 Nov 10:00 → Thu 6 Nov 6:00 pm (IST). Answer by Wed 5 Nov 10:00 am." `[Accept] [Suggest other times] [Keep Tue 10:00]`
2. _They asked, any time:_ "<Name> can't make Tue 10:00 and is happy with any time. Pick one by Wed 10:00 am, or Tue 10:00 stays." `[Pick a time] [Keep Tue 10:00]`
3. _You asked:_ "You asked to move Tue 10:00 → Thu 6 pm. <Name> has until Wed 10:00 am." `[Withdraw — keep Tue 10:00]`
4. _Original time gone:_ "Tue 10:00 was booked by someone else while you waited." Consultee: `[Message <C>] [Cancel for a full refund]`; consultant: `[Place a new time]`.
5. _Lapsed:_ (history line) "No answer by Wed 10:00 — the booking stayed at Tue 10:00."

### D3 — Decline semantics at the server

**Recommendation:** make the PATCH REJECTED door refuse any wrapper that was ever confirmed (has a SUCCEEDED payment and a non-tentative occurrence history, or an open reschedule) with 409 `USE_RESCHEDULE_RESPOND` / `USE_CANCEL_ENDPOINT`. Decline is for _requests_; ending a confirmed booking is Cancel (strike + policy) or respond-decline (restore). This makes A-01 impossible from any surface, including stale tabs and the detail page's needs-you slot that shares `declineRequest` (`request-decision.ts:1-7`).
