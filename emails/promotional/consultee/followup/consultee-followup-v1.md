# Consultee Follow-Up - Version 1
## 3-Day Follow-Up | Curiosity-Driven Tone

---

### Subject Line Options:
1. Saw you opened my email about Familiarise
2. Which expert would you want to learn from?
3. Quick follow-up on connecting with experts

---

### Email Body:

Hi [Consultee Name],

I reached out a few days ago about **Familiarise** — a platform where you can connect with consultants and experts for 1-on-1s, mentorship, webinars, and structured classes.

I'm following up because I'm curious: **What kind of expert would be most valuable to you right now?**

- Career coach to help you navigate a transition?
- Business consultant for your side project?
- Technical mentor to level up your skills?
- Creative expert to help you start that project?

**Why I'm asking:**

We're actively onboarding experts across every field, and I want to make sure we're building the platform you'd actually use. Your answer helps me understand what you're looking for.

**Quick reminder about Familiarise:**
- Browse experts across every field imaginable
- Book what you need: one session, monthly mentorship, webinars, or courses
- Free early access (we're still improving the platform)
- Flexible pricing - pay per session or subscribe

**The honest truth:** We're in launch mode, so you might encounter minor booking quirks. But if you tell us what you need help with, I'll personally point you to the right experts on our platform.

Interested in exploring?

**[Find your expert on Familiarise]**

Or just hit reply and tell me what you're trying to learn — I'd love to help point you in the right direction.

[Your Name]
Founder, Familiarise

P.S. - Even if you're just browsing, your feedback on what works (and what doesn't) is super valuable to us.

---

### Personalization Variables:
- `[Consultee Name]` - Recipient's first name
- `[Your Name]` - Sender's name
- `[CTA Link]` - Platform URL

### Send Timing:
- 3 days after initial email
- Best sent: Monday-Wednesday, 9 AM - 12 PM

### Best For:
- Non-responders to initial email
- Engaging through questions
- Building conversation vs. hard sell
