# Consultant Follow-Up - Version 2

## 7-Day Follow-Up | Urgency + Social Proof

---

### Subject Line Options:

1. [Last call] Free platform access closes soon
2. 47 consultants joined Familiarise this week
3. Your spot is still available (but not for long)

---

### Email Body:

Hi [Consultant Name],

Quick update: We've onboarded **47 consultants** to Familiarise over the past week, and the feedback has been incredible.

**What they're saying:**

- "Finally, one dashboard for everything I offer"
- "Saved me 6 hours this week on scheduling alone"
- "My clients love having one place to book everything"

I reached out last week because I thought Familiarise could help you manage consultations, subscriptions, webinars, and classes from a single platform.

**Here's where we're at:**

We're closing free early access soon to focus on supporting our founding consultants. If you've been considering joining, now's the time.

**What you're missing out on:**

- Zero platform fees while we're in launch mode
- Direct influence on our roadmap (we shipped 3 features this week based on consultant feedback)
- First-mover advantage in your niche
- A complete practice management system

**The reality check:** Yes, we're still improving our booking algorithm. But our founding consultants are seeing real value despite minor quirks, and we're fixing issues within hours, not weeks.

Still interested in being part of this?

**[Claim your spot before we close early access]**

This is the last email I'll send about this opportunity. No hard feelings if it's not the right fit.

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, Familiarise

P.S. - If you're on the fence, here's my direct calendar link: [LINK]. Book 15 minutes and I'll show you exactly how it works.

---

### Personalization Variables:

- `[Consultant Name]` - Recipient's first name
- `[Your Name]` - Sender's name
- `[CTA Link]` - Sign-up URL
- `[Calendar Link]` - Personal demo booking

### Send Timing:

- 7 days after initial email
- Best sent: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM

### Best For:

- Non-responders after 7 days
- Creating urgency without being pushy
- Consultants who need social proof
