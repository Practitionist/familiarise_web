# Consultant Follow-Up - Version 1

## 3-Day Follow-Up | Soft Reminder Tone

---

### Subject Line Options:

1. Quick question about Familiarise
2. Following up on consulting platform opportunity
3. Did you get a chance to look at Familiarise?

---

### Email Body:

Hi [Consultant Name],

I sent you an email a few days ago about **Familiarise**, our new platform for consultants to manage their entire practice in one place.

I wanted to follow up because we're actively onboarding our first consultants this week, and I'd love to have you as part of our early community.

**Quick recap:**

- All-in-one platform for consultations, subscriptions, webinars, and classes
- Free access during our launch phase
- Direct input into what we build next
- No platform fees eating into your revenue

**Being transparent:** We're in active development and refining our booking algorithm based on real consultant feedback. You might encounter minor issues, but we fix them fast and keep you in the loop.

Is this something that could help grow your consulting practice? Even if you just want to explore and give feedback, that would be incredibly valuable to us.

**[Set up your account here - Takes 5 minutes]**

Thanks for considering!

[Your Name]
Founder, Familiarise

P.S. - If now isn't the right time, no worries. Just let me know if you'd like me to reach out again when we're more polished.

---

### Personalization Variables:

- `[Consultant Name]` - Recipient's first name
- `[Your Name]` - Sender's name
- `[CTA Link]` - Sign-up URL

### Send Timing:

- 3 days after initial email
- Best sent: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM

### Best For:

- Non-responders to initial email
- Consultants who opened but didn't click
- Gentle nudge without pressure
